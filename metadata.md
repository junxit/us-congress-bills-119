# S.Res. 964

A resolution designating November 21, 2026, as National Adoption Day and November 2026 as National Adoption Month to promote national awareness of adoption and the children awaiting families, celebrating children and families involved in adoption, and encouraging the people of the United States to secure safety, permanency, and well-being for all children.
