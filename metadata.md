# S.Res. 961

A resolution expressing the sense of the Senate that the Federal Emergency Management Agency is an essential Federal institution, that disaster response and long-term recovery are critical statutory obligations of the Federal Government, and that robust Federal coordination is required to support States facing catastrophic natural disasters, including devastating wildfires in the western United States.
