# H.R. 3436

> To authorize a Law Enforcement Education Grant program to encourage students to pursue a career in law enforcement.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Law Enforcement Education Grant Program Act of 2025”.

## § 3. Repeal of University Sustainability Programs
