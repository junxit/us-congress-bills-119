# S. 815

> To designate the outdoor amphitheater at the Blue Ridge Music Center in Galax, Virginia, as the “Rick Boucher Amphitheater”.

## § 1. Designation of the Rick Boucher Amphitheater

- **(a)** *Designation* The outdoor amphitheater at the Blue Ridge Music Center, located at 700 Foothills Road, Galax, Virginia, a facility within the Blue Ridge Parkway, which is a unit of the National Park System, shall be known and designated as the “Rick Boucher Amphitheater”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the outdoor amphitheater referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Rick Boucher Amphitheater”.
