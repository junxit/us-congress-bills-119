# H.R. 10398

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to modify payment rates under the Federally qualified health center prospective payment system under the Medicare program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Health Center Reforms Enabling Better Access for Seniors and Elders Act of 2026” or the “CHC REBASE Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Modifying payment rates under the Federally qualified health center prospective payment system under the Medicare program

Section 1834(o) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m(o)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(B)(ii), by striking “Payment” and inserting “Subject to paragraph (6), payment”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(6)** *Adjustment of payment rates*
  >   - **(A)** *In general* The Secretary shall, through notice and comment rulemaking and taking into account data collected under subparagraph (B) and recommendations of the working group established under subparagraph (C)—
  >     - **(i)** adjust payment rates determined under paragraph (2)(B) for Federally qualified health center services furnished in 2028 to account for increases in the type, intensity, duration, and amount of services furnished in a unit of service and corresponding increases in the cost of furnishing a unit of service in a manner such that the estimated aggregate amount of prospective payment rates (determined prior to the application of section 1833(a)(1)(Z)) for such services is equal to 100 percent of the estimated amount of reasonable costs (determined without the application of a per visit payment limit or productivity screen and prior to the application of section 1866(a)(2)(A)(ii)) that would have occurred for such services under this title in such year had the system established under this subsection never been implemented; and
  >     - **(ii)** not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this paragraph, modify the list of services furnished to an individual at a Federally qualified health center under subparagraph (1)(A).
  >   - **(B)** *Collection of data*
  >     - **(i)** *In general* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this paragraph, the Secretary shall require Federally qualified health centers to submit cost reports, uniform data system reports, and any other health center program reports required by law to facilitate the adjustment described in subparagraph (A).
  >     - **(ii)** *Provision of information to the working group* The Secretary shall make data required to be submitted under clause (i) available to the working group established under subparagraph (C).
  >   - **(C)** *Working group*
  >     - **(i)** *In general* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this paragraph, the Secretary shall convene a working group to make recommendations to the Secretary on modifications to the prospective payment system established under this subsection.
  >     - **(ii)** *Composition* The working group shall be composed of such members as selected by the Secretary and shall include—
  >       - **(I)** a patient or member of the board of a Federally qualified health center;
  >       - **(II)** a physician, or a practitioner described in section 1842(b)(18)(C), who works at such a center;
  >       - **(III)** at least 1 representative from a primary care association;
  >       - **(IV)** at least 1 representative from a Health Center Controlled Network (as defined in section 330(e)(1)(C) of the Public Health Service Act);
  >       - **(V)** a representative from a national organization that represents Federally qualified health centers;
  >       - **(VI)** a representative from the hospital and ambulatory policy group of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services; and
  >       - **(VII)** such other individuals as the Secretary may specify.
  >     - **(iii)** *Duties* The working group shall—
  >       - **(I)** examine potential adjustments to payment rates under paragraph (2)(B) to reflect changes in the type, intensity, duration, or amount of services furnished within a unit of service, and the average cost of providing care per unit of service;
  >       - **(II)** evaluate whether such rates capture 100 percent of the estimated amount of reasonable costs for Federally qualified health center services (determined without the application of a per visit payment limit or productivity screen and prior to the application of section 1866(a)(2)(A)(ii)) that would have occurred for such services under this title in such year had the system established under this subsection never been implemented;
  >       - **(III)** examine which services are excluded from payment under such system and whether such services should be payable as Federally qualified health center services;
  >       - **(IV)** evaluate the possibility of extending application of new payment and delivery system innovations developed with respect to physicians’ services under section 1848 to Federally qualified health centers; and
  >       - **(V)** examine such other issues relating to payment for Federally qualified health centers as determined appropriate by the working group.
  >     - **(iv)** *Recommendations* Not later than 1 year after the date on which the working group first convenes and after providing opportunity for public comment, such group shall make such recommendations to the Secretary regarding payment for Federally qualified health centers as determined appropriate by the working group.

## § 3. Ensuring appropriate payments for telehealth services furnished by FQHCs and RHCs

Section 1834(m)(8) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m(m)(8)) is amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A)(ii), by inserting “(or, with respect to such services furnished on or after January 1, 2027, under subparagraph (C))” after “subparagraph (B)”;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B)—
  - **(A)** in the header, by inserting “before 2027” after “rule”;
  - **(B)** in clause (i), by striking “during the periods for which subparagraph (A) applies” and inserting “before January 1, 2027”; and
  - **(C)** in clause (ii), by inserting “furnished to an individual before January 1, 2027” after “telehealth services”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(C)** *Payment rule for 2027*
  >   - **(i)** *In general* A telehealth service furnished to an eligible telehealth individual by a Federally qualified health center or rural health clinic during any portion of the period described in subparagraph (A) beginning on or after January 1, 2027, shall be paid as a Federally qualified health center service or rural health clinic service (as applicable) under the prospective payment system established under section 1834(o) or the methodology for all-inclusive rates established under section 1833(a)(3), respectively.
  >   - **(ii)** *Treatment of costs* Costs associated with the furnishing of telehealth services by a Federally qualified health center or rural health clinic during any portion of the period described in subparagraph (A) beginning on or after January 1, 2027, shall be considered allowable costs for purposes of the prospective payment system established under section 1834(o) and the methodology for all-inclusive rates established under section 1833(a)(3), as applicable.

## § 4. Ensuring appropriate payment of Federally qualified health center services furnished to Medicare Advantage enrollees

- **(a)** *Clarifying wraparound payments* Not later than December 31, 2026, the Secretary of Health and Human Services (in this section referred to as the “Secretary”) shall—
  - **(1)** issue guidance to Federally qualified health centers (as defined in section 1861(aa)(4) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x(aa)(4))) on—
    - **(A)** the calculation of payment amounts under section 1833(a)(3)(B) of such Act (42 U.S.C. 1395l(a)(3)(B)); and
    - **(B)** procedures for submitting a claim for such a payment;
  - **(2)** issue guidance to Medicare administrative contractors to ensure timely and accurate payment of amounts owed under such section that—
    - **(A)** outlines a standardized process for providing Federally qualified health centers such payments; and
    - **(B)** clarifies that such contractors may not require such a center (or any entity acting on behalf of such center) to submit the Medicare Advantage plan contract at issue to such contractor as a prerequisite to receiving such payments; and
  - **(3)** establish a mechanism by which such a center may seek review by the Secretary of such a contractor’s determination of amounts payable to the center under such section.
- **(b)** *Evaluation of access to FQHCs in MA networks* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this paragraph, the Comptroller General of the United States shall submit to Congress a report that examines the inclusion of Federally qualified health centers in Medicare Advantage plan provider networks. Such report shall include recommendations for actions to be taken to ensure adequate access to Federally qualified health centers for rural and underserved populations.
