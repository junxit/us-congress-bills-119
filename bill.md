# H.R. 10654

> To ensure that a member separated from an Armed Force, solely on the basis that the member has pseudofolliculitis barbae, receives an honorable discharge and veterans benefits.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting our Forces’ Benefits Act” or the “PFB Act”.

## § 2. Member of the Armed Forces separated on the sole basis of pseudofolliculitis barbae: honorable discharge; eligibility for veterans benefits

- **(a)** *Honorable discharge* The Secretary concerned (as such term is defined in section 101 of title 10, United States Code) shall characterize the separation of a member from an Armed Force, solely on the basis that the member has pseudofolliculitis barbae, as honorable.
- **(b)** *Veterans benefits* A member separated from an Armed Force solely because the member has pseudofolliculitis barbae may not be treated as ineligible for a benefit administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs on the basis that the member has not completed the applicable minimum active-duty service requirement established in section 5303A of title 38, United States Code.
