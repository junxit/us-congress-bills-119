# S. 2216

> To extend war reserves stockpile authority.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Weapons Resupply, Stockpile, and Alliance–Israel Act”.

## § 2. Extension of war reserves stockpile authority

Section 12001(d) of the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2005 (Public Law 108–287; 118 Stat. 1011) is amended by striking “after January 1, 2027” and inserting “after January 1, 2029”.
