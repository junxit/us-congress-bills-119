# S. 4305

> To increase the criminal penalties for assaulting a Bureau of Prisons correctional officer.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safer Prisons Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Enhanced penalties for assault on Bureau of Prisons correctional officers

Section 111 of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (c) as subsection (d); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (b) the following:
  > - **(c)** *Enhanced penalties for assault on Bureau of Prisons correctional officers* In the case of any violation of this section against a correctional officer of the Bureau of Prisons, the applicable maximum term of imprisonment under this section shall be doubled, and the applicable maximum fine shall be adjusted accordingly.
