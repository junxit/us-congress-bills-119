# S. 5540

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to provide for teacher and school leader quality enhancement and to enhance institutional aid.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preparing and Retaining Education Professionals Act of 2026” or the “PREP Act of 2026”.

  - **(1)** by redesignating paragraph (13) as paragraph (14); and
