# H.R. 10253

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1706 South Burnside Avenue in Gonzales, Louisiana, as the “Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal service located at 1706 South Burnside Avenue in Gonzales, Louisiana, shall be designated as the “Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building”.
