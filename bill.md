# H.R. 1342

> To prohibit discrimination based on political affiliation in granting disaster assistance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stopping Political Discrimination in Disaster Assistance Act”.

## § 2. Nondiscrimination in disaster assistance

Section 308(a) of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5151(a)) is amended by striking “or economic status” and inserting “economic status, or political affiliation”.
