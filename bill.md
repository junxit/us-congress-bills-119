# H.R. 10853

> To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Yemen for temporary protected status.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Yemen TPS Act”.

## § 2. Designation of Yemen for temporary protected status

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall designate Yemen for temporary protected status until the date that is 3 months after January 20, 2029.
