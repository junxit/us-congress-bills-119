# S. RES. 953

> Condemning the horrific and senseless attack in Bend, Oregon, and expressing support and prayers for all those impacted by the tragedy.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on October 15, 2026, a mass shooting took place at the Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon;
- Whereas the people of the United States mourn the four bright lives that were lost at the Silver Rail Elementary School in this horrific tragedy;
- Whereas the people of the United States continue to pray for and hold deep sympathy for those who were injured and are recovering from the attack; and
- Whereas the people of the United States are thankful for the first responders, educators, and families who responded to this attack: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate—

- **(1)** condemns the senseless attack at the Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon;
- **(2)** honors the memory of the victims that were killed in the attack;
- **(3)** expresses hope for a full and speedy recovery and pledges continued support for the people who were injured in the attack;
- **(4)** offers heartfelt condolences and deepest sympathies to all of the students and educators of Silver Rail Elementary School, as well as the families, friends, community members, and loved ones affected by the tragedy;
- **(5)** honors the selfless and dedicated services of the teachers, school administrators, school support staff, medical professionals, first responders, law enforcement, family members, and other community members who responded to the attack; and
- **(6)** commits to working together to prevent such tragic attacks.
