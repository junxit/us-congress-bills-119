# H.R. 10724

> To amend title 11, United States Code, to clarify the rules for the exceptions to the discharge of the debt of an individual debtor, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Clarification of exception to discharge of individual debtor

Section 523(a) of title 11, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)—
  - **(A)** by amending subparagraph (A) to read as follows:
    > - **(A)** false pretenses used by the debtor, a false representation made by the debtor, or actual fraud committed by the debtor, other than a statement respecting the debtor's or an insider's financial condition;

  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), in the matter preceding clause (i), by inserting after “use” the following: “, by the debtor,”; and
- **(2)** by amending paragraph (4) to read as follows:
  > - **(4)** for, when committed by the debtor—
  >   - **(A)** fraud or defalcation while acting in a fiduciary capacity;
  >   - **(B)** embezzlement; or
  >   - **(C)** larceny;
