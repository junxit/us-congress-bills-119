# S. RES. 458

> Extending the enforcement of certain budgetary points of order in the Senate.

## § 1. Extension of enforcement of budgetary points of order in the Senate

Notwithstanding any provision of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 621 et seq.), subsections (c)(2) and (d)(3) of section 904 of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 U.S.C. 621 note) shall remain in effect for purposes of Senate enforcement through September 30, 2027.
