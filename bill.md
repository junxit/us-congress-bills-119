# S. 5607

> To amend the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 to restore the full 75-percent payment to counties from the Oregon and California land-grant fund, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Clarification Act”.

## § 3. Clarification of election options

Section 102(b)(1)(A) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7112(b)(1)(A)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking the subparagraph designation and heading and all that follows through “as applicable, shall” and inserting the following:
  > - **(A)** *Making of election*
  >   - **(i)** *In general* An election described in clause (ii) shall

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(ii)** *Election described* An election referred to in clause (i) is an election to receive, as applicable—
  >   - **(I)** the county payment;
  >   - **(II)** a share of the State payment;
  >   - **(III)** the county payment and a share of the State payment;
  >   - **(IV)** the BLM revenue-based payment;
  >   - **(V)** a share of the Forest Service revenue-based payment;
  >   - **(VI)** the BLM revenue-based payment and a share of the Forest Service revenue-based payment;
  >   - **(VII)** the BLM revenue-based payment and a share of the State payment; or
  >   - **(VIII)** the county payment and a share of the Forest Service revenue-based payment.
