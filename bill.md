# H.R. 3801

> To codify Executive Order 14280 (related to reinstating common sense school discipline policies).

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “EO 14280 Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Codification of Executive Order 14280

Executive Order 14280 (90 Fed. Reg. 17533; Reinstating Commonsense School Discipline Policies) shall have the force and effect of law.
