# H.R. 11097

> To authorize the purchase of the Potter Valley Hydroelectric Project in northern California, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Northern California Water and Power Act”.

## § 2. Purchase of the Potter Valley Hydroelectric Project

- **(a)** *In general* If, during the 10-year period after the date of the enactment of this Act, Pacific Gas & Electric offers to sell the Project to the United States, the President, acting through the appropriate Secretary, may purchase the Project.
- **(b)** *Operation and maintenance*
  - **(1)** *In general* The designated Secretary shall—
    - **(A)** be responsible for maintaining and operating the Project; and
    - **(B)** in carrying out subparagraph (A), comply with all applicable requirements of the Federal Power Act and other Federal, State, Tribal, and local laws.
  - **(2)** *Agreements* In carrying out paragraph (1), the designated Secretary may enter into an agreement or contract with a State, Tribal, local, or private entity as necessary to maintain and operate the Project or a component of the Project.
- **(c)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Appropriate Secretary* The term appropriate Secretary means—
    - **(A)** the Secretary of Agriculture;
    - **(B)** the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers; or
    - **(C)** the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Commissioner of Reclamation.
  - **(2)** *Designated Secretary* The term designated Secretary means the Secretary through whom the President acts to purchase the Project pursuant to subsection (a).
  - **(3)** *Project* The term Project means the Potter Valley Hydroelectric Project (Federal Energy Regulatory Commission Project No. 77) and associated facilities in the State of California.
