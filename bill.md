# H.R. 2373

> To amend the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2022 with respect to a civil action relating to the disclosure of intimate images.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Increased Accountability for Nonconsensual Pornography Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Civil action relating to disclosure of intimate images

Section 1309 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2022 (15 U.S.C. 6851) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (2), by inserting “competent,” after “conscious,”; and
  - **(B)** in paragraph (5)(A)—
    - **(i)** in clause (i), by striking “or” at the end;
    - **(ii)** in clause (ii)—
      - **(I)** in subclause (I), by striking the semicolon at the end and inserting “; or”;
      - **(II)** in subclause (II), by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon; and
      - **(III)** by striking subclause (III); and
    - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(iii)** an identifiable individual engaging in sexually explicit conduct; and

- **(2)** in subsection (b)(3)(A)(i), by striking “$150,000” and inserting “$500,000”.
