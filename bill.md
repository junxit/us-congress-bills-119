# H.R. 9765

> To amend the requirements for teaching residency programs under part A of title II of the Higher Education Act of 1965.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Educators for America Act”.

## § 2. Teacher quality partnerships

Section 202(e)(2)(B)(ii) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1022a(e)(2)(B)(ii)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subclauses (I), (II), and (III) as items (aa), (bb), and (cc), respectively, and adjusting the margins accordingly;
- **(2)** in the matter preceding item (aa), as so redesignated, by striking “shall” and all that follows through “characteristics:” and inserting the following: “shall carry out the following:
  > - **(I)** Establish criteria for the selection of eligible individuals to participate in the teaching residency program based on the following characteristics:

- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(II)** Prioritize the recruitment and selection of eligible individuals underrepresented in the teaching profession, including men.
