# H.R. 298

> To eliminate certain limitations and exclusions regarding defense articles and requirements regarding security assistance and sales with respect to the Republic of Cyprus.

## § 1. Elimination of requirements concerning the Republic of Cyprus

- **(a)** *Elimination of limitations and exclusions regarding defense articles* Section 1250A of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 (22 U.S.C. 2373 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the heading, by striking “Repeal of prohibition on” and inserting “Sense of Congress regarding”;
  - **(2)** in subsection (a), by striking “(a) Sense of Congress.—It is the sense of Congress that—” and inserting the following:
  - **(3)** by striking subsections (b), (c), and (d).
- **(b)** *Elimination of requirements regarding security assistance and certain sales* Section 620C of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2373) is amended by striking subsections (d) and (e).
