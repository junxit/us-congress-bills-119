# H.R. 9718

> To require the Administrator of the Small Business Administration to maintain a website for small business concerns relating to onshoring manufacturing capacity, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reshoring American Manufacturing Act of 2026” or the “RAM Act”.

## § 2. Information and resources for small business concerns relating to onshoring manufacturing capacity

- **(a)** *Webpage required* Not later than 60 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Administrator of the Small Business Administration, acting through the Associate Administrator for the Office of Manufacturing and Trade of the Administration, shall establish and maintain a webpage containing a list of resources and information for small business concerns (as defined under section 3 of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632)) relating to onshoring to the United States part or all of the manufacturing capacity of such concerns.
- **(b)** *Contents* The resources and information included on the webpage required by subsection (a) shall include—
  - **(1)** contact information for general inquiries, relevant offices of the Small Business Administration, and any partner organizations;
  - **(2)** a free tool designed to help small business concerns identify entities that could assist with onshoring to the United States the manufacturing capacity of such concerns; and
  - **(3)** other information, as determined appropriate by the Associate Administrator for the Office of Manufacturing and Trade of the Administration.
- **(c)** *Updates* Until the date that is 4 years after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Associate Administrator for the Office of Manufacturing and Trade annually shall seek input from the Chief Counsel for Advocacy of the Office of Advocacy of the Small Business Administration for improvements to the webpage and updates to the information and resources included on such webpage.
- **(d)** *Study and report* Not later than 2 years after the date on which the webpage is established, the Administrator of the Small Business Administration shall conduct a study and submit to Congress a report on the effectiveness of the webpage required under this Act. The Administrator shall submit to Congress an updated report annually thereafter until the date that is 4 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(e)** *Termination* This Act and the requirements of this Act shall terminate 4 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
