# H. RES. 1659

> Honoring the life and legacy of Kylie Kupperman and supporting the designation of November 13, 2026, as “Kylie’s Day”.

## Preamble

- Whereas Kylie Kupperman was born on November 13, 2005, in Englewood, New Jersey, to Hayley and Adam Kupperman, and grew up in Chappaqua, New York;
- Whereas Kylie was a beloved daughter and a loving older sister to Jared and Alexa;
- Whereas just weeks before her 4th birthday, Kylie was diagnosed with medulloblastoma, a rare and fast-growing pediatric brain tumor;
- Whereas Kylie underwent surgery, followed by months of chemotherapy and proton therapy, before being declared cancer free;
- Whereas Kylie was affectionately known as “Smiley Kylie” for her enduring smile, even in the most difficult moments of her illness and treatment;
- Whereas Kylie faced lasting effects of treatment on her growth and learning, yet persevered in her education and loved Yankee games, ballet, and performing in dance recitals;
- Whereas Kylie loved the color pink;
- Whereas Kylie earned a merit scholarship to her first-choice college, the University of Delaware, where she pursued her passion for fashion design and product innovation;
- Whereas just as Kylie was beginning her freshman year at the University of Delaware, her cancer returned;
- Whereas Kylie’s cancer spread to areas of her brain where surgery was not possible; and
- Whereas Kylie passed on September 10, 2025, at the age of 19: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** honors the life of Kylie Kupperman and recognizes the joy, kindness, courage, and love of pink that she shared with her family, friends, and communities;
- **(2)** supports the designation of “Kylie’s Day” in celebration of her life and legacy;
- **(3)** urges support for the “Kylie’s Voices for Childhood Cancer Act” to ensure voices for pediatric cancer patients always have a seat at the table; and
- **(4)** encourages individuals and communities to observe “Kylie’s Day” by wearing pink in her honor.
