# S. 3133

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to update the juvenile transfer for criminal prosecution process, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Violent Juvenile Offender Accountability Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Transfer for criminal prosecution of certain juvenile offenders

Section 5032 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> -  Notwithstanding any other provision in this section, criminal prosecution of a juvenile in the appropriate district court of the United States may be begun without a motion to transfer of the Attorney General if the juvenile is 16 years of age or older and is alleged to have committed—
>   - **(1)** any crime of homicide under chapter 51;
>   - **(2)** aggravated assault, as described in section 111(b);
>   - **(3)** theft of a motor vehicle under section 2119;
>   - **(4)** robbery under section 1951, to which section 924(c) applies; or
>   - **(5)** aggravated sexual abuse under section 2241, to which section 924(c) applies.
