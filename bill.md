# H. RES. 1617

> Recognizes October as “American Pharmacy Month” in honor of the contributions of pharmacists and pharmacy teams in providing safe, accessible, affordable, and effective patient care services and products to all residents and protecting public health in communities across the United States;.

## Preamble

- Whereas pharmacists are some of the most accessible health care providers delivering critical services to patients and contributing to the health and well-being of all people;
- Whereas pharmacists are uniquely positioned to help ensure optimal medication therapy outcomes and improve patient adherence to prescribed medications;
- Whereas, with a community pharmacy located within 5 miles of nearly all Americans, pharmacists and pharmacy personnel work collaboratively with other health care providers to help patients overcome illness, manage chronic disease, and achieve better health outcomes through the safe and effective use of medications;
- Whereas, today, there are over 330,000 licensed pharmacists, over 400,000 pharmacy technicians, and 47,000 student pharmacists providing care and serving as patient advocates to ensure access to the best and safest therapy to the patients they serve;
- Whereas the American Pharmacists Association (APhA), founded on October 6, 1852, is the oldest national professional organization of pharmacists in the United States and has played a leading role in advancing the profession and improving patient care;
- Whereas October 6, 2027, will mark the 175th anniversary of the founding of the American Pharmacists Association, a milestone reflecting its longstanding leadership in pharmacy practice, education, science, and public health advocacy; and
- Whereas the recognition of this historic anniversary provides an opportunity to honor the enduring contributions of APhA and our nation’s pharmacists to the health and well-being of the Nation: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** recognizes “American Pharmacy Month” in honor of the contributions of pharmacists and pharmacy teams in providing safe, accessible, affordable, and beneficial patient care services and products to all residents and protect the public health of communities across the United States;
- **(2)** pledges to celebrate the contributions of pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, student pharmacists, and other pharmacy personnel to the health and well-being of all people in each State;
- **(3)** recognizes the upcoming 175th anniversary of the American Pharmacists Association, founded October 6, 1852, and celebrates its longstanding legacy of advancing pharmacy practice, pharmaceutical science, patient care, and public health;
- **(4)** recognizes pharmacists as among the most accessible health care providers delivering critical services to patients and contributing to the health and well-being of all people; and
- **(5)** commits to working collaboratively with pharmacists and other health care providers to help patients overcome illness and live healthier lives through chronic disease management and the full, effective use of their medicines.
