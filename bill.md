# H.R. 10250

> To amend titles 41 and 10, United States Code, to prohibit a requirement or preference for the use of union labor for Federal contracts.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Preference Act”.

## § 2. Federal contracting prohibition for executive agencies to require or give a preference for union labor

- **(a)** *Amendment* Chapter 47 of title 41, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 4715. Prohibition on requirement or preference for the use of union labor
  >
  > The head of an executive agency may not require or give preference to an offeror on the basis of whether the offeror will use workers subject to a collective bargaining agreement.

- **(b)** *Technical and conforming amendment* The table of sections for chapter 47 of title 41, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new item:

## § 3. Defense contracting requirement to use union labor

Chapter 243 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 3345. Requirement to use union labor
>
> The Secretary of Defense may not require or give preference to an offeror for a contract on the basis of whether the offeror will use workers subject to a collective bargaining agreement.
