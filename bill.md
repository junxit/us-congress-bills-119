# S. 4660

> To provide appropriations to the Secretary of Agriculture to make payments to producers of specialty crops.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Economic Relief for Specialty Crops Act”.

## § 2. Specialty crop farmers assistance

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, for fiscal year 2027, there is appropriated to the Secretary of Agriculture $5,000,000,000, to remain available until expended, to make payments to producers of a specialty crop (as defined in section 3 of the Specialty Crops Competitiveness Act of 2004 (7 U.S.C. 1621 note; Public Law 108–465)) under the Assistance for Specialty Crop Farmers program of the Department of Agriculture (as announced on February 13, 2026; Release No.: 0031.26) or any substantially similar subsequent assistance program initiated by the Secretary of Agriculture under the authority of the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act (15 U.S.C. 714 et seq.).
- **(b)** *Terms and conditions* Payments under subsection (a) shall be made in a manner consistent with the terms and conditions specified in the notice entitled “Notice; Marketing Assistance for Specialty Crops” (90 Fed. Reg. 1434 (January 8, 2025)).
