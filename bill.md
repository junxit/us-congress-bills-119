# S. 1116

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the provision of certain additional burial benefits for individuals for whom an urn or plaque is furnished, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ensuring Veterans’ Final Resting Place Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Department of Veterans Affairs provision of additional burial benefits when an urn or plaque is furnished

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2306(h) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(A)** by striking “In lieu of furnishing a headstone or marker under this section for” and inserting “In the case of”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “paragraph (3)” and inserting “paragraph (2)”;
  - **(2)** by striking paragraph (2); and
  - **(3)** by redesignating paragraphs (3) through (5) as paragraphs (2) through (4), respectively.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to an individual who dies on or after January 5, 2021.
