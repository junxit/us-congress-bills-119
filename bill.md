# H.R. 2774

> To amend section 5 of the Flood Control Act of 1941 to impose a deadline for initiating the repair or restoration of project under such section, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safeguarding Our Levees Act”.

## § 2. Emergency response to natural disasters

Section 5 of the Flood Control Act of 1941 (33 U.S.C. 701n(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a) by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(6)** *Deadline* Not later than 180 days after receiving from a non-Federal sponsor of a project a request to repair or rehabilitate a flood control work described in paragraph (1), the Secretary shall initiate the repair or restoration of such flood control work.

- **(2)** in subsection (d)(2) by inserting “25 percent of” before “the difference”.
