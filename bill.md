# H.R. 10325

> To increase the mandatory contribution amount of Federal Home Loan Banks to the Affordable Housing Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Increased affordable housing program investment

Section 10(j)(5) of the Federal Home Loan Bank Act (12 U.S.C. 1430 (j)(5)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “In 1995, and subsequent years,” and inserting “In 1995 through 2025,”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(D)** In 2026, and subsequent years, 15 percent of the preceding year's net income, or such prorated sums as may be required to assure that the aggregate contribution of the Banks shall not be less than $100,000,000 for each such year.
