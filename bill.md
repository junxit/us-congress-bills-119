# H.R. 10450

> To amend chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to prohibit certain political officials from owning or trading stocks or trading on prediction markets.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Revealing Egregious Violators of Ethics and the Law Act” or the “REVEAL Act”.
