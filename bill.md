# S.J. RES. 15

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network relating to “Anti-Money Laundering Regulations for Residential Real Estate Transfers”.

That Congress disapproves the final rule submitted by the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network relating to “Anti-Money Laundering Regulations for Residential Real Estate Transfers” (89 Fed. Reg. 70258 (August 29, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
