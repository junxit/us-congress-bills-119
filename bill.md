# H.R. 9523

> To permit judicial review of Temporary Protected Status (TPS) claims.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “TPS Relief Act”.

## § 2. Permitting judicial review of TPS claims

Section 1254(a)(b)(5)(A) of title 8, United States Code, is amended by striking out the word “no”.
