# H.R. 3672

> To amend the Securities Act of 1933 to expand the research report exception to include reports about any issuer that undertakes a proposed offering of public securities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securities Research Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Provision of research

Section 2(a)(3) of the Securities Act of 1933 (15 U.S.C. 77b(a)(3)) is amended—

- **(a)** by striking “an emerging growth company” and inserting “an issuer”;
- **(b)** by striking “the common equity” and inserting “any”; and
- **(c)** by striking “such emerging growth company” and inserting “such issuer”.
