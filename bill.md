# H. RES. 1510

> Expressing support for the designation of September 4, 2026, as “Taekwondo Grandmasters Appreciation Day”.

## Preamble

- Whereas Taekwondo is a Korean martial art that developed through thousands of years of Korean history;
- Whereas the origins of Taekwondo are rooted in the ancient kingdom of Koguryo with the defense martial art known as “Subak” or “Taekkyon”;
- Whereas the word “taekwondo” is composed of 3 parts: “tae,” meaning foot or to step on, “kwon,” meaning fist or fight and “do,” meaning the way or discipline;
- Whereas, while both hands and feet are used in taekwondo, the sport is characterized by its unique combination of kick movements;
- Whereas, in addition to sporting and self-defense skills, Taekwondo also provides health benefits, including flexibility, stamina, strength and posture;
- Whereas Taekwondo also emphasizes mental discipline and character through the Taekwondo tenets of courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, and indomitable spirit;
- Whereas, in 1973, the World Taekwondo Federation was founded as the official governing body over Taekwondo;
- Whereas, in 2008, South Korea designated September 4 as “International Taekwondo Day” in recognition of the day Taekwondo became an Olympic sport on September 4, 1994;
- Whereas, today, Taekwondo is practiced worldwide by 80,000,000 people of all ages and backgrounds;
- Whereas the title of “grandmaster” is reserved for the highest-ranking, most experienced, and most respected Taekwondo practitioners; and
- Whereas Taekwondo and its grandmasters provide numerous physical and mental benefits to many people across the United States: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives expresses support for “Taekwondo Grandmasters Appreciation Day”.
