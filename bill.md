# S. 5451

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to require that recipients of Pell Grants be citizens or nationals of the United States.

## § 1. Amendment to the Higher Education Act of 1965

- **(a)** *Pell Grants* Section 401(a) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1070a(a)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the subsection heading, by inserting “; Eligible student” after “Definitions”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(3)** *Eligible student* Notwithstanding section 484, or any other provision of law, for the purposes of this section, the term eligible student means a student who—
    >   - **(A)** meets the requirements of paragraphs (1) through (4) of section 484(a); and
    >   - **(B)** is a citizen or national of the United States.

- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 484(a) (20 U.S.C. 1091(a)) is amended by striking “In order to receive” and inserting “Subject to section 401(a)(3), in order to receive”.
