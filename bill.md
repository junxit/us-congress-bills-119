# S. 5583

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the small agri-biodiesel producer credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Agri-Biodiesel Producer Credit Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of small agri-biodiesel producer credit

Section 40A(g) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2026” and inserting “December 31, 2029”.
