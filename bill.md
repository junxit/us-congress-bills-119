# S. 3263

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to prohibit qualified professional asset managers from exercising voting rights associated with the ownership of securities by the Thrift Savings Fund.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop TSP ESG Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on exercising voting rights associated with the ownership of securities by the Thrift Savings Fund

Section 8438(f) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by inserting “a qualified professional asset manager,” after “The Board,”.
