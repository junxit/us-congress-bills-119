# H.R. 6368

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to modify the thrifty food plan to apply to the urban and rural parts of the State of Hawaii.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Feeding Rural Families Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Amendment

Section 3(u)(2) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2012(u)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “urban and rural parts of” before “Hawaii” the 1st place it appears, and
- **(2)** by inserting “urban and rural” before “Hawaii” the 2d place it appears.
