# H.R. 4547

> To advance Thomas B. Hagen on the retired list of the Navy.

## § 1. Advancement of Captain Thomas B. Hagen, United States Navy, on the retired list

- **(a)** *Advancement* Captain Thomas B. Hagen, United States Navy (retired), is entitled to hold the rank of rear admiral (lower half) while on the retired list of the Navy.
- **(b)** *Additional benefits not To accrue* The advancement of Thomas B. Hagen on the retired list of the Navy under subsection (a) shall not affect—
  - **(1)** the retired pay or other benefits from the United States to which Thomas B. Hagen would have been entitled based upon his military service; or
  - **(2)** any benefits to which any other person may become entitled based on such military service.
