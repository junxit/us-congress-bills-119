# H.R. 10493

> To apply certain requirements for the structure of metropolitan planning organizations designated under title 23, United States Code, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reclaim Local Control Act”.

## § 2. Application of structure requirements to metropolitan planning organizations

Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, any metropolitan planning organization designated under section 134(d) of title 23, United States Code, that is composed entirely of gubernatorial appointees as of such date of enactment shall be—

- **(1)** redesiganted pursuant to paragraph (6) of such section; or
- **(2)** restructured to comply with the requirements of paragraph (2) of such section.
