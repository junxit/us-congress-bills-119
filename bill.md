# H.R. 10637

> To require States to submit a report regarding the role of voter vouchers in registrations to vote in elections for Federal office, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Voter Vouching Transparency Act”.

## § 2. Report on voter vouchers with respect to Federal elections

- **(a)** *Report required* Each State that permits an individual to register to vote in an election for Federal office with the attestation of a voter voucher shall, after each regularly scheduled general election for Federal office, submit to the Election Assistance Commission a report that includes, with respect to the period between such general election and the previous regularly scheduled general election for Federal office, the following:
  - **(1)** The total number of individuals who registered to vote in an election for Federal office with the attestation of a voter voucher.
  - **(2)** The total number of individuals who served as voter vouchers.
  - **(3)** The number of individuals vouched for by each voter voucher.
  - **(4)** The total number of attempted registrations to vote in an election for Federal office with the attestation of a voter voucher that were not accepted by the State, disaggregated by the reason for the nonacceptance, to the extent that the State maintains records of such reasons.
- **(b)** *Prohibited information* The report required in subsection (a) may not contain personally identifying information, including—
  - **(1)** names;
  - **(2)** street addresses;
  - **(3)** voter identification numbers;
  - **(4)** phone numbers;
  - **(5)** signatures; and
  - **(6)** dates of birth.
- **(c)** *Submission through the Election Administration and Voting Survey* The Election Assistance Commission shall, if practicable, permit the report required in subsection (a) to be submitted through the Election Administration and Voting Survey administered by the Commission.
- **(d)** *Public availability of reports* The Election Assistance Commission shall make each report required in subsection (a) publicly available for a period of 22 months from the date such report was received by the Commission.
- **(e)** *Voter voucher defined* In this section, the term voter voucher means an individual who—
  - **(1)** is authorized under State law to attest to the identity or residence of another individual for purposes of voter registration; and
  - **(2)** attests to the identity or residence of another individual for the purpose of registering the other individual to vote in such State.
