# H.R. 10384

> To authorize the Secretary of the Army to conduct a study to determine the feasibility of carrying out a project for flood risk management, including riverbank stabilization, ecosystem restoration, and recreation, along the Maumee River in the vicinity of Glass City Riverwalk, Toledo, Ohio, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Authorization of feasibility study for maumee river, ohio, multipurpose project

- **(a)** *New study* The Secretary of the Army is authorized to conduct a feasibility study for the project identified as the “Glass City Riverwalk” in the report titled “2026 Report to Congress on Future Water Resources Development” submitted to Congress pursuant to section 7001 of the Water Resources Reform and Development Act of 2014 (33 U.S.C. 2282d) for the purposes identified below in subsection (b).
- **(b)** *Project purposes* The feasibility study authorized by subsection (a) shall be for a project along the Maumee River, Ohio, in the vicinity of Glass City Riverwalk, City of Toledo, Ohio, for flood risk management, including riverbank stabilization, ecosystem restoration, and recreation.
