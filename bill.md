# H.R. 5817

> To prohibit the election to Congress of any person holding foreign citizenship.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Disqualifying Dual Loyalty Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Prohibition on election of any person holding foreign citizenship

No person, without regard to whether that person is a United States national, may be elected to the office of Representative or Senator if that person is a national of any country other than the United States.
