# S. 4283

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize a public health and bio-preparedness workforce loan repayment program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Public Health and Bio-Preparedness Workforce Loan Repayment Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Public Health and Bio-Preparedness Workforce Loan Repayment Program

Section 776(i) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 295f–1(i)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2023 through 2025” and inserting “fiscal years 2027 through 2031”.
