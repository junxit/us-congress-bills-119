# H.R. 9521

> To amend title VI of the Social Security Act to extend that date on which certain funds related to broadband infrastructure projects may be expended.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Broadband Infrastructure Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of deadlines for certain fiscal recovery and critical capital projects funds

- **(a)** *State fiscal recovery fund* Section 602(c)(5)(E) of the Social Security Act is amended by adding at the end the following: “Notwithstanding the previous sentence, such funds provided for projects related to broadband infrastructure may be expended through September 30, 2027.”.
- **(b)** *Local fiscal recovery fund* Section 603(c)(6)(D) of the Social Security Act is amended by adding at the end the following: “Notwithstanding the previous sentence, such funds provided for projects related to broadband infrastructure may be expended through September 30, 2027.”.
- **(c)** *Capital projects fund* Section 604 of the Social Security Act is amended by—
  - **(1)** redesignating subsection (d) as subsection (e); and
  - **(2)** by inserting after subsection (c) the following:
    > - **(d)** *Availability* Funds provided under a payment made under this section for a project related to broadband infrastructure may be expended prior to January 1, 2028.
