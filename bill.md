# H.R. 10596

> To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act to reauthorize the drinking water fountain replacement for schools grant program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safe Water for Schools Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Drinking water fountain replacement for schools

Section 1465(d) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–25) is amended by striking “$5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2019 through 2021” and inserting “$7,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2029”.
