# H.R. 10386

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to increase the amount and purposes of funds authorized for the Secretary of the Army to carry out water and wastewater infrastructure projects in Toledo and Oregon, Ohio, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Toledo and Oregon, Ohio, water and wastewater infrastructure modification

Section 219(f)(565) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 335; 138 Stat. 3125) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$10,500,000” and inserting “$45,000,000”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “, including stormwater management, water supply, and environmental restoration,” after “wastewater infrastructure”.
