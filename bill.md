# H.R. 11155

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to establish a presumption that sarcoidosis is a service-connected disease for certain veterans.

## § 1. Veterans compensation: presumption of service connection for sarcoidosis

Section 1112(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (5), by adding “or” after the semicolon; and
- **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (5) the following new paragraph:
  > - **(6)** any form of sarcoidosis developing a 10 percent degree of disability or more after separation from such service;
