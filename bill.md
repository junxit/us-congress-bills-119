# H.R. 7991

> To prohibit States from imposing charges for the purpose of funding the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative Energy Efficiency Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Taxing Our Power Act” or the “STOP RGGI Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on State funding of the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative Energy Efficiency Program

No State may impose a charge for purposes of funding the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative Energy Efficiency Program.
