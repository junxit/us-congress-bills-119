# H.R. 10252

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to expand coverage for adult children.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Launching with Healthcare Act”.

## § 2. Extending required dependent coverage

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2714(a) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–14(a)) is amended by striking “26” and inserting “31”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to plan years beginning after December 31, 2026.
