# H.R. 6534

> To repeal a restriction on assistance to Azerbaijan.

## § 1. Repeal of restriction on assistance in FREEDOM Support Act

- **(a)** *Findings* Congress finds that—
  - **(1)** the Republic of Azerbaijan has remained a steadfast ally to the United States and United States allies since its independence—providing critical cooperation in times of war and times of peace;
  - **(2)** Azerbaijan has demonstrated a clear commitment to prioritizing peace in the South Caucasus; and
  - **(3)** Azerbaijan reached a peace agreement in which the respective borders and territorial integrity of Azerbaijan and the Republic of Armenia have been agreed upon.
- **(b)** *Repeal of restriction on assistance to Azerbaijan* Section 907 of the FREEDOM Support Act (22 U.S.C. 5812 note) is repealed.
- **(c)** *Clerical amendment* The table of contents for such Act is amended by striking the item relating to section 907.
