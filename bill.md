# H.R. 9406

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to eliminate the cap on the number of waivers to certain pay limitations that the Secretary of Veterans Affairs may issue for critical health care personnel of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Modification of limitation on waiver for pay of critical health care personnel of the Department of Veterans Affairs

Section 7431(e)(6) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subparagraph (D); and
- **(2)** by redesignating subparagraphs (E), (F), and (G) as subparagraphs (D), (E), and (F), respectively.
