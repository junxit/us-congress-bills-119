# H.R. 5487

> To prohibit foreign ownership of public utilities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keep the Lights Local Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on foreign ownership

42 U.S. Code Chapter 149 Subchapter XII Part D—Repeal of Public Utility Holding Company Act of 1935 is amended by inserting at the end the following:

> ## § 16464. Prohibition on foreign ownership
>
> - **(a)** *Prohibition on foreign ownership* No foreign corporation or foreign government shall be a public utility holding company.
> - **(b)** *Effective date* The prohibition provided in subsection (a) shall be effective on the date that is 180 days after the effective date of this Act.
