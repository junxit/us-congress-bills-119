# H.R. 10461

> To improve the ability of United States citizens and businesses to travel to and invest within Somaliland.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Somaliland Travel and Investment Act”.

## § 2. Ensuring smooth travel and investment in Somaliland

The Secretary of State shall, to improve the ability of United States citizens and businesses to travel to and invest within Somaliland, consider—

- **(1)** establishing a representative office in Hargeisa, Somaliland; and
- **(2)** bifurcating the travel advisory warnings between Somalia and Somaliland.
