# H.R. 10192

> To require the Comptroller General to submit to Congress a report on grants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Grant Responsibility and Accountability for Numerical Transparency Reform Act of 2026” or the “GRANT Reform Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Federal grants

- **(a)** *Annual GAO report on Federal grants required* Not later than 6 months after the date of the enactment of this Act, and annually thereafter, the Comptroller General shall make available on a publicly available website and submit to the relevant committees of Congress a report on Federal grants, with respect to the previous fiscal year, that includes the following:
  - **(1)** The name of, purpose for, and amount of money dispensed for each grant.
  - **(2)** The name of the entity that received funding for the grant.
  - **(3)** The name and a description of the project the grant is funding.
- **(b)** *Project reports from grant recipients required*
  - **(1)** *In general* Not later than 3 months after the date on which a project is completed, a recipient of a Federal grant for that project shall submit to the Comptroller General a report on the following:
    - **(A)** The aggregate amount of Federal funds used for salaries under the grant.
    - **(B)** The names of and amount of payments to subcontractors.
    - **(C)** The amount of administrative costs, materials, and any unspent funds.
    - **(D)** Any other information determined by the Director of the Office of Management and Budget.
  - **(2)** *Penalty for noncompliance* Any grant recipient that does not comply with paragraph (1) shall not be eligible for a Federal grant until the requirement is met.
  - **(3)** *Applicability* This subsection applies with respect to any grant distributed on and after the date of the enactment of this section.
- **(c)** *GAO annual report* Not later than 9 months after the date of the enactment of this section, and annually thereafter, the Comptroller General shall submit to the relevant committees of Congress a report that compiles the information submitted pursuant to subsection (b)(1).
- **(d)** *OMB guidance* Not later than 3 months after the date of the enactment of this section, the Director—
  - **(1)** shall issue guidance on the reports required pursuant to subsections (a), (b), and (c); and
  - **(2)** shall notify grant recipients of the report requirement described in subsection (b).
- **(e)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Director* The term Director means the Director of the Office of Management and Budget.
  - **(2)** *Relevant committees of Congress* The term relevant committees of Congress means the following:
    - **(A)** Each authorizing committee of the House of Representative and the Senate.
    - **(B)** The Appropriations Committee of the House of Representative and the Senate.
    - **(C)** The Committee on the Budget of the House of Representative and the Senate.
