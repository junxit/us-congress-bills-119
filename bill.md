# H.R. 11126

> To amend the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 to require the Secretary of Transportation to issue a final rule relating to family seating on flights, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Families Fly Together Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Family seating

Section 516 of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 (49 U.S.C. note prec. 42301) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsections (c) and (d) as subsections (d) and (e), respectively;
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (b) the following:
  > - **(c)** *Final rule* Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this subsection, the Secretary shall issue a final rule based on the notice of proposed rulemaking issued under subsection (a), which shall include the following:
  >   - **(1)** A prohibition on airlines from separating eligible families solely because they did not purchase premium seat assignments.
  >   - **(2)** A requirement on airlines to make family seating accommodations at no additional cost during the booking process.
  >   - **(3)** Compliance enforcement.
  >   - **(4)** Establishment of penalties for airlines that repeatedly violate the requirement.

- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(f)** *Eligible family* In this section, the term “eligible family” means a young child and an accompanying parent or legal guardian.
