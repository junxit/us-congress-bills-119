# H.R. 8537

> To authorize the construction of a ballroom on the grounds of the White House, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tactical Revitalization and Upgrade of Modern Presidential Ballroom Act” or the “TRUMP Ballroom Act”.

## § 2. Authorization for construction of ballroom on White House grounds

- **(a)** *Authorization* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the President may design and construct a ballroom facility on the grounds of the White House.
- **(b)** *Approval authority* The President shall have sole design and approval authority for the ballroom facility described in subsection (a).
