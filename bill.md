# S. 947

> To prohibit importation of commercially produced fresh citrus fruit originating from the People’s Republic of China.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States Citrus Protection Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on importation of commercially produced fresh citrus fruit originating from the People’s Republic of China

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, articles of commercially produced fresh citrus fruit originating from the People’s Republic of China may not be imported into the United States.
- **(b)** *Effective date* This Act shall take effect on the date that is 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
