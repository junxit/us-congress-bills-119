# H.R. 10624

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to carry out Executive Order 14253.

## § 1. Executive Order 14253

No Federal funds may be used to carry out Executive Order 14253 (90 Fed. Reg. 14563; relating to restoring truth and sanity in American history).
