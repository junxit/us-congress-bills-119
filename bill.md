# H.R. 9640

> To amend the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act to reauthorize the Earth Mapping Resources Initiative, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Earth MRI Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Earth Mapping Resources Initiative reauthorization

- **(a)** *Purpose* Section 40201(c)(1) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (43 U.S.C.31l(c)(1)) is amended by inserting “, which may include data derived from modern sensing technologies, including advanced geophysical data acquisition approaches such as airborne gravity gradiometry, digital geochemistry and other advanced geochemical characterization methods, real-time or near real-time data acquisition systems, and data-driven analytical methods to improve the characterization of critical mineral, geothermal, geologic hydrogen, and other subsurface resource systems” after “geophysical mapping”.
- **(b)** *Integration of data sources* Section 40201(g) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (43 U.S.C.31l(g)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by redesignating paragraph (5) as paragraph (6);
  - **(2)** in paragraph (4), by striking “and” at the end; and
  - **(3)** by inserting after paragraph (4) the following:
    > - **(5)** the 3D Hydrography Program of the United States Geological Survey; and

- **(c)** *Reauthorization* Section 40201(h) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (43 U.S.C.31l(h)) is amended by inserting “and $84,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2027 through 2031” after “2022 through 2026”.
- **(d)** *Contracts* Section 40201 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (43 U.S.C.31l) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(i)** *Contracts*
  >   - **(1)** *Preference given to certain entities* In entering into contracts pursuant to carrying out the Initiative, the Director of the United States Geological Survey shall, to the extent practicable, give preference to United States-owned and United States-controlled entities while ensuring that the selection process appropriately considers specialized expertise essential to the objectives of the Initiative.
  >   - **(2)** *Disclosure of ownership* To enter into a contract with the Director of the United States Geological Survey pursuant to the Initiative, an entity shall disclose the ownership of the primary contractor and each subcontractor expected to carry out any part of the contract.
