# H. RES. 726

> Supporting the recognition of September 16, 2025, as “National Voter Registration Day”.

## Preamble

- Whereas September 16th would be an appropriate day to establish National Voter Registration Day: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** supports the designation of “National Voter Registration Day”; and
- **(2)** encourages each voting-eligible citizen of the United States to—
  - **(A)** register to vote in his or her respective jurisdiction;
  - **(B)** confirm with their appropriate election official that his or her name, address, and other personal identifying information on record is current; and
  - **(C)** fulfill his or her civic duty by voting on election day if he or she would like to do so.
