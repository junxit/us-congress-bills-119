# H.R. 522

> To ensure equal treatment for certain faith-based organizations in certain Small Business Administration programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Assistance and Impartial Treatment of Help In Small Business Act” or the “FAITH in Small Business Act”.

## § 2. Ensuring equal treatment for certain faith-based organizations in certain Small Business Administration programs

The proposed rule of the Small Business Administration titled “Ensuring Equal Treatment for Faith-Based Organizations in SBA’s Loan and Disaster Assistance Programs” issued January 19, 2021, shall have the force and effect of law.
