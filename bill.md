# H.R. 10995

> To reauthorize the Intermediary Relending Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Borrowing Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Intermediary relending program

Section 310H(i) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1936b(i)) is amended by striking “2014 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
