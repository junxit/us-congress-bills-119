# S. 4484

> To amend the Intermodal Surface Transportation Efficiency Act of 1991 to designate United States Route 287 as Interstate Route I–47, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “I–47 Future Interstate Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Designation of Interstate Route I–47

- **(a)** *Designation as high priority corridor* Section 1105(c) of the Intermodal Surface Transportation Efficiency Act of 1991 (Public Law 102–240; 105 Stat. 2032; 135 Stat. 596) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(103)** United States Route 287 from State Highway 87 in Port Arthur, Texas, to United States Route 89 in Choteau, Montana.

- **(b)** *Designation and numbering as Interstate* Section 1105(e)(5) of the Intermodal Surface Transportation Act of 1991 (Public Law 102–240; 109 Stat. 597; 135 Stat. 598) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), in the first sentence, by striking “and subsection (c)(102)” and inserting “subsection (c)(102), and subsection (c)(103)”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (C)(i), by adding at the end the following: “The route referred to in subsection (c)(103) is designated as Interstate Route I–47.”.
