# H.R. 10379

> To establish certain requirements with respect to congressional districts in each State, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Problem Solvers Map-drawing Accountability and Principled Standards Act” or the “Problem Solvers MAPS Act”.

## § 2. Requirements for congressional districts

- **(a)** *Requirements with respect to congressional districts in each State*
  - **(1)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the congressional districts of each State shall meet each of the following requirements:
    - **(A)** Each congressional district in the State shall consist of contiguous territory (except to the extent necessary to include any area which is surrounded by a body of water).
    - **(B)** Each congressional district in the State shall be as compact in form as practicable.
    - **(C)** No congressional district in the State shall be drawn for the purpose of favoring or disfavoring competition, an incumbent, a particular candidate, or a particular political party.
    - **(D)** Congressional districts shall be drawn to achieve population equality as nearly as practicable, consistent with the Constitution of the United States.
  - **(2)** *Additional requirement with respect to establishment of congressional districts* In establishing each congressional district, the State shall consider the preservation of pre-existing political subdivisions, including counties, cities, tribal land boundaries, and towns.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The requirements under subsection (a) shall apply with respect to any congressional district established on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
