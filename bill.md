# H.R. 11111

> To direct the Secretary of Defense to submit a report to the congressional defense committees regarding the establishment of separation battalions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Units for Separation, Transition, Employment, and Readiness Act” or the “MUSTER Act”.

## § 2. Report regarding establishment of separation battalions

- **(a)** *Report required* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Defense shall submit to the congressional defense committees a written report regarding the feasibility and advisability of establishing dedicated separation battalions (or equivalent commands) in the Department of Defense to—
  - **(1)** streamline the last 90 days of service on active duty of a member separating from a covered Armed Force; and
  - **(2)** issue verified and interoperable learning and employment records to such members.
- **(b)** *Elements* The report under subsection (a) shall include the following elements regarding such establishment:
  - **(1)** Operational impacts.
  - **(2)** Estimated costs to the United States.
  - **(3)** Recommendations of the Secretary regarding pilot programs and partnerships with State entities.
- **(c)** *Covered Armed Force defined* In this section, the term “covered Armed Force” means the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, or Space Force.
