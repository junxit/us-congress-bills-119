# H.R. 10639

> To designate the Great Lakes Commission as a Regional Great Lakes Partnership, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Regional Great Lakes Partnership Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Designating the Great Lakes Commission as a Regional Great Lakes Partnership

Section 10202(b) of the James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023 (16 U.S.C. 1468(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (3), by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(E)** The Great Lakes Commission (established pursuant to the Great Lakes Basin Compact to which Congress granted consent in the Act of July 24, 1968 (Public Law 90–419; 82 Stat. 414)), comprised of the States of Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Wisconsin, New York, Ohio, and Pennsylvania.

- **(2)** in paragraph (4)—
  - **(A)** by striking “A partnership established under this section” and inserting “The partnership designated under paragraph (3)(E)”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “a Great Lake” and inserting “the Great Lakes”.
