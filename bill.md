# S. 3984

> To authorize a 2-year extension of the United States Commission on International Religious Freedom.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States Commission on International Religious Freedom Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. United States Commission on International Religious Freedom

- **(a)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 207(a) of the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6435(a)) is amended by striking “2025 and 2026” and inserting “2026 through 2028”.
- **(b)** *Extension of authorization* Section 209 of the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6436) is amended by striking “September 30, 2026” and inserting “September 30, 2028”.
