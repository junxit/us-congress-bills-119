# H.R. 1201

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to increase the number of physicians who may be provided Conrad 30 waivers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Doctors in our Borders Act”.

## § 2. Increased number of Conrad 30 waivers

Section 214(l)(1)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1184(l)(1)(B)) is amended by striking “to exceed 30” and inserting “to exceed 100”.
