# H.R. 10262

> To require the Secretary of Energy to request the National Petroleum Council to issue a report with respect to the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “21st Century Strategic Petroleum Reserve Act” or the “21st Century SPR Act”.

## § 2. Report on Strategic Petroleum Reserve modernization

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this section, the Secretary of Energy shall request that the National Petroleum Council—
  - **(1)** not later than one year after so requested, submit to the Secretary of Energy and Congress a report containing—
    - **(A)** an analysis of opportunities to improve and modernize the physical infrastructure and operation of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to respond to petroleum product supply shocks, including by—
      - **(i)** expanding the geographic distribution of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve;
      - **(ii)** expanding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to include storage of refined petroleum products;
      - **(iii)** eliminating congressionally mandated sales of petroleum products from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve;
      - **(iv)** creating storage facilities sufficiently resilient to withstand drawdowns of petroleum products of the volume and frequency required over the last 10 years of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s use;
      - **(v)** expanding the storage capacity of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve;
      - **(vi)** encouraging purchases of petroleum products to replenish the Strategic Petroleum Reserve during periods of low petroleum prices; and
      - **(vii)** increasing—
        - **(I)** the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s rate of fill; and
        - **(II)** the bidirectional flow capability of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve’s pipeline infrastructure;
    - **(B)** any recommendations for Federal agencies and Congress to improve and modernize the physical infrastructure and operation of the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to respond to petroleum product supply shocks; and
    - **(C)** an analysis and evaluation of the Life Extension II project, including work that was originally in-scope for the Life Extension II project but was subsequently not carried out; and
  - **(2)** make publicly available the report submitted under paragraph (1).
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Petroleum product* The term petroleum product has the meaning given such term in section 3 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6202).
  - **(2)** *Storage facility* The term storage facility has the meaning given such term in section 152 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6232).
  - **(3)** *Strategic Petroleum Reserve* The term Strategic Petroleum Reserve has the meaning given such term in section 152 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6232).
