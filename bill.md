# H.J. RES. 82

> Disapproving the action of the District of Columbia Council in approving the Insurance Regulation Amendment Act of 2024.

That the Congress disapproves of the action of the District of Columbia Council described as follows: The Insurance Regulation Amendment Act of 2024 (D.C. Act 25–699), enacted by the Council of the District of Columbia on January 15, 2025, and transmitted to Congress pursuant to section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act on February 6, 2025.
