# H.R. 9009

> To repeal the Hughes Amendment to the Firearm Owners’ Protection Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Firearm Freedom Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Repeal of ban on possession or transfer of machineguns

Section 922 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking subsection (o).
