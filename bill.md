# S. 419

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to reauthorize grants to support law enforcement officers and families, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reauthorizing Support and Treatment for Officers in Crisis Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 1001(a)(21) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10261(a)(21)) is amended by striking “2020 through 2024” and inserting “2025 through 2029”.
