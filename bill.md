# H.R. 10128

> To codify Secretary’s Order 3434 of the Department of the Interior.

## § 1. Codification of DOI Secretary’s Order 3434

Secretary’s Order 3434 issued by the Secretary of the Interior and dated June 25, 2025 (relating to strengthening coordination with gateway communities), shall have the force and effect of law.
