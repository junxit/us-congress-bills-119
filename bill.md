# H.R. 10592

> To amend the Fentanyl Sanctions Act to authorize such Act through December 31, 2033, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fentanyl Sanctions Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Extension of Fentanyl Sanctions Act

Section 7234 of the Fentanyl Sanctions Act (21 U.S.C. 2334) is amended by striking “the date that is 7 years after the date of the enactment of this Act” and inserting “December 31, 2033”.
