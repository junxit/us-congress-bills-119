# S. RES. 956

> Honoring the service and sacrifice of the veterans of the United States on Veterans Day, 2026.

## Preamble

- Whereas President Woodrow Wilson declared November 11, 1919, as the first observance of Armistice Day, on the one-year anniversary of the end of hostilities in World War I;
- Whereas, in 1938, Congress passed a law to establish November 11 annually as Armistice Day to honor World War I veterans;
- Whereas, following heroic actions from servicemembers in World War II and the Korean War, Congress passed Public Law 83–380 in 1954, to change Armistice Day to Veterans Day;
- Whereas Veterans Day honors all who have served in the United States military during war or peacetime;
- Whereas the Department of Veterans Affairs indicates that since the founding of the country, more than 41,000,000 people of the United States have earned the title of veteran;
- Whereas the Department of Veterans Affairs reports that, as of the date of the introduction of this resolution, approximately 17,300,000 veterans are living;
- Whereas veterans have kept the darkness of evil from extinguishing the blessed light of freedom;
- Whereas veterans have gone forward to fight kaisers, commissars, fascists, Nazis, and terrorists to preserve the freedoms of the United States;
- Whereas the families of the veterans also sacrifice greatly for the United States;
- Whereas veterans must continue to receive the services and support they rightfully deserve; and
- Whereas veterans service organizations and other groups provide critical advocacy on behalf of the veterans of the United States: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate—

- **(1)** honors the service and sacrifice of the veterans of the United States; and
- **(2)** calls on the people of the United States to observe Veterans Day in recognition of the heroic actions of veterans to protect the United States and allow freedom to endure.
