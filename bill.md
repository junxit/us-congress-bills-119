# H.R. 9278

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide that judicial review under the Administrative Procedure Act requires de novo trial of the facts when agency action seeks a sanction.

## § 1. De novo trial of facts when agency action seeks a sanction

Section 706 of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by inserting after “questions of law” the following “and questions of fact”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (F), by striking “to the extent” and all that follows through “by the reviewing court” and inserting the following: “, including the product of any rulemaking or adjudication conducted in accordance with the procedures required under section 553 or section 554”.
