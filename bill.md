# S. 5469

> To establish the Heart Security Foundation, a federally chartered charitable and nonprofit corporation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Heart Event Avoidance, Research, and Treatment Foundation Act” or the “HEART Foundation Act”.
