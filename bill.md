# S. 5325

> To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to cease all immigration detention operations at the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Delaney Hall Closure Act”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Department* The term Department means the Department of Homeland Security.
- **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Homeland Security.

## § 3. Closure of Delaney Hall Detention Center

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary—
  - **(1)** not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, shall cease all immigration detention operations at the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey; and
  - **(2)** shall not enter into any contract or agreement to operate the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey, as a facility to detain aliens in the custody of the Secretary.
- **(b)** *Termination for convenience*
  - **(1)** *In general* In the Government’s interest, the Secretary shall terminate any contract for the operation of the Delaney Hall Detention Center in Newark, New Jersey.
  - **(2)** *Liability* The Department shall be liable only for payment under the payment provisions of a contract described in paragraph (1) for services rendered before the date on which the termination of such contract becomes effective.
