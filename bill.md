# S. 5503

> To prohibit the salary of political appointees from exceeding the salary for Members of Congress.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Taxpayer Funded Compensation Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on salaries of political appointees

- **(a)** *Definition* In this section, the term political position—
  - **(1)** means—
    - **(A)** a position described under sections 5312 through 5316 of title 5, United States Code (relating to the Executive Schedule);
    - **(B)** a noncareer position in the Senior Executive Service, as described under section 3132(a)(7) of that title;
    - **(C)** a position in the executive branch of the Government of a confidential or policy-determining character under schedule C of subpart C of part 213 of title 5, Code of Federal Regulations;
    - **(D)** a position which has been excepted from the competitive service by reason of its confidential, policy-determining, policy-making, or policy-advocating character;
    - **(E)** a position described in section 105 of title 3, United States Code; or
    - **(F)** a position as a special Government employee (as defined in section 202 of title 18, United States Code), except for a member of the Armed Forces serving in such a position; and
  - **(2)** does not include the position of Vice President.
- **(b)** *Limitation* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the annual rate of basic pay that is payable for service in a political position may not be in excess of the annual rate of basic pay for a Senator or Member of the House of Representatives.
