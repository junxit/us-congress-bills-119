# H.R. 6720

> To amend the Farm Credit Act of 1971 to modify rural housing financing under that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fostering the Availability in Rural Markets of Home Loans Act of 2025” or the “FARM Home Loans Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Rural housing financing

Section 1.11(b) of the Farm Credit Act of 1971 (12 U.S.C. 2019(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “appurtenances” and inserting “appurtenances, including accessory dwelling units,”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (3), by striking “2,500” and inserting “10,000”.
