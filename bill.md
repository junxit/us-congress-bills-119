# H.R. 1120

> To abolish the John E. Fogarty International Center for Advanced Study in the Health Sciences.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Abolish the Fogarty International Center Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Abolishment of Fogarty International Center

The John E. Fogarty International Center for Advanced Study in the Health Sciences is abolished.
