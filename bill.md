# S. 2136

> To modify and extend the pilot program to accelerate contracting and pricing processes, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Smart Pricing Practices Permanence Act”.

## § 2. Modification and extension of pilot program to accelerate contracting and pricing processes

Section 890 of the John S. McCain National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2019 (Public Law 115–232; 10 U.S.C. note prec. 3701) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)(2), by striking “minimal reporting” and inserting “no unique reporting”; and
- **(2)** by striking subsection (d).
