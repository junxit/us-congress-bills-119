# H.R. 7631

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1999 to include Colorado in a program for western rural water, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Water Security Act”.

## § 2. Western rural water

Section 595 of the Water Resources Development Act of 1999 is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b), by inserting “Colorado,” before “Arizona”;
- **(2)** in subsection (c)(1), by inserting “Colorado,” before “Arizona”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (i)(1), by inserting “Colorado,” before “Idaho”.
