# S. 5620

> To amend title IV of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to establish the UNPLUGGED Schools Grant Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Utilize No Phones in Learning to Unleash Growth in Grades and Educate Distraction-free Schools Grant Act of 2026” or the “UNPLUGGED Schools Grant Act of 2026”.
