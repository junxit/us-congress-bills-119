# H.R. 10621

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to increase the State share of recovery under laws relating to false or fraudulent claims under the Medicaid program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicaid Fraud Fighting and Taxpayer Protection Act”.

## § 2. Increasing State share of recovery under law relating to false or fraudulent claims

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1909(a) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396h(a)) is amended by striking “10 percentage points” and inserting “25 percentage points”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply with respect to amounts recovered under a State action brought on or after the date of the enactment of this section.
