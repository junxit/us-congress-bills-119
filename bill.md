# H.R. 4564

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to create a special rule for treating trained individuals as trained personnel of a school for purposes of giving preference to States for children’s asthma treatment grant programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as “Dillon’s Law”.

## § 2. Preference for States that allow trained individuals to administer epinephrine at schools

- **(a)** *In general* Section 399L(d) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280g(d)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1)(F)—
    - **(A)** in the subparagraph heading, by striking “School personnel” and inserting “Trained personnel”;
    - **(B)** in the heading for clause (ii), by striking “school” and inserting “trained”; and
    - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(iv)** *Special rule* For purposes of this subparagraph, an elementary school or secondary school in a State may treat an individual who is not an employee of the school as being part of the trained personnel of the school if—
      >   - **(I)** the individual meets the requirements of paragraph (3)(E); and
      >   - **(II)** the State attorney general reaffirms the certification of the State under subparagraph (G), taking into account the treatment of individuals authorized by this clause.

  - **(2)** in paragraph (3)(E), in the matter preceding clause (i), by striking “, such as the school nurse”.
- **(b)** *Epinephrine delivery systems* Section 399L(d)(3)(C) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280g(d)(3)(C)) is amended by striking “auto-injectable epinephrine” and inserting “epinephrine delivery systems”.
- **(c)** *Correction* Section 399L(d)(1)(F)(i) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280g(d)(1)(F)(i)) is amended by striking “schoolin” and inserting “school in”.
