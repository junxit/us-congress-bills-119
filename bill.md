# H.R. 10997

> To reauthorize the National Rural Development Partnership.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Connecting Rural Stakeholders Act”.

## § 2. National Rural Development Partnership

Section 378 of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 2008m) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (g)(1), by striking “2008 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (h), by striking “2023” and inserting “2031”.
