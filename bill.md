# S. 4808

> To amend the Federal Credit Union Act with respect to conversion modernization for privately insured credit unions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Privately Insured Credit Unions Conversion Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Conversion modernization for privately 6 insured credit unions

Section 206(d)(2) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1786(d)(2)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the first sentence, by striking “in a vote in which at least 20 per centum of the total membership of the credit union participates”; and
- **(2)** in the second sentence, by striking “not more than thirty nor less than seven days” and inserting “not less than 90 days”.
