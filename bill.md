# H.R. 7913

> To amend the Food and Agriculture Act of 1977 to reauthorize grants and fellowships for food and agricultural sciences education, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Higher Education Challenge Grant Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Grants and fellowships for food and agricultural sciences education

- **(a)** *Priorities* Section 1417(c) of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 3152(c)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** applications for teaching enhancement projects, including paid work-based learning, that address a need for additional trained professionals in—
    >   - **(A)** food and agricultural sciences; or
    >   - **(B)** rural economic, community, and business development.

- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 1417(m) of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1977 (7 U.S.C. 3152(m)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (1), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** $60,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 through 2035.
