# H.J. RES. 40

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Defense relating to “Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Program”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Defense relating to “Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Program” (89 Fed. Reg. 83092 (October 15, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
