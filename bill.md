# S. 1013

> To require the headquarters of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration to be transferred to Brevard County, Florida.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Consolidating Aerospace Programs Efficiently at Canaveral Act” or “ CAPE Canaveral Act”.

## § 2. Transfer of National Aeronautics and Space Administration headquarters to Brevard County, Florida

Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the headquarters of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration shall be transferred to Brevard County, Florida.
