# S. 5518

> To establish the Artificial Intelligence Horizon Fund, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Make AI Work for Americans Act”.

## § 2. Table of contents

## § 3. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Artificial intelligence* The term artificial intelligence has the meaning given the term in section 5002 of the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020 (15 U.S.C. 9401).
- **(2)** *Artificial intelligence horizon fund* The term Artificial Intelligence Horizon Fund means the fund established under section 101(b)(1).
- **(3)** *Artificial intelligence advisory council* The term Artificial Intelligence Advisory Council means the council established by section 101(c)(1).
