# H.R. 4172

> To abolish the Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations within the Department of Energy, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “OCED Elimination Act”.

## § 2. Abolishment of OCED

The Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations within the Department of Energy is hereby abolished.

## § 3. Repeal of OCED provisions

Section 41201 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (42 U.S.C. 18861) is repealed.
