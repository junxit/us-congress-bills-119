# H.R. 9315

> To amend the Small Business Act, the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, title 40, United States Code, and title IV of the Social Security Act to replace the term “sheltered workshop” with “community rehabilitation program”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Workforce Dignity Act”.

## § 2. Replacing the term “sheltered workshop” with “community rehabilitation program”

- **(a)** *Programs for blind and handicapped individuals* Section 15(c)(6) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 644(c)(6)) is amended by striking “sheltered workshop” each place it appears and inserting “community rehabilitation program”.
- **(b)** *Task force on purchases from the blind and severely handicapped* Section 133(c) of the Small Business Administration Reauthorization and Amendment Act of 1988 (15 U.S.C. 644 note) is amended by striking “sheltered workshops” and inserting “community rehabilitation programs”.
- **(c)** *Deductions for personal exemptions* Section 152(d)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “sheltered workshop” each place it appears and inserting “community rehabilitation program”;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “such workshop” each place it appears and inserting “such program”; and
  - **(3)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “Sheltered workshop defined” in the heading and inserting “Community rehabilitation program defined”.
- **(d)** *Protection for veterans preference employees* Section 593 of title 40, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “sheltered workshop” each place it appears and inserting “community rehabilitation program”;
  - **(2)** by striking “sheltered workshops” each place it appears and inserting “community rehabilitation programs”; and
  - **(3)** in subsection (a)(2), by striking “Sheltered workshop” in the heading and inserting “Community rehabilitation program”.
- **(e)** *Foster care programs* Section 477(b)(3)(F) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 677(b)(3)(F)) is amended by striking “sheltered workshops” and inserting “community rehabilitation programs”.
- **(f)** *Rule of construction* This Act shall be construed to amend Federal law to remove the term “sheltered workshop” without any intent to change the application of such law.
