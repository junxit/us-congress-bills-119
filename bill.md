# H.R. 9811

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to increase funding for the Health Care Fraud and Abuse Control Account.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Anti-Fraud Fund Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Increasing funding for the Health Care Fraud and Abuse Control Account

Section 1817(k)(3)(A)(i) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395i(k)(3)(A)(i)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subclause (IV), by inserting “and further increased, for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2030, by $7,000,000,000” before the semicolon; and
