# S. 5679

> To prohibit the naming or renaming of a Naval vessel for an individual serving as the President or Vice President.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Officeholder Self-Endorsement of Legacy for Navy Vessels Act” or the “NO SELF Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on naming or renaming a Naval vessel for a sitting President or Vice President

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of the Navy may not name or rename a vessel of the Navy for an individual who, at the time of such naming or renaming, is serving as President or Vice President of the United States.
- **(b)** *Permissible naming or renaming* Nothing in this section shall prohibit the naming or renaming of a vessel for a former President or former Vice President after such individual leaves office.
