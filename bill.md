# H.R. 10602

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to authorize assistance for the project for water and wastewater infrastructure, Ottawa County, Michigan.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ottawa County Environmental Infrastructure Act”.

## § 2. Environmental infrastructure

Section 219(f) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 335; 138 Stat. 3115) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(599)** *Ottawa County, Michigan* $15,000,000 for water and wastewater infrastructure, including stormwater management and surface water resource protection, in Ottawa County, Michigan.
