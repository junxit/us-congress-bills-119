# H.R. 3625

> To prohibit the allocation of funds to the National Endowment for Democracy.

## § 1. Prohibition on the allocation of funds to the National Endowment for Democracy

- **(a)** *Prohibition* The head of an agency may not allocate any funds to the National Endowment for Democracy.
- **(b)** *Agency defined* In this section the term agency has the meaning given that term in section 551 of title 5, United States Code.
