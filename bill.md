# H.R. 4729

> To codify Executive Order 14292 relating to improving the safety and security of biological research.

## § 1. Codification of Executive Order 14292

Executive Order 14292 (90 Fed. Reg. 19611; relating to improving the safety and security of biological research) shall have the force and effect of law.
