# H.R. 6596

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to adjust the eligibility for the rural emergency hospital designation under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Adjusting eligibility for rural emergency hospital designation under Medicare

Section 1861(kkk)(3) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x(kkk)(3)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “as of” and inserting “, at any point during the period beginning on January 1, 2015, and ending on”;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(3)** in subparagraph (B), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(4)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(C)** was an off-campus outpatient department of a hospital (as defined in section 1833(t)(21)(B)) that—
  >   - **(i)** was a dedicated emergency department (as defined in section 489.24(b) of title 42, Code of Federal Regulations (or any successor regulation)); and
  >   - **(ii)** was located in a county (or equivalent unit of local government) in a rural area (as defined in section 1886(d)(2)(D)).
