# H.R. 11131

> To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to require paid rest breaks for certain construction employees, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lorenzo Salgado Araujo Construction Injury Prevention Act”.

## § 2. Findings

Congress finds the following:

- **(1)** Construction work is physically and mentally demanding. Construction workers routinely perform strenuous tasks, including lifting, climbing, bending, and operating machinery, requiring adequate opportunities for rest and recovery.
- **(2)** Rest breaks allow workers to recover from physical exertion. Regular breaks reduce physical strain, restore energy, and help workers to maintain focus and safely perform their jobs.
- **(3)** Construction workers face substantial heat exposure. Strenuous work, direct sunlight, hot environments, and protective equipment can prevent workers from effectively cooling their bodies and increase the risk of heat-related illness.
- **(4)** As extreme weather conditions, like extreme heat, increase in frequency, workers are forced to rely on electrolytes, personal fans, neck fans, cooling towels, and other cooling devices to manage heat while continuing to work. While these methods offer relief, these measures do not replace the need for adequate rest, recovery, and cooling breaks.

## § 3. Paid rest breaks for certain construction employees

- **(a)** *Paid rest break* The Fair Labor Standards Act (29 U.S.C. 201 et seq.) is amended by inserting after section 7 the following:
  > ## § 8. Paid rest breaks for construction employees
  >
  > - **(a)** *Requirements* An employer shall—
  >   - **(1)** with respect to each construction employee of such employer—
  >     - **(A)** provide at least one 15-minute paid rest break that meets the requirements of subsection (e) for every four hours of work to each such employee; and
  >     - **(B)** at the time of hiring, provide written notice in the primary language of such employee explaining that—
  >       - **(i)** such employee is entitled to the paid rest breaks described in subparagraph (A);
  >       - **(ii)** retaliation by such employer against such employee for requesting or taking such paid rest breaks is prohibited; and
  >       - **(iii)** such employee has a right to file a complaint with the Secretary for any violation of this section by such employer; and
  >   - **(2)** display a sign, in a conspicuous and accessible location, at the place of business of such employer and at the applicable construction work site that includes, in both English and Spanish, the information described in clauses (i) through (iii) of paragraph (1)(B).
  > - **(b)** *Posted notice* Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of the Lorenzo Salgado Araujo Construction Injury Prevention Act, the Secretary shall issue regulations with respect to the design and content of the sign referred to in subsection (a)(2).
  > - **(c)** *Retaliation prohibited* It is unlawful for any person to discharge or in any other manner discriminate against, or to cause any person to intimidate or in any other manner discriminate against any employee or job applicant for—
  >   - **(1)** requesting or taking paid rest breaks in accordance with this section;
  >   - **(2)** filing a complaint with the Secretary alleging a violation of this section; or
  >   - **(3)** cooperating in any investigation or other compliance action concerning an alleged violation of this section.
  > - **(d)** *Interaction with other laws* No provision of this section shall excuse noncompliance with any Federal, State, local law, municipal ordinance, or collective bargaining agreement requiring longer rest breaks than the rest break required under subsection (a)(1)(A).
  > - **(e)** *Rate of compensation* For each 15-minute paid rest break required under subsection (a)(1)(A), an employer shall compensate a construction employee of such employer at the regular rate at which the employee would have been paid during such break if the employee were performing work for the employer.
  > - **(f)** *Definitions* In this section:
  >   - **(1)** The term construction employee means an individual who is employed by an employer, or is hired as a contractor of such an employer, to perform general construction services.
  >   - **(2)** The term general construction services means all activities described in section 23 of the North American Industry Classification System.
  >   - **(3)** The term rest break means a break from work during work hours, but does not include a regular meal break period provided to a construction employee by an employer.

- **(b)** *Penalties* Section 16(b) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 216(b)) is amended by adding after the third sentence the following: “If the Secretary makes a determination that an employer has violated section 8(a) of this Act, and such employer fails to correct the violation during the 30-day period beginning on the date on which such employer receives notice from the Secretary of such determination, the Secretary shall assess an administrative penalty to such employer in an amount that equals 1 hour of the employee’s regular rate of pay for each day that a required rest break has not been provided to such employee pursuant to section 8(a) of this Act. Any employer that violates the provisions of section 8(c) of this Act shall be liable for such legal or equitable relief as may be appropriate, including without limitation employment, reinstatement, promotion, and the payment of wages lost and an additional equal amount as liquidated damages.”.
