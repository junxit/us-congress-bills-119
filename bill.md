# H.R. 10784

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide home ownership assistance, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Own Not Rent Act”.

## § 2. State programs to insure and guarantee secured mortgages

- **(a)** *In general* It is the sense of the Congress that States should establish programs to insure or guarantee mortgages that are secured by property located in such State.
- **(b)** *Fannie Mae* Section 302(b)(2) of the Federal National Mortgage Association Charter Act (12 U.S.C. 1717(b)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “; or (C) that portion” and inserting “; (C) that portion”;
  - **(2)** by striking “by a qualified insurer as determined by the corporation” and inserting the following: “by a qualified insurer as determined by the corporation; or (D) that portion of the unpaid principal balance of the mortgage which is in excess of such 80 per centum is insured or guaranteed under a qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program and the property securing the mortgage is located within the State whose program insures or guarantees the mortgage”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(8)** *Qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program defined* In this section, the term “qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program” means a mortgage insurance or guarantee program established under the laws of a State—
    >   - **(A)** that insures or guarantees, at a minimum, that portion of the unpaid principal balance of residential mortgages secured by properties located within such State which is in excess of 80 per centum of the value of the property securing the mortgage at the time of the mortgage;
    >   - **(B)** that is approved by the Director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency; and
    >   - **(C)** the obligations of which are backed by—
    >     - **(i)** the full faith and credit of the State in which such program is established; or
    >     - **(ii)** a dedicated insurance or guarantee fund that is established by State law and maintained in accordance with capital and reserve requirements determined by the Director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency to be sufficient to ensure the ability of the program to satisfy its obligations with respect to all insured or guaranteed mortgages.

- **(c)** *Freddie Mac* Section 305(a)(2) of the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation Act (12 U.S.C. 1454(a)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “; or (C) that portion” and inserting “; (C) that portion”;
  - **(2)** by striking “by a qualified insurer as determined by the corporation” and inserting the following: “by a qualified insurer as determined by the corporation; or (D) that portion of the unpaid principal balance of the mortgage which is in excess of such 80 per centum is insured or guaranteed under a qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program and the property securing the mortgage is located within the State whose program insures or guarantees the mortgage”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(10)** *Qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program defined* In this section, the term “qualifying State mortgage insurance or guarantee program” means a mortgage insurance or guarantee program established under the laws of a State—
    >   - **(A)** that insures or guarantees, at a minimum, that portion of the unpaid principal balance of residential mortgages secured by properties located within such State which is in excess of 80 per centum of the value of the property securing the mortgage at the time of the mortgage;
    >   - **(B)** that is approved by the Director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency; and
    >   - **(C)** the obligations of which are backed by—
    >     - **(i)** the full faith and credit of the State in which such program is established; or
    >     - **(ii)** a dedicated insurance or guarantee fund that is established by State law and maintained in accordance with capital and reserve requirements determined by the Director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency to be sufficient to ensure the ability of the program to satisfy its obligations with respect to all insured or guaranteed mortgages.

## § 3. Increase in limitation on penalty-free first-time homebuyer distributions

- **(a)** *In general* Section 72(t)(8)(B)(i) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “$10,000” and inserting “$25,000”.
- **(b)** *Inflation adjustment* Section 72(t)(8) of such Code is amended by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(G)** *Inflation adjustment*
  >   - **(i)** *In general* In the case of any taxable year beginning in a calendar year after 2026, the $25,000 amount in subparagraph (B)(i) shall be increased by an amount equal to—
  >     - **(I)** such dollar amount, multiplied by
  >     - **(II)** the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar year in which the taxable year begins, by substituting ‘calendar year 2026’ for ‘calendar year 2016’ in subparagraph (A)(ii), thereof.
  >   - **(ii)** *Rounding* Any increase determined under the preceding sentence shall be rounded to the nearest multiple of $100.

- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to distributions made after December 31, 2026, in taxable years ending after such date.

## § 4. Down payment savings accounts

- **(a)** *In general* Part VII of subchapter B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by redesignating section 224 as section 225 and inserting after section 223 the following new section:
  > ## § 224. Down payment savings accounts
  >
  > - **(a)** *Deduction allowed* In the case of an individual, there shall be allowed as a deduction for the taxable year an amount equal to the aggregate first-time homebuyer contributions paid in cash by or on behalf of such individual to a down payment savings account of such individual.
  > - **(b)** *Limitation*
  >   - **(1)** *Overall limitation on accounts* No deduction shall be allowed under subsection (a) with respect to any contribution made by or on behalf of any account beneficiary to the extent that, after such contribution, the fair market value of all assets of all down payment savings accounts of such account beneficiary would exceed $100,000.
  >   - **(2)** *Annual limitation on contributions*
  >     - **(A)** *In general* The amount allowable as a deduction under subsection (a) to an individual for any taxable year shall not exceed $25,000.
  >     - **(B)** *Reduction based on adjusted gross income*
  >       - **(i)** *In general* In the case of a taxpayer whose adjusted gross income for the taxable year immediately preceding the taxable year referred to in subparagraph (A) exceeds $250,000 (twice such amount in the case of a joint return), the dollar amount in effect under subparagraph (A) shall be reduced (but not below zero) by the amount which bears the same ratio to the dollar amount in effect under subparagraph (A) as such excess bears to the dollar amount in effect under subparagraph (A).
  >       - **(ii)** *Election to determine income based on current taxable year* In the case of a taxpayer which elects the application of this clause (at such time and in such manner as the Secretary may provide), clause (i) shall be applied with respect to the taxpayer’s adjusted gross income for the taxable year referred to in subparagraph (A).
  >     - **(C)** *Coordination with employer exclusion* The limitation which would (but for this subparagraph) apply under this paragraph to an individual for any taxable year shall be reduced (but not below zero) by the aggregate amount contributed to down payment savings accounts of such individual which is excludable from the taxpayer’s gross income for such taxable year under section 139J.
  >   - **(3)** *Denial of deduction to dependents* No deduction shall be allowed under this section to any individual with respect to whom a deduction under section 151 is allowable to another taxpayer for a taxable year beginning in the calendar year in which such individual’s taxable year begins.
  > - **(c)** *Qualified first-Time homebuyer contribution* For purposes of this section, the term qualified first-time homebuyer contribution means any contribution made to a down payment savings account if, at all times during the 2-year period ending on the date of such contribution, the account beneficiary (and, if married, such beneficiary’s spouse) did not have any present ownership interest in a principal residence (within the meaning of section 121).
  > - **(d)** *Down payment savings account* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *In general* The term down payment savings account means a trust created or organized in the United States as a down payment savings account exclusively for the purpose of making a qualified down payment of the account beneficiary, but only if the written governing instrument creating the trust meets the following requirements:
  >     - **(A)** Except in the case of a rollover contribution described in subsection (f)(5), no contribution will be accepted—
  >       - **(i)** unless it is in cash,
  >       - **(ii)** to the extent such contribution, when added to previous contributions to the trust for the calendar year, exceeds the dollar amount in effect under subsection (b)(2)(A), or
  >       - **(iii)** to the extent that, after such contribution, the fair market value of the assets of the trust would exceed the dollar amount in effect under subsection (b)(1).
  >     - **(B)** The trustee is a bank (as defined in section 408(n)), an insurance company (as defined in section 816), or another person who demonstrates to the satisfaction of the Secretary that the manner in which such person will administer the trust will be consistent with the requirements of this section.
  >     - **(C)** No part of the trust assets will be invested in life insurance contracts.
  >     - **(D)** The assets of the trust will not be commingled with other property except in a common trust fund or common investment fund.
  >     - **(E)** The interest of an individual in the balance in his account is nonforfeitable.
  >   - **(2)** *Qualified down payment* The term qualified down payment means, with respect to an account beneficiary, the qualified acquisition costs (as defined in section 72(t)(8)(C)) with respect to a principal residence (within the meaning of section 121) of such individual.
  >   - **(3)** *Account beneficiary* The term account beneficiary means the individual on whose behalf the down payment savings account was established.
  >   - **(4)** *Certain rules to apply* Rules similar to the following rules shall apply for purposes of this section:
  >     - **(A)** Section 219(d)(2) (relating to no deduction for rollovers).
  >     - **(B)** Section 219(f)(3) (relating to time when contributions deemed made).
  >     - **(C)** Section 219(f)(5) (relating to employer payments).
  >     - **(D)** Section 408(g) (relating to community property laws).
  >     - **(E)** Section 408(h) (relating to custodial accounts).
  > - **(e)** *Tax treatment of accounts*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* A down payment savings account is exempt from taxation under this subtitle unless such account has ceased to be a down payment savings account. Notwithstanding the preceding sentence, any such account is subject to the taxes imposed by section 511 (relating to imposition of tax on unrelated business income of charitable, etc. organizations).
  >   - **(2)** *Account termination* Rules similar to the rules of paragraphs (2) and (4) of section 408(e) shall apply to down payment savings accounts, and any amount treated as distributed under such rules shall be treated as not used to make a qualified down payment.
  > - **(f)** *Tax treatment of distributions*
  >   - **(1)** *Amounts used for qualified down payment* Any amount paid or distributed out of a down payment savings account which is used exclusively to make qualified down payments of any account beneficiary shall not be includible in gross income.
  >   - **(2)** *Inclusion of amounts not used for qualified down payments* Any amount paid or distributed out of a down payment savings account which is not used exclusively to make qualified down payments of the account beneficiary shall be included in the gross income of such beneficiary.
  >   - **(3)** *Excess contributions returned before due date of return*
  >     - **(A)** *In general* If any excess contribution is contributed for a taxable year to any down payment savings account of an individual, paragraph (2) shall not apply to distributions from the down payment savings accounts of such individual (to the extent such distributions do not exceed the aggregate excess contributions to all such accounts of such individual for such year) if—
  >       - **(i)** such distribution is received by the individual on or before the last day prescribed by law (including extensions of time) for filing such individual’s return for such taxable year, and
  >       - **(ii)** such distribution is accompanied by the amount of net income attributable to such excess contribution.
  >     - **(B)** *Excess contribution* For purposes of subparagraph (A), the term excess contribution means any contribution (other than a rollover contribution described in paragraph (5)) which is neither excludable from gross income under section 139J nor deductible under this section.
  >   - **(4)** *Additional tax on distributions not used for qualified down payments*
  >     - **(A)** *In general* The tax imposed by this chapter on the account beneficiary for any taxable year in which there is a payment or distribution from a down payment savings account of such beneficiary which is includible in gross income under paragraph (2) shall be increased by 20 percent of the amount which is so includible.
  >     - **(B)** *Exception for disability or death* Subparagraph (A) shall not apply if the payment or distribution is made after the account beneficiary becomes disabled within the meaning of section 72(m)(7) or dies.
  >   - **(5)** *Rollover contribution* An amount is described in this paragraph as a rollover contribution if it meets the requirements of subparagraphs (A) and (B).
  >     - **(A)** *In general* Paragraph (2) shall not apply to any amount paid or distributed from a down payment savings account to the account beneficiary to the extent the amount received is paid into a down payment savings account for the benefit of such beneficiary not later than the 60th day after the day on which the beneficiary receives the payment or distribution.
  >     - **(B)** *Limitation* This paragraph shall not apply to any amount described in subparagraph (A) received by an individual from a down payment savings account if, at any time during the 1-year period ending on the day of such receipt, such individual received any other amount described in subparagraph (A) from a down payment savings account which was not includible in the individual’s gross income because of the application of this paragraph.
  >   - **(6)** *Transfer of account incident to divorce* The transfer of an individual’s interest in a down payment savings account to an individual’s spouse or former spouse under a divorce or separation instrument described in clause (i) of section 121(d)(3)(C) shall not be considered a taxable transfer made by such individual notwithstanding any other provision of this subtitle, and such interest shall, after such transfer, be treated as a down payment savings account with respect to which such spouse is the account beneficiary.
  >   - **(7)** *Treatment after death of account beneficiary*
  >     - **(A)** *Treatment if designated beneficiary is spouse* If the account beneficiary’s surviving spouse acquires such beneficiary’s interest in a down payment savings account by reason of being the designated beneficiary of such account at the death of the account beneficiary, such down payment savings account shall be treated as if the spouse were the account beneficiary.
  >     - **(B)** *Other cases*
  >       - **(i)** *In general* If, by reason of the death of the account beneficiary, any person acquires the account beneficiary’s interest in a down payment savings account in a case to which subparagraph (A) does not apply—
  >         - **(I)** such account shall cease to be a down payment savings account as of the date of death, and
  >         - **(II)** an amount equal to the fair market value of the assets in such account on such date shall be includible if such person is not the estate of such beneficiary, in such person’s gross income for the taxable year which includes such date, or if such person is the estate of such beneficiary, in such beneficiary's gross income for the last taxable year of such beneficiary.
  >       - **(ii)** *Deduction for estate taxes* An appropriate deduction shall be allowed under section 691(c) to any person (other than the decedent or the decedent's spouse) with respect to amounts included in gross income under clause (i) by such person.
  > - **(g)** *Inflation adjustment*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* In the case of any taxable year beginning after December 31, 2026, the dollar amounts in paragraphs (1), (2)(A), (2)(B)(i) of subsection (b) shall each be increased by an amount equal to—
  >     - **(A)** such dollar amount, multiplied by
  >     - **(B)** the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar year in which such taxable year begins determined by substituting “calendar year 2026” for “calendar year 2016” in subparagraph (A)(ii), thereof.
  >   - **(2)** *Rounding* If any increase under paragraph (1) is not a multiple of $1,000, such increase shall be rounded to the nearest multiple of $1,000.
  > - **(h)** *Reports* The Secretary may require the trustee of a down payment savings account to make such reports regarding such account to the Secretary and to the account beneficiary with respect to contributions, distributions, the return of excess contributions, and such other matters as the Secretary determines appropriate. The reports required by this subsection shall be filed at such time and in such manner and furnished to such individuals at such time and in such manner as may be required by the Secretary.

## § 5. Credit for new owner-occupied homes

- **(a)** *In general* Subpart D of part IV of subchapter A of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 45BB. New owner-occupied homes credit
  >
  > - **(a)** *In general* For purposes of section 38, in the case of an eligible contractor, the new owner-occupied homes credit for any taxable year is 35 percent of the eligible construction costs of each new owner-occupied home which is—
  >   - **(1)** constructed by the eligible contractor, and
  >   - **(2)** acquired during such taxable year from such eligible contractor by an individual who certifies under penalty of perjury that such individual intends to use such home as the individual’s principal residence for not less than the 1-year period beginning on the date of acquisition.
  > - **(b)** *Definitions* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *Eligible contractor* The term eligible contractor has the meaning given such term by section 45L(b)(1).
  >   - **(2)** *Eligible construction costs* The term eligible construction costs means amounts properly chargeable to capital account with respect to a new owner-occupied home other than amounts allocable to land.
  >   - **(3)** *New owner-occupied home* The term new owner-occupied home means a dwelling unit—
  >     - **(A)** located in the United States, and
  >     - **(B)** the construction of which is substantially completed after the date of the enactment of this section.
  >   - **(4)** *Construction* The term construction shall not include reconstruction or rehabilitation.
  >   - **(5)** *Acquire* The term acquire includes purchase.
  > - **(c)** *Basis adjustment* For purposes of this subtitle, if a credit is allowed under this section in connection with any expenditure for any property, the increase in the basis of such property which would (but for this subsection) result from such expenditure shall be reduced by the amount of the credit so determined.
  > - **(d)** *Regulations* The Secretary shall issue such regulations or other guidance as the Secretary determines necessary to carry out the purposes of this subsection, including regulations or other guidance which provides for requirements for recordkeeping or information reporting for purposes of administering the requirements of this subsection.

- **(b)** *Credit made part of general business credit* Subsection (b) of section 38 of such Code is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “plus” at the end of paragraph (40),
  - **(2)** by striking the period at the end of paragraph (41) and inserting “, plus”, and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(42)** the new owner-occupied homes credit determined under section 45BB.

- **(c)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for subpart D of part IV of subchapter A of chapter 1 of such Code is amended by adding at the end the following new item:
- **(d)** *Effective Date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years ending after the date of the enactment of this Act.
