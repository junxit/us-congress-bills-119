# S.J. RES. 113

> Providing congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Rescission of Principles for Climate-Related Financial Risk Management for Large Financial Institutions”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Rescission of Principles for Climate-Related Financial Risk Management for Large Financial Institutions” (90 Fed. Reg. 51756 (November 18, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
