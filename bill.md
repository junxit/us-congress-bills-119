# H.R. 10141

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4450 Oak Park Lane in Fort Worth, Texas, as the “United States Representative Kay Granger Post Office Building”.

## § 1. United States Representative Kay Granger Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4450 Oak Park Lane in Fort Worth, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “United States Representative Kay Granger Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “United States Representative Kay Granger Post Office Building”.
