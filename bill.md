# H.R. 11165

> To amend the Egg Products Inspection Act to protect the sale by small farms and use of ungraded shell eggs that are clean, sound, and clearly labeled, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Treatment of ungraded shell eggs under Egg Products Inspection Act

The Egg Products Inspection Act is amended by inserting after section 7 (21 U.S.C. 1036) the following:

> ## § 7A. Ungraded shell eggs
>
> - **(a)** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, the Secretary may not find as adulterated or misbranded under this Act any ungraded shell eggs solely on the basis that such shell eggs have not been officially graded or found to meet specific grading standards, provided that such shell eggs meet the requirements described in subsection (b).
> - **(b)** With respect to ungraded shell eggs, the requirements described in this subsection are that such shell eggs—
>   - **(1)** are produced by an egg producer with a flock of three thousand or fewer hens;
>   - **(2)** are sold by such producer to consumers, restaurants, retail food establishments, or food service establishments within the State of such producer;
>   - **(3)** are clean and sound shell eggs;
>   - **(4)** are conspicuously labeled, on the carton, container, or document accompanying the sale or delivery of the shell eggs, with the statement “UNGRADED EGGS”;
>   - **(5)** in the case that the shell eggs have not undergone chemical sanitation, bear, on the carton, container, or document accompanying the sale or delivery of the shell eggs, a statement that the shell eggs have not been treated with a chemical sanitary agent;
>   - **(6)** bear, on the carton, container, or document accompanying the sale or delivery of the shell eggs, the name and physical address of the producer of the shell eggs; and
>   - **(7)** are stored and transported at an ambient temperature of not greater than 45 degrees Fahrenheit after being packed into a container destined for sale or delivery.

## § 2. Revisions to FDA Food Code

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall revise the 2022 Food Code, and any successor model food code, model ordinance, retail food protection standard, guidance document, training material, or other similar publication of the Food and Drug Administration, to reflect the amendment made by section 1.
