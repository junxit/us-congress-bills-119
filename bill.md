# H.R. 6307

> To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to fund the information clearinghouse through fiscal year 2032, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hunger Clearinghouse Enhancement Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Information clearinghouse

Section 26 of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1769g) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “, including information with respect to the utilization of trained volunteers,” after “provide information”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “resources that address ways to prevent hunger,” after “food assistance,”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (d)—
  - **(A)** by striking “and” before “$250,000”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “through 2027.” and inserting “through 2025, and $750,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 through 2032.”
