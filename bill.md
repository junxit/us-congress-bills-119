# S.J. RES. 195

> Disapproving the action of the District of Columbia Council in approving the Full Accountability in Arrest Reporting Temporary Amendment Act of 2026.

That the Congress disapproves of the action of the District of Columbia Council described as follows: The Full Accountability in Arrest Reporting Temporary Amendment Act of 2026 (D.C. Act 26–304), enacted by the Council of the District of Columbia on April 29, 2026, and transmitted to Congress pursuant to section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act on April 29, 2026.
