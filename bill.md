# S. 5427

> To require annual reports on the implementation of the strategic plan to counter illicit cross-border tunnel operations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Subterranean Border Defense Act”.

## § 2. Annual reports on counter illicit cross-border tunnel operations

Section 7134(a)(2) of the James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023 (Public Law 117–263; 6 U.S.C. 257 note) is amended by inserting “and annually thereafter,” after “strategic plan,”.
