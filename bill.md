# S. 941

> To prohibit natural asset companies from entering into any agreement with respect to land in the State of Utah or natural assets on or in land in the State of Utah.

## § 1. Prohibition of natural asset companies from entering into certain agreements

- **(a)** *Definition of natural asset company* In this section, the term “natural asset company” means—
  - **(1)** a corporation that—
    - **(A)** holds the rights to the ecological performance of a defined area; and
    - **(B)** has the authority to manage the defined area for conservation, restoration, or sustainable management; or
  - **(2)** a company or organization that is substantially similar to a corporation described in paragraph (1).
- **(b)** *Prohibition* A natural asset company may not enter into any agreement with respect to—
  - **(1)** land in the State of Utah; or
  - **(2)** natural assets on or in land in the State of Utah.
