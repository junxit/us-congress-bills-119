# S.J. RES. 168

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Consumer Financial Protection Circular 2024–01: Preferencing and Steering Practices by Digital Intermediaries for Consumer Financial Products or Services”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Consumer Financial Protection Circular 2024–01: Preferencing and Steering Practices by Digital Intermediaries for Consumer Financial Products or Services (89 Fed. Reg. 17706 (March 12, 2024))” (90 Fed. Reg. 20084 (May 12, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
