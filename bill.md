# H. RES. 1453

> Directing the Committee on Ethics to publicly release certain final reports prepared during the One Hundred Nineteenth Congress.

That the Committee on Ethics shall, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, publicly release any final report prepared by the Committee during the One Hundred Nineteenth Congress as of the date of the adoption of this resolution, including any associated findings, recommendations, and proposed disciplinary actions, with respect to investigations into any alleged violation by a Member of the House of the Code of Official Conduct of the House or of a law, rule, regulation, or other standard of conduct.
