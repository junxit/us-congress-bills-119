# H.R. 10966

> To require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to ensure that medical facilities of the Department of Veterans Affairs maintain sufficient staffing levels of maternity care coordinators, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Veteran Mothers Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Requirements for coordination of maternity care

- **(a)** *Staffing of maternity care coordinators*
  - **(1)** *In general* Chapter 17 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting after section 1712C the following new section:
    > ## § 1713. Maternity care; staffing of coordinators
    >
    > - **(a)** *Staffing requirement* The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall ensure that each covered medical facility maintains a staffing level for maternity care coordination services sufficient to ensure that the facility maintains, as a minimum requirement, at least one full-time equivalent individual assigned to maternity care coordination duties (and not assigned to any unrelated duties) for—
    >   - **(1)** every 75 to 100 pregnant or postpartum patients per year; and
    >   - **(2)** every 100 pregnant or postpartum patients per year.
    > - **(b)** *Periodic review* The Secretary shall require the head of each covered medical facility to carry out, at least once every two years, a review of its staffing levels to—
    >   - **(1)** document—
    >     - **(A)** each individual assigned to maternity care coordination duties and the extent to which the individual is also assigned to unrelated duties; and
    >     - **(B)** the number of pregnant or postpartum patients per year; and
    >   - **(2)** determine whether the facility meets the minimum requirement specified in subsection (a)(2).
    > - **(c)** *Staffing action plan* If the head of a covered medical facility determines that the facility does not meet the minimum requirement specified in subsection (a)(2), the head of the facility shall, not later than 45 days after completion of the review, submit to the Secretary and the appropriate congressional committees a staffing action plan that describes the staffing adjustments required to meet such minimum requirement and the timelines and resources required to make those adjustments. The head of the facility shall promptly implement the plan.
    > - **(d)** *Waiver* The head of a covered medical facility may request, and the Secretary may grant, a waiver of the requirement under subsection (c) to submit and implement a plan if there are extraordinary circumstances (such as a workforce shortage in a specific geographic area) make such a plan unlikely to succeed within a reasonable time.
    > - **(e)** *Guidance* The Secretary shall issue guidance to carry out this section, which shall include standard methodologies to be used by covered medical facilities in carrying out the reviews under subsection (b).
    > - **(f)** *Definitions* In this section:
    >   - **(1)** The term appropriate congressional committees means the Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Senate.
    >   - **(2)** The term “covered medical facility” means a medical facility of the Department of Veterans Affairs that has more than 100 patients.
    >   - **(3)** The term maternity care coordination duties means the duties specified in Veterans Health Administration Directive 1330.03, or any successor guidance, including coordination of maternity care from confirmation of pregnancy through the postpartum period.

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of contents for such chapter is amended by inserting after the item relating to section 1712C the following new item:
- **(b)** *Implementation*
  - **(1)** *Guidance* The initial guidance required by subsection (d) of section 1713 of title 38, United States Code, as added by this section, shall be issued not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
  - **(2)** *Reviews* The first reviews required to be carried out by the heads of covered medical facilities under subsection (b) of such section 1713 shall be completed not later than the date that is 240 days after the date on which the initial guidance is issued, as described in paragraph (1).
- **(c)** *Annual reports*
  - **(1)** *To congressional committees* Not later than one year after the date on which the first reviews are required to be carried out, as described in subsection (b)(2), and annually thereafter for four years, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall submit to the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the Senate a report that includes—
    - **(A)** the number of facilities that completed the review during the period covered by the report and the dates of completion;
    - **(B)** aggregated data on the number of individuals and patients documented under subsection (b) of such section 1713, the number of full-time equivalent individuals described in subsection (a) of such section 1713, and the percentage of facilities that met the minimum requirement in subsection (a)(2) of such section 1713;
    - **(C)** a summary of staffing action plans submitted by the heads of facilities under subsection (c) of such section 1713, including timelines and resource needs;
    - **(D)** the number and disposition of any waiver requests under subsection (d) of such section 1713; and
    - **(E)** metrics on the capacity and performance of maternity care coordinators, including—
      - **(i)** the percentage of maternity care coordinators who self-report having sufficient time to perform maternity care coordination duties (using the same response categories as used by the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Veterans Affairs in preparing VA OIG 22–00900–186 (June 27, 2024; relating to “Review of Perceived Barriers in Coordinating Veteran Maternity Care”)); and
      - **(ii)** any other metrics the Secretary determines appropriate.
  - **(2)** *Public summary* The Secretary shall publish a public summary of each such report on the public website of the Department.
