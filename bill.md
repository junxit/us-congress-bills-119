# H.R. 1192

> To ensure that Big Cypress National Preserve may not be designated as wilderness or as a component of the National Wilderness Preservation System, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition on Big Cypress National Preserve designation as Wilderness

Big Cypress National Preserve may not be designated as wilderness or as a component of the National Wilderness Preservation System.
