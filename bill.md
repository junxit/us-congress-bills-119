# H.R. 10772

> To evaluate and invest in Ukrainian defense technologies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Frontline Innovation Act”.

## § 2. Actions to evaluate and invest in Ukrainian defense technologies

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Defense Innovation Unit of the Department of Defense may take such actions as may be necessary to—

- **(1)** evaluate and invest in Ukrainian defense technologies; and
- **(2)** ensure that the United States can benefit directly from defense technologies described in paragraph (1), including those technologies that have been developed and refined on Ukraine’s battlefield.
