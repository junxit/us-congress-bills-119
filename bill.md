# H.R. 10168

> To authorize the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to establish a pilot program to recapture and reallocate rental assistance for veterans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans Housing Opportunity Act”.

## § 2. HUD-VASH National Pool pilot program

- **(a)** *Establishment* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this section, the Secretary, in consultation with the Secretary of Veterans Affaris, shall establish a pilot program to be known as the “HUD-VASH National Pool Pilot Program”.
- **(b)** *Recapture and reallocation*
  - **(1)** *In general* Under the pilot program, the Secretary—
    - **(A)** shall identify public housing agencies with chronically underutilized HUD-VASH voucher assistance, as determined by the Secretary;
    - **(B)** may recapture such voucher assistance; and
    - **(C)** may reallocate such assistance to redistribute HUD-VASH voucher assistance to public housing agencies through a process established by the Secretary.
  - **(2)** *Notice to PHAs with underutilization* Upon a finding that a public housing agency is chronically underutilizing HUD-VASH voucher assistance as described in paragraph (1)(A), the Secretary shall—
    - **(A)** notify such public housing agency of the underutilization; and
    - **(B)** provide such public housing agency with a period of time, as determined by the Secretary, to utilize such voucher assistance before recapturing and reallocating pursuant to subparagraphs (B) and (C) of paragraph (1).
- **(c)** *Availability of vouchers* The HUD-VASH voucher assistance that is reallocated pursuant to subsection (b)(1)(C) shall be made available to veterans that are—
  - **(1)** homeless; or
  - **(2)** at risk of homelessness, as determined by the Secretary.
- **(d)** *Report* Not later than 3 years after the date of the enactment of this section, the Secretary shall submit to the Congress a report that describes—
  - **(1)** HUD-VASH voucher utilization rates;
  - **(2)** housing outcomes for veterans;
  - **(3)** impacts of the pilot program on the administration of HUD-VASH vouchers; and
  - **(4)** whether the pilot program has improved the efficiency of the HUD-VASH voucher program.
- **(e)** *Sunset* The pilot program established by this section shall terminate on the date that is 5 years after the date of the enactment of this section, except as provided in subsection (f).
- **(f)** *Participants after termination* Any individual that receives a HUD-VASH voucher under the pilot program, and retains such voucher after the sunset described in subsection (d), shall retain such voucher under the standard HUD-VASH program described in section 8(o)(19) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437f(o)(19)) and any implementing regulations.
- **(g)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Chronically underutilized* The term “chronically underutilized” means sustained underutilization over a meaningful period of time, as determined by the Secretary.
  - **(2)** *HUD-VASH voucher assistance* The term “HUD-VASH voucher assistance” means rental assistance provided—
    - **(A)** under section 8(o)(19) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437f(o)(19)); or
    - **(B)** any similar assistance made under an appropriations Act.
  - **(3)** *Public housing agency* The term “public housing agency” has the meaning given the term in section 3(b) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437a(b)).
  - **(4)** *Secretary* The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development.
  - **(5)** *Veteran* The term “veteran” has the meaning given the term in section 2002 of title 38, United States Code.
