# S. 5031

> To amend the National and Community Service Act of 1990 to expand participation by underrepresented populations in service programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Building Community in America Act”.

## § 2. Expansions of participation in service programs

- **(a)** *Purpose* Section 2(b) of the National and Community Service Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 12501(b)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (18), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (19), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(20)** expand participation in national and community service among populations underrepresented in service programs, including young adults underrepresented in mentoring, coaching, youth development, and other community-building activities.

- **(b)** *Priority*
  - **(1)** *National and Community Service Act of 1990* The National and Community Service Act of 1990 is amended—
    - **(A)** in section 122(f) (42 U.S.C. 12572(f)), by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(5)** *Required priority* The national service priorities under paragraph (1) shall include a priority for programs that prioritize the recruitment and training of underrepresented participants, including men, for activities including—
      >   - **(A)** youth coaching or mentoring activities;
      >   - **(B)** school attendance or after-school support activities;
      >   - **(C)** activities in a national service position as part of a conservation corps;
      >   - **(D)** disaster response activities;
      >   - **(E)** public safety, violence interruption, or reentry support activities; or
      >   - **(F)** skilled-trades, apprenticeships, or workforce-linked services.

    - **(B)** in section 157 (42 U.S.C. 12617), by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(d)** *Priority* In developing project proposals under subsection (b)(1) and selecting projects to be performed under subsection (c)(1), there shall be a priority for projects that prioritize the recruitment and training of underrepresented participants, including men, for any of the activities described in section 122(f)(5).

  - **(2)** *Domestic volunteer service act of 1973* Section 103 of the Domestic Volunteer Service Act of 1973 (42 U.S.C. 4953) is amended by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(j)** In the assignment of volunteers under this section, the Director shall give priority to projects or programs that prioritize the recruitment and training of underrepresented participants, including men, for any of the activities described in section 122(f)(5) of the National and Community Service Act of 1990.
