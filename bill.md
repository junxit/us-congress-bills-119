# H.R. 10456

> To provide for the replacement of the plaque at Lincoln Memorial commemorating the “I Have a Dream” speech of Martin Luther King, Jr.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renewing the Dream Act”.

## § 2. Replacement of plaque at Lincoln Memorial

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of the Interior shall remove the plaque at Lincoln Memorial placed pursuant to Public Law 106–365 and install a new plaque commemorating the speech of Martin Luther King, Jr., known as the “I Have a Dream” speech.
- **(b)** *Relation to CWA* Chapter 89 of title 40, United States Code (commonly known as the “Commemorative Works Act”), shall apply to the design and placement of the new plaque.
- **(c)** *Acceptance of contributions* The Secretary of the Interior may accept contributions toward the cost of preparing and installing the new plaque without further appropriation.
- **(d)** *Federal funds* Federal funds may be used to—
  - **(1)** remove the plaque placed pursuant to Public Law 106–365; and
  - **(2)** design, procure, and install the new plaque.
