# S. 4823

> To amend the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 to include quantum information science and technology in the areas covered by the Program on China and Transformational Exports.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Advancing American Quantum Leadership Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Modification to Program on China and Transformational Exports

Section 2(l)(1)(B)(v) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 (12 U.S.C. 635(l)(1)(B)(v)) is amended by striking “Quantum computing.” and inserting “Quantum information science and technology.”.
