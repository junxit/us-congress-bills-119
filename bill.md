# S. 2906

> To amend the Investment Company Act of 1940 to address entities that are not considered to be investment companies for the purposes of that Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expanding American Entrepreneurship Act”.

## § 2. Investment companies

Section 3(c)(1) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80a–3(c)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), in the first sentence, by striking “250 persons” and inserting “500 persons”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C)(i), by striking “$10,000,000” and inserting “$50,000,000”.
