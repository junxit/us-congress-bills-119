# S. 1894

> To amend MAP–21 to modify provisions relating to a categorical exclusion for projects of limited Federal assistance, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Projects Expedited Execution and Delivery Act” or the “SPEED Act”.

## § 2. Categorical exclusion for projects of limited Federal assistance

Section 1317(1) of MAP–21 (23 U.S.C. 109 note; Public Law 112–141) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “$6,000,000” and inserting “$12,000,000”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “$35,000,000” and inserting “$70,000,000”.
