# H.R. 4662

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6444 San Fernando Road in Glendale, California, as the “Paul Ignatius Post Office”.

## § 1. Paul Ignatius Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6444 San Fernando Road in Glendale, California, shall be known and designated as the “Paul Ignatius Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Paul Ignatius Post Office”.
