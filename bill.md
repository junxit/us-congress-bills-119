# H.J. RES. 91

> Relating to a national emergency by the President on April 2, 2025.

That pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on April 2, 2025, in Executive Order 14257 (25 Fed. Reg. 06063), is hereby terminated.
