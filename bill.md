# H.R. 10711

> To require the Secretary of State to submit a report to Congress regarding staffing levels and wait times related to visa applications, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reducing Visa Delays Act”.

## § 2. Report and authorization regarding embassy staffing levels and visa wait times

- **(a)** *Report to Congress* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall submit to Congress a report regarding—
  - **(1)** the adequacy of staffing levels at United States embassies and consulates to reduce visa processing wait times and visa appointment delays;
  - **(2)** whether each embassy and consulate is equipped with the technology and resources necessary to efficiently carry out the consular duties of such embassy or consulate;
  - **(3)** the effects of the social media vetting policies for visa applicants instituted by the Secretary after January 20, 2025, on visa processing wait times and visa appointment delays; and
  - **(4)** the number of security flags that were identified through such social media vetting.
- **(b)** *Publication of wait times* The Secretary shall publish monthly on a publically accessible website of the Department of State—
  - **(1)** information regarding the previous average and current estimated time between an interview for a visa and the adjudication of the application for such visa, in aggregate and disaggregated by diplomatic or consular post and visa category; and
  - **(2)** a justification for such average and estimated times.
- **(c)** *Strategic plan for increased staffing* The Secretary shall develop a strategic plan—
  - **(1)** to increase staffing levels at consular offices and embassies to reduce visa appointment wait times and processing delays; and
  - **(2)** that includes any other solutions found to reduce appointment wait times and processing delays with respect to visa processing.
