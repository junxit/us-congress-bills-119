# S. 4838

> To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to exempt certain employees engaged in outdoor recreational outfitting or guiding services from minimum wage and maximum hours requirements.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Outdoor Recreational Outfitting and Guiding Act”.

## § 2. Application of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to certain employees engaged in outdoor recreational outfitting or guiding services

Section 13(a) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 213(a)) is amended by inserting after paragraph (1) the following:
