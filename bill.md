# S. 5684

> To require the debarment of any Federal contractor who improperly discloses personally identifiable information, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Cash for Incompetent and Malicious Contractors Act”.

## § 2. Debarment of contractors who improperly disclose personally identifiable information

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 47 of title 41, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 4715. Debarment of contractors who improperly disclose personally identifiable information
  >
  > - **(a)** *Debarment for legal finding of improper disclosure*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* The head of an executive agency shall debar a contractor from eligibility for Federal contracts for a conviction or civil judgment against the contractor, or an employee of the contractor, for non-consensual disclosure of personally identifiable information of a client of the contractor, including with respect to the client—
  >     - **(A)** the social security number;
  >     - **(B)** return or return information, as defined in paragraphs (1) and (2) of section 6103(b) of title 26;
  >     - **(C)** individually identifiable health information, as defined in section 160.103 of title 45, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor regulation; or
  >     - **(D)** any trade secret, as defined in section 1839 of title 18.
  >   - **(2)** *Length of debarment* A debarment under paragraph (1) shall be for a period of not less than one year and not more than 5 years, dependent on the severity of the conduct.
  >   - **(3)** *Procedure* A debarment under paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the procedural requirements of section 9.406–3 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, or any successor regulation.
  > - **(b)** *Suspension pending investigation of improper disclosure*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* The head of an executive agency shall suspend a contractor from eligibility for Federal contracts if the contractor, or an employee of the contractor, is charged with commission of a criminal offense involving disclosure of personally identifiable information of a client of the contractor, including the information listed in subparagraphs (A) through (D) of subsection (a)(1).
  >   - **(2)** *Length of suspension* A suspension under paragraph (1) shall be for the period prescribed in section 9.407–4 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, or any successor regulation.
  >   - **(3)** *Procedure* A suspension under paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the procedural requirements of section 9.407–3 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation, or any successor regulation.
