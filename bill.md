# H.R. 7476

> To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to reauthorize certain water infrastructure grants, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Water Infrastructure Assistance Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Water, waste disposal, and wastewater facility grants

Section 306(a)(2)(B)(vii) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1926(a)(2)(B)(vii)) is amended by striking “2019 through 2023” and inserting “2026 through 2031”.
