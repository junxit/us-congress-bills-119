# S. 4650

> To amend the Plant Protection Act to improve plant pest and disease preparedness for specialty crops.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Improving Pest and Disease Preparedness for Specialty Crops Act”.

## § 2. Plant pest and disease management and disaster prevention

Section 420 of the Plant Protection Act (7 U.S.C. 7721) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b), by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(8)** *Priority* In using funds made available to carry out this section, the Secretary shall prioritize cooperative agreements under this subsection to carry out activities under this subsection in high-risk States.

- **(2)** in subsection (f)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (6), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (7), by striking “and each fiscal year thereafter.” and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(8)** $150,000,000 for fiscal year 2027 and each fiscal year thereafter.

- **(3)** in subsection (g), by striking “$5,000,000” and inserting “$12,000,000”.
