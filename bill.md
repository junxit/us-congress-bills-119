# H.J. RES. 48

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System relating to “Quality Control Standards for Automated Valuation Models”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System relating to “Quality Control Standards for Automated Valuation Models” (89 Fed. Reg. 64538 (August 7, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
