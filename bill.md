# H.R. 10213

> To name the community-based outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs in Medina County, Ohio, as the “Leland John Codding VA Clinic”.

## § 1. Name of Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic, Medina County, Ohio

- **(a)** *In general* The community-based outpatient clinic of the Department of Veterans Affairs located at 6374 Alder Lane, Medina, Ohio 44256, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Leland John Codding Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic” or the “Leland John Codding VA Clinic”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the clinic described in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the “Leland John Codding Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic”.
