# H.R. 9789

> To amend section 1839 of title 18, United States Code, to provide that an entity domiciled in a foreign adversary country is a foreign instrumentality for purposes of the prohibition on economic espionage under such section.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop PRC Economic Espionage Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Providing that an entity domiciled in a foreign adversary country is a foreign instrumentality

Section 1839(1) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “is substantially owned, controlled, sponsored, commanded, managed, or dominated by a foreign government;” and inserting the following:

> is—
> - **(A)** substantially owned, controlled, sponsored, commanded, managed, or dominated by a foreign government; or
> - **(B)** domiciled in a covered nation, as defined in section 4872 of title 10;
