# S. 2211

> To reauthorize the Special Diabetes Program for Type 1 Diabetes and the Special Diabetes Program for Indians.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Special Diabetes Program Reauthorization Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Extension of programs

- **(a)** *Special diabetes programs for type I diabetes* Section 330B(b)(2) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–2(b)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (F), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (G), by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(H)** $160,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 and 2027, to remain available until expended; and
    > - **(I)** $40,000,000 for the period beginning on October 1, 2027, and ending on December 31, 2027, to remain available until expended.

- **(b)** *Special diabetes program for Indians* Section 330C(c)(2) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–3(c)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (F), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (G), by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(H)** $160,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 and 2027, to remain available until expended; and
    > - **(I)** $40,000,000 for the period beginning on October 1, 2027, and ending on December 31, 2027, to remain available until expended.
