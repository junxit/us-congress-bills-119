# H.R. 10571

> To amend the Public Health Service Act with respect to State support for newborn screening, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Surge to Save Newborns Act”.

## § 2. Special program for State support for newborn screening

The Public Health Service Act is amended by inserting after section 330C (42 U.S.C. 254c–3) the following:

> ## § 330D. Special grants for newborn screening
>
> - **(a)** *Establishment* The Secretary shall establish a program to provide grants to States for timely implementation of newborn screening for conditions on the recommended uniform screening panel maintained by the Secretary (or any successor panel).
> - **(b)** *Eligibility* To be eligible to receive a grant under this section, an entity shall—
>   - **(1)** be—
>     - **(A)** a chief executive officer for health of a State, or the designee of such officer; or
>     - **(B)** a State governmental agency; and
>   - **(2)** shall submit to the Secretary an application—
>     - **(A)** at such time and in such manner as the Secretary may require; and
>     - **(B)** containing a description of how the entity proposes to use grant funds for implementation of newborn screening for conditions on the recommended uniform screening panel referred to in subsection (a).
> - **(c)** *Annual reports* Not later than the end of fiscal year 2027, and each fiscal year thereafter through the end of fiscal year 2031, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report that includes—
>   - **(1)** a State-by-State identification of newborn screening implementation for conditions on the recommend uniform screening panel referred to in subsection (a), including—
>     - **(A)** associated conditions; and
>     - **(B)** for each State, the specific conditions for which screening has not been implemented;
>   - **(2)** an evaluation of the effectiveness of grants under this section; and
>   - **(3)** any recommendations of the Secretary for legislative or administrative action.
> - **(d)** *Funding* Out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, there is appropriated $35,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to remain available until expended, for grants under this section.
