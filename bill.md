# H.R. 10166

> To amend the Foreign Agents Registration Act to require social media influencers to submit registration statements, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Foreign Propaganda Disclosure Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of social media influencers

Section 1 of the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as amended (22 U.S.C. 611) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (h), by inserting “a social media influencer or” after “includes”;
- **(2)** in subsection (p), by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new subsections:
  > - **(r)** The term social media influencer means any person who has a monetized social media account on any social media platform;
  > - **(s)** The term social media platform has the meaning given such term defined in section 124 of the Trafficking Victims Prevention and Protection Reauthorization Act of 2022 (42 U.S.C. 1862w).
