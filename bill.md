# S. 3172

> To repeal certain Acts that impose sanctions upon Syria.

## § 1. Repeals

- **(a)** *Syria Accountability and Lebanese Sovereignty Restoration Act of 2003* The Syria Accountability and Lebanese Sovereignty Restoration Act of 2003 (Public Law 108–175; 22 U.S.C. 2151 note) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Syria Human Rights Accountability Act of 2012* The Syria Human Rights Accountability Act of 2012 (title VII of Public Law 112–158; 22 U.S.C. 8701 et seq.) is repealed.
