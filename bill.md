# H.R. 3969

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to repeal the restriction on the use of funds and facilities of the Department of Defense for abortion care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Access to Reproductive Care and Health for Military Servicemembers Act” or the “MARCH for Military Servicemembers Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of restriction on use of funds and facilities of Department of Defense for abortion care

Section 1093 of title 10, United States Code, is repealed.
