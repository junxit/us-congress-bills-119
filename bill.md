# H.R. 10908

> To implement Universal Savings Accounts and expand transparency, competition, and patient rights in health care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ending Patient Runaround Act”.

## § 3. Transparency

- **(a)** *Requirements relating to provision of prior authorization approval or denial* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2730. Requirements relating to provision of prior authorization approval or denial
  >
  > A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage that receives a request to authorize a procedure or the provision of a service or item under the plan or coverage shall—
  >
  > - **(1)** issue the approval or denial of the request—
  >   - **(A)** in writing to the medical provider and patient;
  >   - **(B)** through a secure portal; and
  >   - **(C)** within 30 days after receipt of the request; and
  > - **(2)** in the case a procedure or provision of service is denied and a peer to peer review is invoked, shall provide a peer that is in the same specialty as the medical provider.

- **(b)** *Prohibition on changing the tier list of a drug To be more restrictive, or a service not covered by the insurer* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is further amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2731. Prohibition on changing the tier list of a drug to be more restrictive, or a service not covered by the insurer
  >
  > A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage may not, during the term of the coverage, change—
  >
  > - **(1)** the tier in which a prescription drug is listed to be more restrictive; or
  > - **(2)** the list of services not covered by the plan or coverage.

- **(c)** *Information required To be provided with insurance contract* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is further amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2732. Information required to be provided with insurance contract
  >
  > A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage shall include, with any copy of the contract of insurance or benefits that is provided by the plan or issuer to any person, the following:
  >
  > - **(1)** The procedure code for each procedure covered by the plan or coverage.
  > - **(2)** The amount the plan or coverage will charge for each such procedure.
  > - **(3)** A full list of the services and procedures covered by the plan or coverage.

## § 4. Competition

- **(a)** *Requiring equivalent reimbursement rates for in-Network and out-of-Network providers* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is further amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2733. Requiring equivalent reimbursement rates for in-network and out-of-network providers
  >
  > A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage—
  >
  > - **(1)** may not restrict the ability of participating providers to furnish items or services to individuals who are not enrolled in the plan or coverage; and
  > - **(2)** in the case that a nonparticipating provider furnishes items or services for which benefits are provided under the plan or coverage to an individual enrolled in the plan or coverage, shall pay such provider with respect to such items or services in an amount that is not less than the amount that the plan or issuer would have paid if such items or services had been furnished by a participating provider.

- **(b)** *Exemption of catastrophic health plans from Federal regulation*
  - **(1)** *In general* Section 1302(e) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 18022) is amended to read as follows:
    > - **(e)** *Basic plans* A health plan not providing a bronze, silver, gold, or platinum level of coverage shall be treated as meeting the requirements of subsection (d) with respect to any plan year if the plan provides—
    >   - **(1)** coverage for at least 3 primary care visits; and
    >   - **(2)** no other benefits in any plan year until the individual has incurred cost-sharing expenses in an amount equal to the annual limitation in effect under subsection (c)(1) of this section for the plan year, except as provided for in section 2713 of the Public Health Service Act.

  - **(2)** *Conforming amendment* Section 1312(d)(3)(C) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 18032(c)(3)(C)) is amended by striking “, except that in the case of a catastrophic plan described in section 1302(e), a qualified individual may enroll in the plan only if the individual is eligible to enroll in the plan under section 1302(e)(2)”.
  - **(3)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this subsection shall take effect with respect to plans and coverage beginning in calendar year 2027.

## § 5. Patient rights

- **(a)** *Requirement To provide enrollees with point of contact information* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is further amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2734. Requirement to provide enrollees with point of contact information
  >
  > A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage shall provide each individual enrolled in the plan or coverage with the identity of at least 1 and at most 3 individuals who will serve as the point of contact for the individual with the plan or coverage, each of whom shall be based in the United States and proficient in English.

- **(b)** *Requirement To provide monetary compensation for billing errors* Subpart II of part A of title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–11 et seq.) is further amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2735. Requirement to provide monetary compensation for billing errors
  >
  > A group health plan a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage, and a health care provider shall provide monetary compensation to an individual enrolled in the plan or coverage for any error made by the plan, issuer, or provider, respectively, in billing the individual more than the correct amount owed by the individual. Such compensation shall be equal to 110 percent of the excess charge if the amount is returned to the individual within 30 days, 150 percent of such excess if the amount is returned to the individual after 30 days but before 60 days, and 150 percent plus a number of percentage points equal to the product of 25 and the number of 30-day periods occurring after 60 days. The Secretary shall be regulation prescribed penalties in the case of a health care provider that fails to comply with the preceding sentence.

## § 6. Effective date

Except as otherwise provided in this Act, the amendments made by this Act shall take effect on January 1, 2029.
