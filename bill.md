# H.R. 10707

> To address barriers immigrants and refugees face to entering the health care workforce, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; finding

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Internationally Trained Physicians Assistance Act of 2026”.
- **(b)** *Finding* Congress finds that several States have implemented alternative pathways to licensure for internationally trained physicians to address health workforce shortages and improve access to care in underserved communities.
