# H.R. 49

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds for the HHS Reproductive Healthcare Access Task Force.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Pro-Abortion Task Force Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition against use of Federal funds for HHS Reproductive Healthcare Access Task Force

No Federal funds may be used for—

- **(1)** the HHS Reproductive Healthcare Access Task Force, announced by the Secretary of Health and Human Services on January 21, 2022; or
- **(2)** any successor or substantially similar task force.
