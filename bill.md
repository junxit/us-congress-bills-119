# H.R. 9248

> To amend the FAST Act to include certain recreation activities as covered projects subject to Federal permitting improvement, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Recreation Permitting Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Covered projects for Federal permitting improvement

Section 41001(6)(A) of the FAST Act (42 U.S.C. 4370m(6)(A)) is amended by inserting “recreation,” before “or any other sector”.
