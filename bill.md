# H.R. 10983

> To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to exclude certain gambling winnings from monthly income under the community engagement requirement under the Medicaid program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Gambling for Medicaid Act”.

## § 2. Exclusion of certain gambling winnings from monthly income under community engagement requirement for applicable individuals

Section 1902(xx) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396a(xx)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2)(F), by inserting “, excluding covered gambling winnings,” after “monthly income”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2)(G), by inserting “, excluding covered gambling winnings,” after “monthly income”; and
- **(3)** in paragraph (9), by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(E)** *Covered gambling winnings*
  >   - **(i)** *In general* The term “covered gambling winnings” means any prize, award, or income derived from a bet or wager, except a bet or wager that is engaged in as a trade or business activity.
  >   - **(ii)** *Bet or wager* For purposes of clause (i), the term “bet or wager” has the meaning given such term in section 5362 of title 31, United States Code.
  >   - **(iii)** *Trade or business activity* For purposes of clause (i), the term “trade or business activity” means an activity that an individual engages in—
  >     - **(I)** for the primary purpose of income or profit; and
  >     - **(II)** with continuity and regularity.
