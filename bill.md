# H.R. 11109

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to expand IPEDS data collection to better reflect the experiences of community college students and the economic impact of community colleges, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; Findings

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Capturing Outcomes and Understanding Nontraditional Trajectories Act of 2026” or the “COUNT Act of 2026”.
- **(b)** *Findings* The Congress finds the following:
  - **(1)** Community colleges play an important role in expanding access to affordable postsecondary education, supporting transfer to four-year institutions of higher education, and promoting economic mobility directly into the workforce.
  - **(2)** Community colleges serve diverse student populations with a wide range of educational goals, including part-time students, transfer students, students pursuing certificates or other shorter-term programs, and high school students participating in dual or concurrent enrollment and early college high school programs.
  - **(3)** Existing Federal postsecondary data do not fully reflect the breadth of students served, enrollment patterns, and educational pathways supported by community colleges.
  - **(4)** Improvements to Federal data collection to gather more complete data on community college students and their educational pathways are needed to—
    - **(A)** better reflect the value community colleges provide and the important role they play in the Nation’s higher education system; and
    - **(B)** provide Congress and the public with a more accurate understanding of community colleges and the populations they serve.

## § 2. Expansion of IPEDS community college data reporting

- **(a)** *Program participation agreements* Section 487(a)(17) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1094(a)(17)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “(17) The” and inserting “(17)(A) The”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(B)** With respect to a public institution of higher education that, for the most recent academic year for which data is available, awards more associate’s degrees than bachelor’s degrees, the institution will complete any survey collecting data required under subsection (k).

- **(b)** *Data collection* Section 487 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1094) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(k)** *Community college data reporting*
  >   - **(1)** *Community college data* In updating and improving the Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS) in accordance with section 132(i)(4), the Commissioner for Education Statistics shall, in addition to other data being collected, collect from each institution described in section 487(a)(17)(B) the number of students of the institution who, for the most recent academic year for which data is available, meet the following descriptions:
  >     - **(A)** *Completion numbers*
  >       - **(i)** First-time, full-time students who completed a program of study that awards an associate’s degree within four years.
  >       - **(ii)** First-time, part-time students who completed a program of study that awards an associate’s degree within six years.
  >       - **(iii)** Full-time students who completed a program of study that awards an associate’s degree within four years.
  >       - **(iv)** Part-time students who completed a program of study that awards an associate’s degree within six years.
  >       - **(v)** Students awarded a certificate for a completed program of study (including noncredit programs).
  >     - **(B)** *Enrollment numbers*
  >       - **(i)** Students enrolled part-time.
  >       - **(ii)** Students enrolled in an apprenticeship program.
  >       - **(iii)** Students enrolled in a continuing education program.
  >       - **(iv)** Students enrolled in a non-credit program.
  >       - **(v)** Students enrolled in a dual or concurrent enrollment program, as defined in section 8101 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7801).
  >       - **(vi)** Students enrolled in an early college high school program, as defined in section 8101 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7801).
  >       - **(vii)** Students who have completed more than 24 credit hours and have not completed a degree or certificate.
  >       - **(viii)** Students who indicated to the institution at the time of enrollment the intent to transfer to a 4-year institution.
  >   - **(2)** *Methodology* With respect to determining the number of students who completed a program of study for the purposes of clauses (iii) through (v) of paragraph (1)(A), the Commissioner for Education Statistics shall ensure that—
  >     - **(A)** the data collected includes all applicable students who enroll at an institution during an academic year, regardless of whether the student’s enrollment began in the fall term or another term during such academic year; and
  >     - **(B)** for the purpose of calculating the number of years it takes for a student to complete a program of study, the data collected uses the start of the specific term of the academic year in which the student first enrolled at the institution as the start of each year needed to complete a program of study.

- **(c)** *Applicability* The amendments made by subsections (a) and (b) shall apply to any applicable Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS) report due on or after the date that is one year after the date of enactment of this Act.
