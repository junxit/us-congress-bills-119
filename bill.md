# H.R. 9180

> To require the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to access unmet community development-related needs in areas impacted by disasters on an ongoing basis, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “CDBG–DR Automated Assessment Act”.

## § 2. Unmet needs assessment required

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development (in this section referred to as the “Secretary”) shall not later than 60 days after the President declares a major disaster under the “Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act” and quarterly thereafter—

- **(1)** use Federal disaster and housing data available to the Secretary to—
  - **(A)** evaluate damage to housing and infrastructure in the area impacted by the disaster; and
  - **(B)** identify shortages in affordable housing and broader economic recovery needs; and
- **(2)** submit to the Congress a report about the analysis conducted under paragraph (1) that identifies any unmet needs in the area impacted by the disaster that could be addressed using amounts appropriated for Community Development Block Grant Disaster Recovery and other related Federal disaster programs.
