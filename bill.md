# H.R. 10757

> To provide for a limitation on contributions to the United Nations.

## § 1. Limitation on contributions to the United Nations

None of the funds authorized to be appropriated or otherwise made available may be used for contributions to the United Nations until the President certifies to Congress that the United Nations Security Council has designated Hamas and Lebanese Hezbollah as terrorist organizations.
