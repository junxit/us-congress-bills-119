# H.R. 10717

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide wigs to TRICARE beneficiaries with alopecia, including alopecia caused by chemotherapy, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Wigs for Warriors Act”.

## § 2. Amendment to provide wigs to TRICARE beneficiaries with covered conditions

Section 1074c of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “resulting from the treatment of a malignant disease may” and inserting “caused by a health condition other than androgenic alopecia, or the treatment of a health condition, shall”; and
- **(2)** by striking “if the person has not previously been furnished one”.
