# H.R. 9575

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to decrease the sum of damage or attempted damage to property of the United States, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hold Vandals Accountable Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Government property or contracts

Section 1361 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking “$1,000” and inserting “$500” each place such term appears.
