# S.J. RES. 203

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Preemption Determination: State Interest-on-Escrow Laws”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency relating to “Preemption Determination: State Interest-on-Escrow Laws” (91 Fed. Reg. 29350 (May 19, 2026)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
