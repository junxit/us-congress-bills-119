# H.R. 2459

> To amend the Trade Act of 1974 to eliminate the authority to impose certain import surcharges to address balance of payments deficits, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reclaim Trade Powers Act”.

## § 2. Elimination of balance of payments authority

Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2132) is repealed.
