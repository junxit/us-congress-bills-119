# H.R. 6087

> To amend the Delta Development Act to add the Kentucky counties of Hancock, Ohio, and Daviess to the Delta Regional Authority area, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Delta Regional Authority area

Section 4(2)(C) of the Delta Development Act (42 U.S.C. 3121 note; Public Law 100–460) is amended by striking “and Union” and inserting “Union, Daviess, Hancock, and Ohio”.
