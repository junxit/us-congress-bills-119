# H.R. 1206

> To require the Director of the Bureau of Land Management to withdraw a rule of the Bureau of Land Management relating to conservation and landscape health.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Western Economic Security Today Act of 2025” or the “WEST Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Withdrawal of BLM rule

The final rule based on the proposed rule of the Bureau of Land Management entitled “Conservation and Landscape Health” (88 Fed. Reg. 19583 (April 3, 2023)) shall have no force or effect.
