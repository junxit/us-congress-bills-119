# H.R. 10652

> To reauthorize the San Gabriel Basin Restoration Fund.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “San Gabriel Basin Restoration Fund Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. San Gabriel Basin Restoration Fund

Section 110 of division B of the Miscellaneous Appropriations Act, 2001 (114 Stat. 2763A–222), as enacted into law by section 1(a)(4) of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2001 (Public Law 106–554), is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(3)(A)(ii), by striking “for a period not to exceed 10 years, following the initial date of operation of the project”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (d)(1), by striking “$146,200,000” and inserting “$250,000,000”.
