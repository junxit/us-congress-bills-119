# H.R. 10996

> To reauthorize the Rural Business-Cooperative Service Programs and Technical Assistance and Training Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Streamlining Government Programs for Rural Businesses Act”.

## § 2. Rural Business-Cooperative Service programs technical assistance and training

Section 368(d)(1) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 2008c(d)(1)) is amended by striking “2019 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
