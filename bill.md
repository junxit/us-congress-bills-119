# H.R. 8945

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Wai'anae, Hawai'i, as the “U.S. Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.

## § 1. U.S. Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Wai’anae, Hawai’i, shall be known and designated as the “U.S. Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “U.S. Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.
