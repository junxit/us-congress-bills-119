# H.R. 10445

> To amend chapter 4 of title 5, United States Code, to provide 10 year terms of service for Inspectors General, to provide that Inspectors General may only be removed for cause, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Inspectors General Modernization Act”.

## § 2. 10 year term of service for Inspectors General; removal only for cause

Title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in section 403—
  - **(A)** in subsection (a), by inserting after the first sentence the following: “The term of service of the Inspector General shall be ten years.”; and
  - **(B)** in subsection (b), by striking “may be removed from office by the President” and inserting “may be removed from office by the President only for cause”; and
- **(2)** in section 415—
  - **(A)** in subsection (c), by inserting after the second sentence the following: “The term of service of the Inspector General shall be ten years.”; and
  - **(B)** in subsection (e), by inserting “only for cause and” after “made”.
