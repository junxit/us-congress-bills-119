# H.J. RES. 195

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Restoring Flexibility in the Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF)”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Health and Human Services relating to “Restoring Flexibility in the Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF)”, published May 12, 2026 (91 Fed. Reg. 25796), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
