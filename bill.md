# H.R. 7663

> To amend section 6103 of title 5, United States Code, to change the date designated for Washington’s Birthday and designate Lincoln’s Birthday as a legal public holiday.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Washington's Birthday Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Changes to legal public holidays

Section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting after the item relating to the Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr., the following:
  > -  Lincoln’s Birthday.

- **(2)** in the item relating to Washington’s Birthday, by striking “the third Monday in February” and inserting “February 22”.
