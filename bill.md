# S. 5123

> To amend title 36, United States Code, to designate July 20 as Lunar Landing Day.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lunar Landing Day Act”.

## § 2. Designation

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 1 of title 36, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by redesignating sections 106 through 115 as sections 107 through 116, respectively;
  - **(2)** by moving section 147 so as to appear after section 105 and by redesignating such section 147 as section 106;
  - **(3)** by redesignating the second section designated as section 106 and sections 107 through 116 as sections 107 through 116, respectively;
  - **(4)** by redesignating the second section designated as 116 and section 117 as sections 118 and 119, respectively;
  - **(5)** by moving section 146 so as to appear after section 119, as so redesignated, and by redesignating such section 146 as section 120;
  - **(6)** by redesignating the second section designated as section 118, the second section designated as section 119, and sections 120 through 135 as sections 121 through 138, respectively;
  - **(7)** by moving section 144 so as to appear after section 138, as so redesignated, and by redesignating such section 144 as section 139;
  - **(8)** by redesignating the second section designated as section 136, the second section designated as section 137, the second section designated as section 138, the second section designated as section 139, and sections 140 and 141 as sections 140 through 145, respectively;
  - **(9)** by moving section 148 so as to appear after section 145, as so redesignated, and by redesignating such section 148 as section 146;
  - **(10)** by moving the second section designated as section 145 so as to appear after section 146, as so redesignated, and by redesignating such section 145 as section 147;
  - **(11)** by redesignating the second section designated as section 142 and the second section designated as section 143 as sections 148 and 149, respectively; and
  - **(12)** by inserting after section 116, as so redesignated, the following:
    > ## § 117. Lunar Landing Day
    >
    > - **(a)** *Designation* July 20 is Lunar Landing Day.
    > - **(b)** *Recognition* All private citizens, organizations, and Federal, State, and local governmental and legislative entities are encouraged to recognize Lunar Landing Day through proclamations, activities, and educational efforts—
    >   - **(1)** commemorating the first successful landing of humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969; and
    >   - **(2)** inspiring the continued leadership of the United States in space exploration and science.
