# H.R. 11128

> To require people who manufacture for sale, sell, introduce or deliver for introduction in interstate commerce, or import into the United States clothing or shoes to disclose any hazardous chemical in such clothing and shoes, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting American Shirts and Shoes Act of 2026” or the “PASS Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Required disclosure of hazardous chemicals in clothing and shoes

- **(a)** *Requirement* A person who manufactures for sale, sells, introduces or delivers for introduction in interstate commerce, or imports into the United States clothing or shoes shall disclose, through a label on such clothing or shoes, any hazardous chemical in such clothing or shoes.
- **(b)** *Enforcement*
  - **(1)** *Unfair or deceptive acts or practices* A violation of subsection (a) shall be treated as a violation of a regulation under section 18(a)(1)(B) of the Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 57a(a)(1)(B)) regarding unfair or deceptive acts or practices.
  - **(2)** *Powers of Commission* The Commission shall enforce subsection (a) in the same manner, by the same means, and with the same jurisdiction, powers, and duties as though all applicable terms and provisions of the Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 41 et seq.) were incorporated into and made a part of this section, and any person who violates such subsection shall be subject to the penalties and entitled to the privileges and immunities provided in the Federal Trade Commission Act.
- **(c)** *Effective date* This section shall take effect on the date that is 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(d)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Commission* The term Commission means the Federal Trade Commission.
  - **(2)** *Hazardous chemical* The term hazardous chemical has the same meaning given the term health hazard in section 1910.1200(c) of title 29, Code of Federal Regulations.
