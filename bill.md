# H.R. 10782

> To reauthorize the Clean School Bus Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean School Bus Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 741(f) of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 16091(f)) is amended by striking “2022 through 2026” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
