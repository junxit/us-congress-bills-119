# H.R. 2812

> To make grants to the Girl Scouts of the United States of America, the Boy Scouts of America, the National 4H Council, and the National FFA Organization to establish pilot projects to expand the programs carried out by the organizations in rural areas and small towns, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Youth Lead Act”.

## § 2. Grants for youth organizations

Section 410(d) of the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 (7 U.S.C. 7630(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and” at the end of paragraph (1);
- **(2)** by striking the period at the end of paragraph (2) and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** $5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 through 2030.
