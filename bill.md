# H.R. 10083

> To reauthorize appropriations for the advanced drinking water technology grant program under the Safe Drinking Water Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Water Technology and Resilience Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Advanced Drinking Water Technologies

Section 1459G(b)(5)(A) of the Safe Drinking Water Act is amended by striking “2022 through 2026” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
