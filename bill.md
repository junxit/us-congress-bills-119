# S. 2310

> To repeal the Federal charter for the National Education Association.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Education Association Charter Repeal Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of Federal charter to the National Education Association

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 1511 of title 36, United States Code (which granted a Federal charter to the National Education Association), is repealed.
- **(b)** *Clerical amendment* The table of chapters for title 36, United States Code, is amended by striking the items relating to chapter 1511.
