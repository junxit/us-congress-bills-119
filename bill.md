# S. 5638

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to modernize the wage required for exempt H–1B workers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting American Workers Through H–1B Modernization Act.”

## § 2. Exempt H–1B nonimmigrant wage modernization

Section 212(n)(3)(B)(i)(I) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1182(n)(3)(B)(i)(I)) is amended by striking “at least $60,000” and inserting “not less than twice the national average wage index (as defined in section 209(k)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 409(k)(1))) for the most recently calculated year.”.
