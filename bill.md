# H.R. 6438

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to ensure high-income individuals pay their fair share of taxes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Redistribution of Billions by Instituting New High-Income Obligations on Overlooked Debt Act” or the “ROBINHOOD Act”.
