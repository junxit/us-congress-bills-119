# H.R. 10226

> To prohibit the use of shock gloves by Federal law enforcement officers to enforce the immigration laws, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Shock Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Prohibition on the use of shock gloves

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Homeland Security shall prohibit the use of shock gloves by any Federal law enforcement officer enforcing the immigration laws.
- **(b)** *State and local law enforcement* The requirement described under subsection (a) shall apply to any law enforcement officer enforcing the immigration laws pursuant to section 287(g) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1357(g)).
- **(c)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Immigration laws* The term immigration laws has the meaning given such term under section 101(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)).
  - **(2)** *Shock glove* The term shock glove means a glove that may deliver an electric shock when applied directly to the body of an individual, including the CTG–5 G.L.O.V.E (Generated Low Output Voltage Emitter) conductive distraction and de-escalation device.
