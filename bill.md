# H.R. 10487

> To amend title IV of the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act to require project sponsors to offer substance use disorder treatment to persons admitted to emergency shelter, transitional housing, or permanent housing, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Recovery First Housing Act”.

## § 2. Drug and alcohol treatment requirement

Title IV, subtitle A, of the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act is amending by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 409. Drug and alcohol treatment requirement
>
> - **(a)** *In general* Any project sponsor receiving funds under this title to provide emergency shelter, transitional housing, or permanent housing shall offer substance use disorder treatment, including step-down treatment, to each person with a substance use disorder as determined by the project sponsor that such project sponsor admits to such emergency shelter, transitional housing, or permanent housing.
> - **(b)** *Exception* Subsection (a) shall not apply to a project sponsor who admits a person who is an unaccompanied minor.
> - **(c)** *Definitions*
>   - **(1)** *Substance use disorder treatment* The term “substance use disorder treatment” means an evidence-based substance use disorder intervention that—
>     - **(A)** is delivered by a licensed provider; and
>     - **(B)** provides an appropriate level of care consisting of medically supervised withdrawal management, residential treatment, intensive outpatient treatment, outpatient treatment, medication-assisted treatment where clinically indicated, recovery housing with clinical support, or step-down treatment.
>   - **(2)** *Step-down treatment* The term “step-down treatment” means a structured, phased approach designed to transition unhoused individuals or individuals requiring medical intervention from intensive care to less intensive, community-based, outpatient services to promote recovery, stability, and self-sufficiency.
