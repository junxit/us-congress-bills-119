# H.R. 8974

> To authorize the Development Finance Corporation to invest in Venezuela.

## § 1. Authorization to invest in Venezuela

- **(a)** *Removal as country of concern* Section 1402(3) of the BUILD Act of 2018 (22 U.S.C. 9601(3)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking subparagraph (A); and
  - **(2)** by redesignating subparagraphs (B) through (G) as subparagraphs (A) through (F), respectively.
- **(b)** *Investment authority* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Development Finance Corporation may invest in the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela.
