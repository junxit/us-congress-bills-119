# H. RES. 1670

> Recognizing the importance of wheelchair basketball and other adaptive sports in enhancing the quality of life for people with disabilities in communities across the country through recreation programming, leisure activities, and community involvement.

## Preamble

- Whereas wheelchair basketball was first played by members of Paralyzed Veterans of America at the former Birmingham General Veteran’s Administration Hospital on November 25, 1946;
- Whereas wheelchair basketball was featured in the inaugural Paralympics Games in Rome in 1960 and remains one of the most popular competitive events in the Paralympic games;
- Whereas people with disabilities continuously face societal barriers and discrimination that generally exclude them from education, employment, and community life, depriving them of opportunities essential to their social development, health, and well-being.
- Whereas adaptive sports play a vital role in improving the physical health, skill development, and psychological well-being of children with disabilities, while also increasing healthy lifestyles, social interactions, inclusion, and acceptance in the community;
- Whereas participation in adaptive sports shows a positive influence on the mental and physical quality of life for adults and older adults with disabilities, and can be considered a public health intervention; and
- Whereas November is a fitting time to celebrate the history and legacy of the paralyzed World War II veterans who launched wheelchair basketball into popularity: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** recognizes the importance of wheelchair basketball and supports the designation of a “National Wheelchair Basketball Day”;
- **(2)** affirms the future of adaptive sports is strengthened by the inclusion, vision, and agency of people with disabilities;
- **(3)** encourages the people of the United States to celebrate the advancement of inclusion and accessibility made possible by wheelchair basketball;
- **(4)** pledges to continue to work on a bipartisan basis to identify and address barriers that undermine the national goals of equality of opportunity, independent living, economic self-sufficiency, and full participation for individuals with disabilities; and
- **(5)** pledges to work with States to improve access to recreation opportunities for individuals with disabilities.
