# S. 193

> To repeal the Alien Enemies Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Neighbors Not Enemies Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of Alien Enemies Act

Sections 4067 through 4070 of the Revised Statutes of the United States (50 U.S.C. 21–24) are repealed.
