# H.R. 6926

> To prohibit the naming, renaming, designating, or redesignating of any Federal building, land, or other asset in the name of a sitting President, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Property Integrity Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on building naming after sitting President

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal building, land, or other asset may be named, renamed, designated, or redesignated after a sitting President.
