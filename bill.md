# H.R. 11059

> To amend the John D. Dingell, Jr. Conservation, Management, and Recreation Act to update the local coordinating entity for the Southern Maryland National Heritage Area.

## § 1. Local coordinating entity for the Southern Maryland National Heritage Area

Section 6001(a)(13)(B) of the John D. Dingell, Jr. Conservation, Management, and Recreation Act (Public Law 116–9; 133 Stat. 768; 136 Stat. 6165) is amended by striking “Tri-County Council for Southern Maryland” and inserting “Southern Maryland National Heritage Area, Inc.,”.
