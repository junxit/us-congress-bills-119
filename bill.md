# S. 5629

> To require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to permit veterans to seek a second opinion after the receipt of health care under the laws administered by the Secretary, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Gene Murphy Veterans’ Bill of Rights Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Right of veterans to second opinion in receipt of care furnished by Department of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall ensure that any veteran who receives health care under the laws administered by the Secretary may request a second opinion after the receipt of such care and the Secretary, upon such request, shall provide to the veteran—
  - **(1)** a referral to another health care provider of the Department of Veterans Affairs; or
  - **(2)** a referral to a non-Department provider in the community, including under the Veterans Community Care Program under section 1703 of title 38, United States Code.
- **(b)** *Costs* All costs associated with providing a second opinion under subsection (a) shall be borne by the Department.
