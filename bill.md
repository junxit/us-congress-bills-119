# S. 5548

> To prohibit Federal procurement of certain optical transceivers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Prohibitions on the procurement of certain optical transceivers

Section 5949 of the James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023 (Public Law 117–263; 41 U.S.C. 4713 note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “semiconductor products or services” each place it appears and inserting “semiconductor products or services or covered optical transceivers”; and
  - **(B)** in paragraph (2)(A)—
    - **(i)** in clause (iii)—
      - **(I)** by striking “the effective date” and inserting “the applicable effective date”; and
      - **(II)** by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon;
    - **(ii)** in clause (iv), by striking the period and insert “; or”; and
    - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following new clause:
      > - **(v)** to prohibit or limit the authority of the head of an executive agency to procure or obtain any parts, products, or services that include a covered optical transceiver for a system that is not a critical system.

- **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “on a date later than the effective date described in subsection (c)” each place it appears and inserting “of the prohibitions under subsection (a)”;
- **(3)** in subsection (c)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by striking “The prohibitions under subsection (a)” and inserting the following:
      > - **(A)** *Covered semiconductor products and services* The prohibitions under subsection (a), as they apply to covered semiconductor products and services,

    - **(ii)** by adding to the end the following new subparagraph:
      > - **(B)** *Covered optical transceivers* The prohibitions under subsection (a), as they apply to covered optical transceivers, shall take effect five years after the date of the enactment of the Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026.

  - **(B)** in paragraph (2)—
    - **(i)** by striking “Not later than” and inserting the following:
      > - **(A)** *In general* Not later than

    - **(ii)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
      > - **(B)** *Updates to regulations to account for covered optical transceivers* Not later than three years after the date of the enactment of the Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026, the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council shall—
      >   - **(i)** update regulations prescribed under subparagraph (A) to apply such regulations to covered optical transceivers;
      >   - **(ii)** in such regulations, apply the limitations and restrictions described in subsection (h) to covered optical transceivers to the same extent that such limitations and restrictions apply to covered semiconductor products and services, except—
      >     - **(I)** paragraph (1)(B) of subsection (h) shall not apply to covered optical transceivers;
      >     - **(II)** references in subsection (h) to covered entities shall include, with respect to covered optical transceivers, any entity that supplies a covered optical transceiver; and
      >     - **(III)** the disclosure requirements described in paragraphs (2) and (3) of subsection (h) shall only apply to an entity that supplies covered optical transceivers if the entity knows, or has reason to know, a part, product, or service includes a covered optical transceiver; and
      >   - **(iii)** in such regulations, include provisions to ensure that the limitations and restrictions applicable to covered optical transceivers do not require, and are not satisfied only by, the use of proprietary vendor-authentication features that would prevent the procurement or use of an optical transceiver that is not a covered optical transceiver solely on the basis that it is procured or supplied by an entity other than the manufacturer of the host equipment.

- **(4)** in subsection (d)—
  - **(A)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) and (2) as subparagraphs (A) and (B), respectively;
  - **(B)** by moving such subparagraphs, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(C)** by striking “Not later than 270 days after the effective date described in subsection (c)(1)” and inserting the following:
    > - **(1)** *In general* Not later than 270 days after the effective date described in subsection (c)(1)(A)

  - **(D)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(2)** *Update to report and briefing* Not later than 270 days after the date described in subsection (c)(1)(B), the Director of the Office of Management and Budget, in coordination with the Director of National Intelligence and the National Cyber Director, shall update the report and briefing provided under paragraph (1) to address—
    >   - **(A)** the implementation of the prohibitions under subsection (a) as they apply to covered optical transceivers, including any challenges in such implementation; and
    >   - **(B)** the effectiveness and utility of the waiver authority under subsection (b) as it applies to covered optical transceivers.

- **(5)** in subsection (e)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) and (B) as clauses (i) and (ii), respectively; and
    - **(ii)** by moving such clauses, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (4)—
    - **(i)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) through (C) as clauses (i) through (iii), respectively; and
    - **(ii)** by moving such clauses, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(C)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (5) as subparagraphs (A) through (E), respectively;
  - **(D)** by moving such subparagraphs, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(E)** by striking “Not later than 180 days” and inserting the following:
    > - **(1)** *Semiconductor products and services* Not later than 180 days

  - **(F)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(2)** *Optical transceivers* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of the Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026, the Secretary of Commerce, in coordination with the Secretary of Defense, the Secretary of Homeland Security, the Director of National Intelligence, and the Secretary of Energy and, to the greatest extent practicable, leveraging relevant previous analyses and assessments, shall—
    >   - **(A)** conduct an analysis of optical transceiver design and production capacity domestically and by allied or partner countries required to meet the needs of the Federal Government;
    >   - **(B)** assess the risk posed by the presence of covered optical transceivers in Federal systems;
    >   - **(C)** assess the risk posed by the presence of covered optical transceivers in the supply chains of Federal contractors and subcontractors, including for non-Federal systems;
    >   - **(D)** develop a strategy—
    >     - **(i)** to improve the availability of domestic optical transceiver design and production capacity required to meet the requirements of the Federal Government;
    >     - **(ii)** to support optical transceiver suppliers seeking to contract with domestic, allied, or partner optical transceiver producers and to improve supply chain traceability, including to meet the prohibitions under subsection (a); and
    >     - **(iii)** to either certify the feasibility of implementing such prohibitions or exercising waiver authorities under subsection (b), to ensure uninterrupted Federal Government access to required optical transceivers; and
    >   - **(E)** provide the results of the analysis, assessment, and strategy developed under subparagraphs (A) through (D) to the Federal Acquisition Security Council.

- **(6)** in subsection (g)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) through (E) as clauses (i) through (v), respectively; and
    - **(ii)** by moving such clauses, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) and (2) as subparagraphs (A) and (B), respectively;
  - **(C)** by moving such subparagraphs, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
  - **(D)** by striking “Not later than two years” and inserting the following:
    > - **(1)** *Semiconductor products and services* Not later than two years

  - **(E)** in paragraph (1), as added by subparagraph (D), by striking “subsection (e)” and inserting “subsection (e)(1)”; and
  - **(F)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(2)** *Optical transceivers* Not later than two years after the date of the enactment of the Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026, the Federal Acquisition Security Council, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce, the Secretary of Defense, the Secretary of Homeland Security, the Director of National Intelligence, and the Secretary of Energy, and after engagement with the private sector and other nongovernmental stakeholders in accordance with section 1323 of title 41, United States Code, shall—
    >   - **(A)** issue recommendations to mitigate supply chain risks relevant to Federal Government acquisition of optical transceivers, considering—
    >     - **(i)** the analysis, assessment, and strategy developed under subsection (e)(2) and any related updates;
    >     - **(ii)** the standards provided under section 224 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 (Public Law 116–92), including any tiers of trust, levels of security, or risk-based approaches established under such section;
    >     - **(iii)** the extent to which such recommendations would enhance the security of critical systems;
    >     - **(iv)** the extent to which such recommendations would impact Federal access to commercial technologies; and
    >     - **(v)** any risks to the Federal Government from contracting with microelectronics suppliers that include covered optical transceivers in non-Federal supply chains; and
    >   - **(B)** make recommendations to the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council and the heads of executive agencies for any needed regulations to mitigate supply chain risks.

- **(7)** in subsection (i)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) and (B) as clauses (i) and (ii), respectively;
    - **(ii)** by moving such clauses, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;

      > - **(A)** *Semiconductor products and services* Not later than 60 days after completing the assessment required under subsection (e)(1)

    - **(iv)** by adding to the end the following new subparagraph:
      > - **(B)** *Optical transceivers* Not later than 60 days after completing the assessment required under subsection (e)(2), the Secretary of Commerce shall submit to the appropriate committees of Congress and leadership a report of the findings and recommendations of the analyses, assessment, and strategy developed under such subsection.

  - **(B)** in paragraph (2)—
    - **(i)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) and (B) as clauses (i) and (ii), respectively;
    - **(ii)** by moving such clauses, as so redesignated, 2 ems to the right;
    - **(iii)** by striking “Not later than one” and inserting the following:
      > - **(A)** *In general* Not later than one

    - **(iv)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
      > - **(B)** *Optical transceivers* Not later than one year after the date of the enactment of the Securing National Security Systems from Chinese Optical Transceivers Act of 2026, and annually thereafter for ten years, the Federal Acquisition Security Council shall include in the annual report submitted under section 1325 of title 41, United States Code, a description of—
      >   - **(i)** the development of recommendations under subsection (g)(2); and
      >   - **(ii)** as applicable, the impact of any recommendations or regulations implemented.

- **(8)** in subsection (j)—
  - **(A)** by redesignating paragraphs (3) through (5) as paragraphs (4) through (6), respectively;
  - **(B)** by inserting after paragraph (2) the following new paragraph:
    > - **(3)** *Covered optical transceivers* The term covered optical transceiver means an optical transceiver that—
    >   - **(A)** is designed, developed, manufactured, or supplied by—
    >     - **(i)** Zhongji Innolight Co., Ltd. (InnoLight);
    >     - **(ii)** Eoptolink Technology Inc., Ltd. (Eoptolink);
    >     - **(iii)** any subsidiary, affiliate, or successor of an entity described in clause (i) or (ii); or
    >     - **(iv)** an entity that the Secretary of Defense or the Secretary of Commerce, in consultation with the Director of National Intelligence or the Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, determines to be owned or controlled by, or otherwise connected to, the government of a foreign country of concern, provided that the determination with respect to such entity is published in the Federal Register; or
    >   - **(B)** incorporates firmware, software, or component parts designed, developed, manufactured, or supplied by any entity described in subparagraph (A).

  - **(C)** in paragraph (2)(B), by striking “paragraph (3)” and inserting “paragraph (4)”; and
  - **(D)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(7)** *Optical transceiver* The term optical transceiver means any optical pluggable, optical transceiver, or integrated optical module, including linear pluggable optics, or any product or service that incorporates, uses, or is produced using any such item.
