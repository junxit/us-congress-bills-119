# H.R. 6136

> To amend the Labor-Management Reporting and Disclosure Act of 1959 to require secret ballot elections of members for certain union officers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Union Representation and Elections Act” or the “PURE Act”.

## § 2. Secret ballot for certain elections

- **(a)** *In general* Section 401 of the Labor-Management Reporting and Disclosure Act of 1959 (29 U.S.C. 481) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a)—
    - **(A)** by striking “either”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “or at a convention of delegates chosen by secret ballot”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (d), by striking “or by labor organization officers representative of such members who have been elected by secret ballot”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this Act shall take effect on the date that is 18 months after the date of enactment of this Act.
