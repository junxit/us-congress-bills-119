# H.R. 10211

> To codify Executive Order 14418 (relating to continuing to protect the meaning and value of American citizenship).

## § 1. Continuing to protect the meaning and value of american citizenship

Executive Order 14418 (91 Fed. Reg. 51991; Continuing To Protect the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship) shall have the full force and effect of law.
