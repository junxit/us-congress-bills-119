# H.R. 5192

> To modify the annual report on the Navy Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program.

## § 1. Modification to annual report on Navy Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program

Section 355(c)(2)(A) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2022 (Public Law 117–81; 10 U.S.C. 8013 note) is amended by inserting “and the incorporation of digital infrastructure (including hardware, software, and cloud storage) and platforms” before “; and”.
