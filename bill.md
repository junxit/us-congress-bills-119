# H.R. 7440

> To direct the Secretary of Transportation to convey all right, title, and interest to certain property to the San Mateo County Transit District.

## § 1. Conveyance of certain property

On the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Transportation shall convey, without consideration, all right, title, and interest of the Federal Government in and to the Colma Park and Ride Lot (Federal Aid Project No. IR–280–1 (876)) to the San Mateo County Transit District.
