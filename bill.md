# H.R. 9928

> To prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security from waiving or reducing any fee associated with an application for United States citizenship.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Discounts for Foreigners Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on certain fee waivers and reductions

- **(a)** *Prohibition* Except as provided for under subsection (b), the Secretary of Homeland Security may not waive or reduce any fee associated with an application for United States citizenship.
- **(b)** *Exception* The prohibition under subsection (a) shall not apply to an application submitted pursuant to section 328 or section 329 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1439; 1440) by a member of the Armed Forces or an alien who previously served as a member of the Armed Forces.
- **(c)** *Armed Forces defined* In this section, the term “Armed Forces” shall have the meaning given such term in section 101 of title 10, United States Code.
