# H.R. 7447

> To provide grants for technical assistance for State crisis intervention programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Risk Training and Response Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Grants

The Attorney General shall make grants to States for a term of 1 year in an amount of not less than $200,000 and not more than $500,000 for purposes of providing technical assistance to State and local—

- **(1)** law enforcement;
- **(2)** prosecutors;
- **(3)** judges and court staff;
- **(4)** healthcare providers;
- **(5)** educators; and
- **(6)** agencies designated to coordinate extreme risk protection order implementation,
