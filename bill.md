# H.R. 10554

> To add the Department of State and the Department of Homeland Security to the list of permitted disclosures for the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Strengthened Checks for Reliable Evaluation of Entrants Nationwide Act of 2026” or the “SCREEN Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Permitted disclosures by the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children

Section 2258A(g)(3) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraphs (D) and (E) as subparagraphs (E) and (F), respectively; and
- **(2)** by inserting after subparagraph (C) the following new subparagraph (D):
  > - **(D)** the Department of State and the Department of Homeland Security for purposes of screening and vetting;
