# H.R. 10131

> To amend title 44, United States Code, to prohibit the closure of Federal records centers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Archives Protection Act”.

## § 2. Prohibiting the closure of federal records centers

Section 2907 of title 44, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “The” and inserting
  > - **(a)** *In general* The

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(b)** *Prohibition* The Archivist may not—
  >   - **(1)** close or impose unreasonable restrictions on public access to any record center or archive facility established before the date of the enactment of this subsection; or
  >   - **(2)** reconstruct, convert, or rehabilitate any such center or facility if doing so would require the relocation of records for more than 180 days.
