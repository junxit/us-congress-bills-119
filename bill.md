# H.R. 8905

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to prohibit leaders of certain religious denominations from being admitted to the United States under section 101(a)(15)(R) of that Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Inhibiting Militant Adversarial Mullahs Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on R visas for leaders of certain religious denominations

Section 214 of the Immigration and Nationality Act is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(s)** *Limitation on R visas for leaders of certain religious denominations* An alien may not be admitted under section 101(a)(15)(R) if the alien has any of the following titles:
>   - **(1)** Imam or grand imam.
>   - **(2)** Shaykha.
>   - **(3)** Mufti or grand mufti.
>   - **(4)** Ayatollah or grand ayatollah.
