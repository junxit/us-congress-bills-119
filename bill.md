# H.R. 10428

> To modernize the application process for survivors insurance benefits under section 202 of the Social Security Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Survivor Benefits Application Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Modernization of application process for survivors insurance benefits

- **(a)** *Self-Schedule option* Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Commissioner of Social Security (in this Act referred to as “Commissioner”) shall establish an online portal for individuals filing an application for benefits under subsections (d) through (h) of section 202 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 402) to schedule appointments related to such applications.
- **(b)** *Online application* Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, the Commissioner shall develop a method for individuals to file an application for the benefits described in subsection (a) through the online portal described in such subsection.

## § 3. Report

Not later than 3 years after the date of enactment of this Act, the Commissioner shall submit to Congress a report on the development and implementation of the online portal described in section 2, including—

- **(1)** any increase or decrease in the number of applications for benefits under subsections (d) through (h) of section 202 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 402) received and processed by the Social Security Administration since the date of implementation of such online portal;
- **(2)** the costs or savings attributed to implementation of such online portal; and
- **(3)** any increase or decrease in the number of fraudulent applications for such benefits since the date of implementation of such online portal.

## § 4. Authorization of appropriations

There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this Act.
