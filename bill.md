# H.R. 2132

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to exempt Northern Mariana Islands from certain eligibility requirements for essential air service program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Marianas Air Service Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Exception for locations in Northern Mariana Islands

Section 41731(c) of title 49, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(c)** *Exception for locations in Alaska, Hawaii, and Northern Mariana Islands* Subparagraphs (B), (C), and (D) of subsection (a)(1) shall not apply with respect to locations in the State of Alaska, the State of Hawaii, or the Northern Mariana Islands.
