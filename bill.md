# S. RES. 921

> Expressing the sense of the Senate that the advertisements promoting President Donald Trump that aired in September 2026, which included the direct language “paid for by the U.S. Government,” should not be paid for by the United States Government.

That it is the sense of the Senate that the advertisements promoting President Donald Trump that aired in September 2026, which included the direct language “paid for by the U.S. Government,” should not be paid for by the United States Government.
