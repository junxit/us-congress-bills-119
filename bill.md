# H.R. 6459

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 35 South Main Street in Glendale, Utah, as the “Deputy Sheriff Brian Harris Post Office”.

## § 1. Deputy Sheriff Brian Harris Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 35 South Main Street in Glendale, Utah, shall be known and designated as the “Deputy Sheriff Brian Harris Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Deputy Sheriff Brian Harris Post Office”.
