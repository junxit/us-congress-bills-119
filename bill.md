# S. 3307

> To amend title 5, United States Code, relative to the powers of the Inspector General of the Department of Justice.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Inspector General Access Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Investigations of Department of Justice personnel

Section 413 of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (2), by striking “and paragraph (3)”;
  - **(B)** by striking paragraph (3);
  - **(C)** by redesignating paragraphs (4) and (5) as paragraphs (3) and (4), respectively; and
  - **(D)** in paragraph (4), as so redesignated, by striking “paragraph (4)” and inserting “paragraph (3)”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (d), by striking “, except with respect to allegations described in subsection (b)(3),”.
