# H.J. RES. 15

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Commercial Water Heating Equipment”.

That Congress disapproves the final rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to “Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Commercial Water Heating Equipment” (88 Fed. Reg. 69686 (October 6, 2023)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
