# H.R. 5638

> To amend the Geothermal Steam Act of 1970 to require royalties on production from leased geothermal resources to be based on production from each electric generating facility, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Geothermal Royalty Reform Act”.

## § 2. Geothermal royalties

Section 5(a)(1) of the Geothermal Steam Act of 1970 (30 U.S.C. 1004(a)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “with respect to each electric generating facility producing electricity from such resources,” before “not less than”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “by such facility” after “produced”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “with respect to each electric generating facility producing electricity from such resources,” before “not less than”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “by such facility” after “produced”.
