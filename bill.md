# H.R. 5037

> To expand middle mile infrastructure into rural areas.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Middle Mile for Rural America Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of middle mile infrastructure into rural areas

Section 602(g) of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 950bb–1(g)) is amended by striking “2018 through 2023” and inserting “2026 through 2031”.
