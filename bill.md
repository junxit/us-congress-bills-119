# H.R. 9391

> To authorize an extension and expansion of the reimbursable screening services program of the Transportation Security Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reimbursable Screening Services Program Extension Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Extension and expansion of reimbursable screening services program

- **(a)** *Extension* Subsection (e) of section 225 of division A of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2019 (Public Law 116–6; 49 U.S.C. 44901 note), is amended by striking “through fiscal year 2026” and inserting “through fiscal year 2031”.
- **(b)** *Expansion* Subsection (e) of section 225 of division A of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2019 is further amended by striking “not more than eight” and inserting “not more than 14”.
