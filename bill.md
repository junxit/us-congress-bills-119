# H.R. 10899

> To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to provide for individual and household assistance in the event of a prolonged power outage, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Power Outage Family Assistance Act”.

## § 2. Prolonged power outage

- **(a)** *In general* Title IV of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5170 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 431. Prolonged power outage
  >
  > - **(a)** *Designation* The President may, upon request of the Governor of a State or Chief Executive of an Indian tribal government, designate a prolonged power outage for purposes of providing assistance described in subsection (b) for the duration of such power outage.
  > - **(b)** *Assistance to individuals and households* The President may, in any area affected by a prolonged power outage designated under subsection (a), provide assistance to individuals and households in accordance with section 408, regardless of whether a major disaster is declared under section 401, for—
  >   - **(1)** serious needs;
  >   - **(2)** medical and dental costs;
  >   - **(3)** displacement costs;
  >   - **(4)** lodging expense reimbursements;
  >   - **(5)** miscellaneous item costs; and
  >   - **(6)** personal property costs.
  > - **(c)** *Applicability* This section shall not apply to any power outage that is—
  >   - **(1)** planned, scheduled, or pre-announced, including for maintenance, load management, or system upgrades; or
  >   - **(2)** primarily attributable to negligence, failure to maintain, or failure to comply with applicable reliability or safety standards by an electric utility or grid operator.
  > - **(d)** *Definitions* In this section:
  >   - **(1)** *Prolonged power outage* The term ‘prolonged power outage’ means a loss of electrical power caused by a covered event that affects not fewer than 10 percent of consumers in a political subdivision of a State for not fewer than 72 hours.
  >   - **(2)** *Covered event* The term “covered event” means—
  >     - **(A)** any natural catastrophe, including hurricanes, tornadoes, storms, high water, earthquakes, or volcanic eruptions;
  >     - **(B)** a terrorism or cybersecurity event; or
  >     - **(C)** regardless of cause, any fire, flood, or explosion.

- **(b)** *Recovery of assistance* Section 317 of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5160) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a)—
    - **(A)** by inserting “, or prolonged power outage designated under section 431,” before “shall be liable”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “such disaster or emergency” and inserting “such disaster, emergency, or power outage”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (b) by striking “or emergency” and inserting “, emergency, or prolonged power outage designated under section 431”.
- **(c)** *Rules and regulations* The President shall prescribe such rules and regulations as are necessary to carry out the amendments made by this Act.
