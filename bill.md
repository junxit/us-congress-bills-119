# H.R. 2334

> To amend the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to preempt any squatter’s rights established by State law regarding real property owned by a member of the uniformed services.

## § 1. Servicemembers Civil Relief Act: preemption of squatters’ rights

- **(a)** *Preemption* The Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (54 Stat. 1178; 50 U.S.C. 3901 et seq.) is amended by inserting, after section 301, the following new section:
  > ## § 301A. Preemption of squatter’s rights
  >
  > Any State law establishing squatter’s rights shall not apply to premises—
  >
  > - **(1)** owned by a servicemember; and
  > - **(2)** occupied by a squatter during a period of military service of such servicemember.
