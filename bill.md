# H.R. 5556

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to require air carriers to transfer funds to a ticket agent for cancelled or significantly delayed or changed flights within the time required for air carriers and ticket agents to provide refunds to passengers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Flight Refund Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Refunds for cancelled or significantly delayed or changed flights

Section 42305(e) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “promptly” and inserting “, not later than the date described in subsection (b),”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (3)—
  - **(A)** by striking “A refund” and inserting the following:
    > - **(A)** *Timing* In providing refunds under this subsection, a ticket agent shall provide such a refund to an individual not later than 7 days after the ticket agent receives the funds transferred by the air carrier or foreign air carrier.
    > - **(B)** *Alternatives* A refund

  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), as designated by paragraph (2), by striking “subsections (b) and” and inserting “subsection”.
