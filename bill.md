# S. 5342

> To require a report on the advisability and feasibility of establishing a Drone Center of Excellence within the Army National Guard.

## § 1. Report on establishment of Drone Center of Excellence within Army National Guard

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Army shall submit to the Committee on Armed Services of the Senate and the Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives a report on the advisability and feasibility of establishing a Drone Center of Excellence within the Army National Guard at a National Guard training site.
- **(b)** *Elements* The report required under subsection (a) shall consider performance of the following functions at the potential Center of Excellence:
  - **(1)** Certification of operators of unmanned aerial systems during annual training periods.
  - **(2)** Development of doctrine, tactics, techniques, and procedures for the operation of such systems.
  - **(3)** Experimentation on integrating artificial intelligence applications for target recognition and real-time situational awareness into the operation of such systems.
  - **(4)** Conduct of force-on-force unmanned aerial systems exercises, including brigade-level “drone wars” experimentation integrated with infantry, armor, and joint formations.
