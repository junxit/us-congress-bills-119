# S. 5456

> To prohibit the Secretary of Education from garnishing social security payments from individuals in default on their Federal student loans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Social Security Garnishment Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Prohibition on Social Security Offset

Part G of title IV of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1088 et seq.) is amended by inserting after section 493D the following:

> ## § 493E. Prohibition on Social Security Offset
>
> -  Notwithstanding section 3716(a) of title 31, United States Code, beginning on the date of enactment of the Stop Social Security Garnishment Act of 2026, any payments due to an individual under the Social Security Act shall not be subject to offset under section 3716(c)(3)(A)(i) of such title in the event of a default on a loan made, insured, or guaranteed under this title.
