# S. 5723

> To amend title 9, United States Code, with respect to arbitration of disputes involving child cyber injuries and child sexual exploitation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Forced Arbitration for Child Sexual Exploitation and Cyber Injuries Act”.

## § 2. Predispute arbitration of disputes involving child cyber injuries and child sexual exploitation

- **(a)** *In general* Title 9 of the United States Code is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(d)** *Application* An election under subsection (a) shall apply to the case as a whole and to every claim properly joined in the case by the electing claimant, whether or not each claim independently constitutes or relates to the child cyber injury dispute or child sexual exploitation dispute.

- **(b)** *Technical and conforming amendments*
  - **(1)** *In general* Title 9 of the United States Code is amended—
    - **(A)** in section 2, by inserting “or 5” before the period at the end;
    - **(B)** in section 208, in the second sentence, by inserting “or 5” before the period at the end; and
    - **(C)** in section 307, in the second sentence, by inserting “or 5” before the period at the end.
  - **(2)** *Table of chapters* The table of chapters for title 9, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

## § 3. Applicability

This Act, and the amendments made by this Act, shall apply with respect to any case or proceeding, including an arbitration, that is pending on or commenced on or after the date of enactment of this Act, regardless of when the conduct, injury, or agreement at issue occurred.
