# H.R. 5982

> To amend the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act to clarify that military retirement pay shall not be considered to be a social insurance payment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veteran Benefits Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Military retirement pay

Section 4(a–1)(ii) of the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act (45 U.S.C. 354(a–1)(ii)) is amended by inserting after “any other social insurance payments under any law” the following: “(except for a qualified military benefit (as defined in section 134(b) of title 26, United States Code))”.
