# H. CON. RES. 11

> Providing for a joint session of Congress to receive a message from the President.

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, March 4, 2025, at 9 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.
