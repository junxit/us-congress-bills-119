# H.J. RES. 55

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network relating to “Anti-Money Laundering Regulations for Residential Real Estate Transfers”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network relating to “Anti-Money Laundering Regulations for Residential Real Estate Transfers” (89 Fed. Reg. 70258 (August 29, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
