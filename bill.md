# H.R. 10104

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to eliminate the penalties for sale for use and use of dyed fuel in taxable use.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Economic Diesel Fuel Act of 2026”.

## § 2. No penalties for sale for use and use of dyed fuel in taxable use

- **(a)** *In general* The Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking section 6715.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 9503(b)(5)(A) of such Code is amended by striking “6715,”.
- **(c)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for part I of subchapter B of chapter 68 of such Code is amended by striking the item relating to section 6715.
- **(d)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to fuel sold or used after December 31, 2025.
