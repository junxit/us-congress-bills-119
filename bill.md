# H.R. 6750

> To codify Executive Order 14388 (relating to improving our Nation through better design).

## § 1. Codification of Executive Order 14388

Executive Order 14388 (90 Fed Reg. 41759; relating to improving our Nation through better design) shall have the force and effect of law.
