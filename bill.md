# S. 3816

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to permit a private cause of action for damages in the case of a group health plan which fails to provide for primary payment or appropriate reimbursement.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repair Abuses of MSP Payments (RAMP) Act”.

## § 2. Private cause of action for damages in the case of a group health plan which fails to provide for primary payment or appropriate reimbursement

Section 1862(b)(3)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395y(b)(3)(A)) is amended by striking “primary plan” and inserting “group health plan (as defined in paragraph (1)(A)(v))”.
