# S. 5602

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds for the planning and construction of any structure or monument at Memorial Circle at Columbia Island/ Lady Bird Johnson Park in the District of Columbia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Funds for Trump’s Illegal Arch Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of Federal funds for the planning and construction of any structure at Memorial Circle at Columbia Island/ Lady Bird Johnson Park in the District of Columbia

- **(a)** *Definition of Memorial Circle* In this section, the term Memorial Circle means the George Washington Memorial Parkway management unit designated as National Park Service reservation number 344 and tract identification number 344–012 within Lady Bird Johnson Park in the District of Columbia, which was formerly known as “Columbia Island”.
- **(b)** *Prohibition* No Federal funds may be used for the planning, design, engineering, permitting, or construction of any structure or monument at Memorial Circle, including the conduct of any ground preparation or ground disturbance activity, unless the structure or monument is expressly authorized in accordance with section 8106 of title 40, United States Code, or chapter 89 of that title (commonly known as the “Commemorative Works Act”).
