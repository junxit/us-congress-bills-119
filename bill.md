# H.R. 10803

> To abolish the Department of Homeland Security and establish a Department of Community Safety, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reimagining Safety Act”.

## § 3. Policy statement

Congress finds the following:

- **(1)** True safety is cultivated through universal care, economic equity, housing stability, and climate justice.
- **(2)** Peace and liberation are rooted in collective care and community well-being.
- **(3)** Our safety is interdependent and interconnected to the safety of our neighbors.
- **(4)** Hierarchies of human value, scapegoating, and dehumanization are harmful and dangerous.
- **(5)** Immigrants are our families, friends, and neighbors.
- **(6)** Our safety is protected through oversight, the rule of law, due process, and public accountability.
- **(7)** Safety is built through a robust social safety net and community-driven, community-led intervention.
- **(8)** Civil liberties and privacy are non-negotiable foundations of a safe, democratic society.
- **(9)** We brought the Department of Homeland Security into this world, and we can build a world without it, too.
