# H.R. 11068

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow small group market bronze plans in connection with health savings accounts.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Main Street Health Savings Act”.

## § 2. Allowance of small group market bronze plans in connection with health savings accounts

- **(a)** *In general* Section 223(c)(2)(H)(i) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “or available as coverage in the small group market (as defined in section 1304(a)(3) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act)” before “, and”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to months beginning after December 31, 2026.
