# S. 5273

> To improve fisheries stock assessments by incorporating eDNA, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fisheries Science Modernization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Administrator* The term Administrator means the Administrator of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.
- **(2)** *eDNA* The term eDNA means DNA or genetic material contained in samples of water or other environmental media.
- **(3)** *Fishery-independent* The term fishery-independent means observations not taken in the course of pursuing commercial fisheries activity.
- **(4)** *Indian Tribe* The term Indian Tribe has the meaning given the term in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304).
- **(5)** *National Aquatic Biomolecular Coordination Network* The term National Aquatic Biomolecular Coordination Network means the National Aquatic Biomolecular Coordination Network established by section 201.
- **(6)** *Native Hawaiian organization* The term Native Hawaiian organization has the meaning given the term in section 6207 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7517).

## § 3. Categorical exclusion for water, air, and sediment collection

The collection of water, air, or sediment samples exclusively for eDNA analysis shall not constitute a taking under the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1531 et seq.) or the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 (16 U.S.C. 1361 et seq.).
