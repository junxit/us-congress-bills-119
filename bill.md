# H.R. 11014

> To amend the Poultry Products Inspection Act to prohibit the force feeding of ducks and geese.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ducks and Geese Protection Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on force feeding of ducks and geese

The Poultry Products Inspection Act (21 U.S.C. 451 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 32. Prohibition on force feeding of ducks and geese
>
> - **(a)** *In general* It shall be unlawful for an individual to knowingly force feed a duck or goose for the purpose of producing foie gras.
> - **(b)** *Civil penalty* An individual who violates subsection (a) shall be subject to a civil penalty of not more than $14,000 per violation.
> - **(c)** *Definition* In this section, the term force feed means, with respect to a duck or goose, causing the duck or goose to consume more food than a typical duck or goose of the same species would consume voluntarily, including by delivering feed through a tube or other device inserted into the esophagus of the duck or goose.
