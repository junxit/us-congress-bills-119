# H.R. 6202

> To appropriate funds to the Travel Promotion Fund to offset reductions to the Fund made by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Brand USA Restoration Act”.

## § 2. Appropriation to Travel Promotion Fund

There are appropriated for fiscal year 2026, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, $80,000,000, to be deposited into the Travel Promotion Fund established by subsection (d) of the Travel Promotion Act of 2009 (22 U.S.C. 2131(d)).
