# H.R. 5971

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to increase the number of individuals from the District of Columbia who may be appointed to military service academies.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Service Academies District of Columbia Equality Act”.

## § 2. United States Military Academy

Section 7442(a)(5) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “Five” and inserting “Fifteen”.

## § 3. United States Naval Academy

Section 8454(a)(5) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “Five” and inserting “Fifteen”.

## § 4. United States Air Force Academy

Section 9442(a)(5) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking “Five” and inserting “Fifteen”.
