# H.R. 10886

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to require the Director of the Office of Personnel Management to annually publish a list of dual citizens employed by the Federal Government, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Dual Citizen Disclosure Act”.

## § 2. Publication of list of dual citizens working for Federal Government

Section 1103 of title 5, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(d)** Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of the Dual Citizen Disclosure Act and annually thereafter, the Director of the Office of Personnel Management shall publish a list, on the public website of the Office, with respect to each officer or employee of the Federal Government who is a citizen or national of the United States and a citizen or national of, or who otherwise owes allegiance to, a country other than the United States—
>   - **(1)** the countries of which the individual is a citizen or national;
>   - **(2)** the Federal agency that employs the individual; and
>   - **(3)** the title and annual rate of pay of the position at such Federal agency occupied by the individual.
