# H.R. 249

> To redesignate certain facilities at Paterson Great Falls National Historical Park in honor of Congressman Bill Pascrell, Jr.

## § 1. Redesignation of Paterson Great Falls NHP facilities

- **(a)** *Great Falls Scenic Overlook Trail Bridge* Great Falls Scenic Overlook Trail Bridge at Paterson Great Falls National Historical Park shall hereafter be known and designated as “Bill Pascrell, Jr. Scenic Overlook Trail Bridge”.
- **(b)** *Overlook Park* Overlook Park at Paterson Great Falls National Historical Park shall hereafter be known and designated as “Bill Pascrell, Jr. Overlook Park”.
- **(c)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to—
  - **(1)** Great Falls Scenic Overlook Trail Bridge shall be deemed to be a reference to Bill Pascrell, Jr. Scenic Overlook Trail Bridge; and
  - **(2)** Overlook Park shall be deemed to be a reference to Bill Pascrell, Jr. Overlook Park.
