# H.R. 1614

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to expand practitioners eligible to furnish telehealth services under the Medicare program.

## § 1. Expanding practitioners eligible to furnish telehealth services under Medicare

Section 1834(m)(4)(E) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m(m)(4)(E)) is amended by striking “and,” and all that follows through “shall include” and inserting “and includes”.
