# H.R. 8076

> To amend chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to prohibit covered individuals from trading on prediction markets, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Real-time Exploitation and Deceptive Insider Congressional Trading Act” or the “PREDICT Act”.
