# H.R. 10501

> To amend the Communist Control Act of 1954 to provide that the Democratic Socialists of America shall be treated like the Communist Party of the United States of America, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Communist Control Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Democratic Socialists of America treated as Communist Party

The Communist Control Act of 1954 is amended—

- **(1)** in section 3 (50 U.S.C. 842), by striking “or any successors of such party” and inserting “, the Democratic Socialists of America, or any successors of such party or organization”; and
- **(2)** in section 4(b) (50 U.S.C. 843(b))—
  - **(A)** by inserting “the organization now known as the Democratic Socialists of America,” after “the organization now known as Communist Party of the United States of America,”; and
  - **(B)** by inserting “or any successor organization” after “any unit or subdivision of any such organization”.
