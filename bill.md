# H. RES. 1675

> Commending and congratulating the Kansas City Current on winning the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League Championship.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on November 21, 2026, the Kansas City Current (referred to in this preamble as the “Current”) won the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League (referred to in this preamble as the “NWSL”) Championship;
- Whereas the Current defeated the Washington Spirit by 2 to 1 in the NWSL Championship, which was played on the opposing team’s home field in the District of Columbia;
- Whereas this is the first NWSL Championship win for the Current, with them having previously made it to the NWSL Championship in 2022;
- Whereas the Current defeated Angel City FC in the quarterfinals and Gotham FC in the semifinals on their path to the NWSL Championship;
- Whereas Current midfielder Vanessa DiBernardo— (1)was named the 2026 NWSL Championship Most Valuable Player; and (2)scored directly from a corner kick in the 54th minute of the championship;
- Whereas Current midfielder Debinha— (1) scored in the 97th minute of stoppage time during the championship, putting the Current up 2–0 and assuring the victory; and (2)in doing so, tied the league record for most career goals in an NWSL Championship;
- Whereas goalkeeper Lorena, the 2025 NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year, preserved the Current’s lead in the championship match, including a diving save on a corner-kick header;
- Whereas Temwa Chawinga, a forward for the Current, is a two-time NWSL Most Valuable Player and two-time NWSL Golden Boot winner, and in 2024 set a single-season league record of 20 goals;
- Whereas Croix Bethune, a midfielder for the Current, was named the 2026 NWSL Midfielder of the Year, for the second time in her career;
- Whereas Lo’eau LaBonta, a midfielder who has played for the Current since the club began play in 2021, captained the Current to the championship;
- Whereas Croix Bethune and Michelle Cooper of the Current are members of the United States Women’s National Team;
- Whereas, in 2025, the Current won the NWSL Shield, setting single-season league records of 65 points and 21 wins;
- Whereas the 2026 championship was the first season leading the Current for head coach Chris Armas;
- Whereas CPKC Stadium, where the Current play, is the first privately financed stadium built exclusively for a professional women’s soccer team; and
- Whereas the entire Current squad should be congratulated for its dedication, the resilience of its players in the face of hardship, its teamwork, and its impressive display of athletic talent: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** commends and congratulates the Kansas City Current on winning the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League Championship;
- **(2)** recognizes the achievement of all players, coaches, and staff who contributed to the success of the Kansas City Current during the 2026 season; and
- **(3)** respectfully requests that the Speaker of the House transmit an enrolled copy of this resolution to—
  - **(A)** the president of the Kansas City Current, Raven Jemison;
  - **(B)** the head coach of the Kansas City Current, Chris Armas;
  - **(C)** the co-owners of the Kansas City Current, Angie and Chris Long; and
  - **(D)** the co-owners of the Kansas City Current, Brittany and Patrick Mahomes.
