# H.R. 10982

> To prohibit the availability of Federal funds for the facilitation of euthanasia, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Vulnerable Patients, Seniors, and Families Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on availability of Federal funds

- **(a)** *Prohibition* No Federal funds may be made available to any individual or entity that carries out, assists in, or otherwise facilitates—
  - **(1)** the direct administration of a lethal substance to an individual to cause the death of such individual; or
  - **(2)** the provision to an individual of a lethal substance for the purpose of such individual self-administering such substance to cause the death of such individual.
- **(b)** *Rule of construction* Nothing in this section may be construed to prohibit the availability of Federal funds to any individual or entity on the basis of such individual or entity—
  - **(1)** withholding or withdrawing life-sustaining medical treatment from an individual to allow the death of such individual from an underlying condition; or
  - **(2)** carrying out, assisting in, or otherwise facilitating capital punishment.
