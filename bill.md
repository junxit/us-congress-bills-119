# H.R. 7250

> To reauthorize the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 2000.

## § 1. Reauthorization

-  Section 9 of the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 2000 (Public Law 106–382; 114 Stat. 1457; 123 Stat. 2856; 128 Stat. 164; 132 Stat. 2906) is amended, in each of subsections (a)(1) and (b), by striking “2026” and inserting “2028”.
