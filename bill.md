# H. RES. 202

> Censuring Representative Lauren Boebert of Colorado for her recent disparaging and derogatory comments about Representative Al Green of Texas.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on March 7, 2025, during an interview with Real America’s Voice News, Representative Lauren Boebert of Colorado said “For him to go and shake his pimp cane” about another Member, Representative from Texas, Mr. Al Green; and
- Whereas the words of the Representative from Colorado are disparaging, derogatory, and racist toward another colleague, and are a breach of proper conduct and decorum of the U.S. House of Representatives: Now, therefore, be it

That—

- **(1)** Representative Lauren Boebert be censured;
- **(2)** Representative Lauren Boebert forthwith present herself in the well of the House of Representatives for the pronouncement of censure; and
- **(3)** ) Representative Lauren Bobert be censured with the public reading of this resolution by the Speaker.
