# H.R. 4204

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to allow Medicare beneficiaries to choose their physical and occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, audiologists, and chiropractors.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Patient Choice Act”.

## § 2. Free choice of therapists for Medicare beneficiaries

Section 1802(b) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395a(b)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)—
  - **(A)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “physician or practitioner” and inserting “physician, practitioner, therapist, or qualified audiologist”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), in the matter preceding clause (i), by striking “physician or practitioner” and inserting “physician, practitioner, therapist, or qualified audiologist”;
