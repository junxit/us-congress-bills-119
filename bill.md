# S. 2660

> To amend title 49, United States Code, to protect the use of risk-based approaches by owners and operators of certain pipeline facilities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Modern Risk Detection Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Risk-based approaches

Section 60102 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(u)** *Risk-Based approaches* The Secretary shall ensure that the standards prescribed under this chapter allow for the use, to the maximum extent practicable, of risk-based approaches, concepts, or principles in complying with those standards.
