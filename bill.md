# H.R. 8411

> To increase the annual rate of basic pay for career employees of the Transportation Security Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Original Legislation to Give TSA Employees a Raise”.

## § 2. TSA employee pay increase

- **(a)** *In general* Beginning on the first day of the applicable pay period—
  - **(1)** the annual rate of basic pay for each career employee shall be increased by 15 percent; and
  - **(2)** the annual rate of basic pay for each pay band under the Transportation Security Administration Core Compensation System shall be increased by 15 percent.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Applicable pay period* The term “applicable pay period” means the pay period that—
    - **(A)** is the first pay period beginning on or after the date of the enactment of this Act; and
    - **(B)** occurs prior to the beginning of the second pay period beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
  - **(2)** *Career employee* The term “career employee” means any career agent or officer of the Transportation Security Administration who is not a senior executive, a career appointee, or a senior career employee (as such terms are defined in section 114(n)(2) of title 49, United States Code) of the Administration.
