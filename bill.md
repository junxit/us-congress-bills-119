# S. 4830

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Waianae, Hawaii, as the “United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.

## § 1. United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 86–014 Farrington Highway in Waianae, Hawaii, shall be known and designated as the “United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “United States Representative Colleen Hanabusa Post Office Building”.
