# S. 5776

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to permit a Federal employee to take parental bereavement leave with respect to the death of a child who is younger than 26 years of age, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Parental Bereavement Leave Expansion Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Parental bereavement leave

Section 6329d(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by amending paragraph (1) to read as follows:
  > - **(1)** the term employee has the meaning given the term in section 6381;

- **(2)** in paragraph (2)(C), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(3)** the term son or daughter means a biological, adopted, or foster child, a stepchild, a legal ward, or a child of a person standing in loco parentis, who is—
  >   - **(A)** under 26 years of age; or
  >   - **(B)** 26 years of age or older and incapable of self-care because of a mental or physical disability.
