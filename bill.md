# H.R. 7418

> To convert the program of public financing of presidential election campaigns to a program of providing grants to States for enhancing the security of election systems, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing Tech and Election Administration Defenses For All States and Territories Act” or the “STEADFAST Act”.

## § 2. Conversion of presidential election campaign funding program to State grant program for election security

- **(a)** *Election security program*

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for subtitle H of such Code is amended by adding at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Designation of income tax payments to Election Security Fund*
  - **(1)** *Designation* Section 6096(a) of such Code is amended by striking “the Presidential Election Campaign Fund” and inserting “the Election Security Fund”.
  - **(2)** *Reference to EAC website with public information on Program and Fund* Section 6096(a) of such Code is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: “The Secretary shall ensure that the individual tax return form includes a citation to the hyperlink on the official public website of the Election Assistance Commission which is established and operated under section 9053.”.
  - **(3)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to taxable years ending after December 31, 2025.
