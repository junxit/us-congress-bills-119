# S.J. RES. 122

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Air Plan Approval; Indiana; Regional Haze Plan for the Second Implementation Period”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Air Plan Approval; Indiana; Regional Haze Plan for the Second Implementation Period” (91 Fed. Reg. 3057 (January 26, 2026)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
