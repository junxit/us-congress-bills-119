# H.R. 2453

> To continue Executive Order 14224 in effect indefinitely.

## § 1. Executive Order 14224

Executive Order 14224, entitled “Designating English as the Official Language of the United States” (90 Fed. Reg. 11363; March 1, 2025), and any action taken or regulation issued by any agency pursuant to such Executive Order shall remain in effect.
