# H.R. 3677

> To codify Executive Order 14292 relating to Improving the Safety and Security of Biological Research.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Executive Order 14292 Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Codification of Executive Order

Executive Order 14292 (relating to Improving the Safety and Security of Biological Research (signed on May 5, 2025)) shall have the force and effect of law.
