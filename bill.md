# H.R. 10992

> To reauthorize the Tribal College Initiative Grants Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tribal College and University Essential Community Facilities Act”.

## § 2. Tribal college and university essential community facilities

Section 306(a)(25)(C) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1926(a)(25)(C)) is amended by striking “2008 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
