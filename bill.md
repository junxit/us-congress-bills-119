# H.R. 8818

> To establish grants to combat opioid trafficking.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “End Fentanyl Trafficking with Local Task Forces Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Grant authorization

- **(a)** *In general* Title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10101 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new part:
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 1701(m) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10381(m)) is repealed.
