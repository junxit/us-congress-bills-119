# H.R. 8815

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to change certain eligibility provisions for loan forgiveness for teachers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Teacher Debt Relief Act”.

## § 2. Loan forgiveness for teachers

The Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1001 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 428J(g)(2) (20 U.S.C. 1078–10(g)(2))—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting “or” after the semicolon at the end;
  - **(B)** by striking subparagraph (B); and
  - **(C)** by redesignating subparagraph (C) as subparagraph (B);
- **(2)** in section 455(m) (20 U.S.C. 1087e(m)), in paragraph (4), by striking “both this subsection and section 428J, 428K, 428L, or 460” and inserting “both this subsection and section 428K or 428L”; and
- **(3)** in section 460(g)(2) (20 U.S.C. 1087j(g)(2))—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting “or” after the semicolon at the end;
  - **(B)** by striking subparagraph (B); and
  - **(C)** by redesignating subparagraph (C) as subparagraph (B).
