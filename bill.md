# S. 5658

> To prohibit the construction of data centers on Federal land, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition of construction of data centers on Federal land

- **(a)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Data center* The term “data center” has the meaning given the term in section 453(a) of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17112(a)).
  - **(2)** *Federal land*
    - **(A)** *In general* The term “Federal land” means—
      - **(i)** National Forest System land; and
      - **(ii)** land managed by the Secretary of the Interior.
    - **(B)** *Exclusions* The term “Federal land” does not include—
      - **(i)** any land located within the boundaries of an Indian reservation, pueblo, or rancheria; or
      - **(ii)** any land not located within the boundaries of an Indian reservation, pueblo, or rancheria, the title to which is held—
        - **(I)** in trust by the United States for the benefit of an Indian Tribe or an individual Indian;
        - **(II)** by an Indian Tribe or an individual Indian, subject to restriction against alienation under laws of the United States; or
        - **(III)** by a dependent Indian community.
  - **(3)** *Indian Tribe* The term Indian Tribe has the meaning given the term in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304).
  - **(4)** *National forest system* The term National Forest System has the meaning given the term in section 11(a) of the Forest and Rangeland Renewable Resources Planning Act of 1974 (16 U.S.C. 1609(a)).
- **(b)** *Prohibition on construction of data centers* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the construction of data centers on Federal land is prohibited.
- **(c)** *Reversionary clause required for Federal land conveyances* Any conveyance of Federal land occurring on or after the date of enactment of this Act shall include a reversionary clause to ensure that the conveyed Federal land shall revert to the United States if the conveyed Federal land is used for a data center, unless the use of the conveyed Federal land for a data center is specifically authorized by an Act of Congress.
