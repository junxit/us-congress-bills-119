# H.R. 1118

> To amend title 41, United States Code, to provide best value through the multiple award schedule program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Value Over Cost Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Providing best value through the multiple award schedule program

Section 152(3)(B) of title 41, United States Code, is amended by striking “lowest overall cost alternative” and inserting “best value”.
