# S. 5689

> To prohibit Federal and State incentives for the development or commercial operations of data centers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Recapturing Excessive Corporate Largesse through Accountability and Incentive Measures Act” or the “RECLAIM Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on incentives for covered data centers

- **(a)** *No Federal incentives* A covered data center (as defined in section 5000E(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as added by subsection (b)) shall not be eligible for any Federal grant, loan, loan guarantee, cooperative agreement, tax credit or deduction, exclusion, exemption, abatement, or other form of Federal financial assistance or tax incentive for the development or commercial operations of such covered data center.
- **(b)** *Excise tax on prohibited state and local incentives*

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of chapters for subtitle D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after the item relating to chapter 50A the following new item:
- **(c)** *Sense of Congress* It is the sense of Congress that States and political subdivisions thereof should promptly repeal or reform any tax incentive programs that are specific to data centers in order to restore tax neutrality, protect public revenues, and eliminate distortions in business location decisions that disadvantage domestic manufacturing and other job-creating investments.
- **(d)** *Effective dates* The provisions of this section, and any amendment to the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 made by this section, shall take effect on the day after the date on which final regulations are issued by the Secretary pursuant to section 5000E(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (as added by subsection (b)).

## § 3. Severability

If any provision of this Act or amendment made by this Act or the application of such provision or amendment to any person or circumstance is held to be unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act and the amendments made by this Act, and the application of the provision or amendment to any other person or circumstance, shall not be affected.
