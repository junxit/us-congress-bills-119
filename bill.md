# S. 4662

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to ensure that high net-worth individuals cannot avoid paying taxes on their income and assets.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Redistribution Of Billions by Instituting New High-income Obligations on Overlooked Debt Act of 2026” or the “ROBINHOOD Act of 2026”.
