# S.J. RES. 29

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Air Plan Approval; Ohio; Withdrawal of Technical Amendment”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “Air Plan Approval; Ohio; Withdrawal of Technical Amendment” (90 Fed. Reg. 6811 (January 21, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
