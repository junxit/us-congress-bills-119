# S. 3319

> To promote a 21st century workforce, to authorize grants to support emerging and advanced technology education, and to support training and quality employment for workers in industries most impacted by artificial intelligence.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Workforce of the Future Act of 2025”.
