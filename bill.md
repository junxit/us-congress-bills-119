# H. RES. 839

> Condemning Hamas for assassinating innocent Palestinians.

## Preamble

- Whereas, since the implementation of the ceasefire agreement on October 10, 2025, Hamas has carried out a campaign of executions and intimidation against innocent Palestinians in Gaza as it seeks to suppress dissent and reassert control over the territory, resulting in the deaths of scores of civilians; and
- Whereas Hamas is designated as a Foreign Terrorist Organization by the United States: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** condemns in the strongest terms the killings and acts of terror committed by Hamas against innocent Palestinians in Gaza; and
- **(2)** reaffirms the commitment of the United States to supporting the implementation of the ceasefire agreement and to advancing stability and peace for innocent civilians in Gaza.
