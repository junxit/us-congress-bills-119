# H.R. 10951

> To amend the Commodity Exchange Act to prohibit the listing of contracts relating to wildfires.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Bets on Wildfires Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on event contracts on wildfires

Section 5c of the Commodity Exchange Act (7 U.S.C. 7a–2) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(d)** *Prohibition on event contracts relating to wildfires* A registered entity shall not list for trading or accept for clearing on or through the registered entity any agreement, contract, transaction, or swap that involves a wildfire caused by whatever means.
