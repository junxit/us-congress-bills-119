# H.R. 8828

> To direct the Attorney General to establish an official station for the United States attorney for the Western District of Washington in the county of Clark, Washington, and for other purposes.

## § 1. United States attorney official station in Clark county, Washington

The Attorney General shall establish an official station for the United States attorney for the Western District of Washington in the county of Clark, Washington.
