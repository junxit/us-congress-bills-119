# H.R. 11048

> To direct the Director of the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency to conduct a comprehensive assessment of commercially available artificial intelligence tools capable of automated analysis of satellite and geospatial imagery, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Geospatial Intelligence Transparency and Security Act of 2026” or the “GITS Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Assessment of commercial artificial intelligence tools for geospatial imagery analysis

- **(a)** *Findings* Congress finds the following:
  - **(1)** Commercial artificial intelligence tools capable of automated analysis of satellite and geospatial imagery have proliferated rapidly and are broadly accessible on the open market without meaningful access restrictions.
  - **(2)** Such tools are capable of performing military-relevant functions, including automated object identification, activity analysis, change detection, order-of-battle analysis, military vehicle classification, and facility monitoring at scale, that previously required classified national technical means or state-level intelligence resources.
  - **(3)** Adversary state actors and nonstate armed groups have demonstrated the ability and willingness to exploit commercially available geospatial artificial intelligence tools for military targeting purposes, including during recent conflicts in the Middle East.
  - **(4)** The existing regulatory framework has not kept pace with the rapid evolution of commercial geospatial artificial intelligence capabilities, leaving a significant and growing gap in the national security posture of the United States.
  - **(5)** The National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, as the lead Federal agency for geospatial intelligence and manager of the National System for Geospatial Intelligence, possesses unique technical expertise and visibility into both classified and commercial geospatial intelligence capabilities, making it the appropriate agency to assess the national security implications of this commercial landscape.
- **(b)** *Assessment*
  - **(1)** *Requirement* The Director of the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency shall carry out a comprehensive assessment of commercially available artificial intelligence tools capable of automated analysis of satellite and geospatial imagery.
  - **(2)** *Scope* The assessment under paragraph (1) shall include the following:
    - **(A)** A landscape assessment of commercially available artificial intelligence tools, including hosted inference services, application programming interfaces, pretrained models, and software platforms, capable of automated military-relevant object identification, activity analysis, or targeting-support functions using satellite or other geospatial imagery, with specific attention to tools and capabilities that are broadly accessible on the commercial market and are not subject to meaningful access restrictions.
    - **(B)** An assessment of the degree to which adversary state actors, nonstate armed groups, and other malicious actors are currently accessing or could foreseeably access such commercial tools, directly, through intermediaries, or through third-party markets, and what military or intelligence advantage such access would confer upon those actors.
    - **(C)** An evaluation of the specific commercial geospatial artificial intelligence capabilities that present the greatest national security concern, including identification of technical characteristics common to such tools, with attention to the gap between what these tools can deliver commercially and what previously required classified national technical means.
- **(c)** *Report*
  - **(1)** *Requirement* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Director shall submit to the Secretary of Defense and the appropriate congressional committees a report on the findings of the assessment under subsection (b).
  - **(2)** *Form* The report under paragraph (1) shall be submitted in unclassified form, but may include a classified annex.
  - **(3)** *Transmission to relevant agencies* Concurrent with the submission of the report to the Secretary of Defense and the appropriate congressional committees under paragraph (1), the Director shall transmit the unclassified findings of the report, together with any unclassified recommendations, to—
    - **(A)** the Secretary of Commerce, for consideration in any rulemaking or review of export control classifications applicable to geospatial imagery analysis software or related artificial intelligence tools under the Export Administration Regulations; and
    - **(B)** the Secretary of State, for consideration in any bilateral or multilateral negotiations relating to the control or restriction of commercial geospatial artificial intelligence capabilities.
- **(d)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** The term appropriate congressional committees means—
    - **(A)** the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence of the House of Representatives;
    - **(B)** the Select Committee on Intelligence of the Senate;
    - **(C)** the Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives; and
    - **(D)** the Committee on Armed Services of the Senate.
  - **(2)** The term geospatial imagery analysis means the automated or semiautomated processing of satellite or aerial imagery to identify, classify, track, or characterize objects, activities, or changes of potential military or intelligence relevance.
