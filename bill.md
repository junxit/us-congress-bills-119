# H.R. 648

> To direct the Secretary of Defense to provide fluid or powdered milk to members of the Armed Forces at dining facilities on military installations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Strengthening our Servicemembers with Milk Act”.

## § 2. Provision of milk to members of the Armed Forces at dining facilities on military installations

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Defense shall provide fluid or powdered milk to members of the Armed Forces at dining facilities on military installations, in varieties that may include—
  - **(1)** unflavored;
  - **(2)** flavored;
  - **(3)** organic;
  - **(4)** non-organic;
  - **(5)** whole;
  - **(6)** reduced-fat;
  - **(7)** low-fat;
  - **(8)** fat-free;
  - **(9)** lactose-free; or
  - **(10)** any combination thereof.
- **(b)** *Prohibition* The Secretary may not, to carry out this section, purchase fluid or powdered milk from an entity owned or controlled by a foreign adversary, as determined by the Secretary of Commerce under section 7.4 of title 15, Code of Federal Regulations (or successor regulation).
