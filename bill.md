# H. RES. 311

> Dismissing the election contest relating to the office of Representative from the Thirtieth Congressional District of Texas.

That the election contest relating to the office of Representative from the Thirtieth Congressional District of Texas is dismissed on the grounds of untimely filing with the House of Representatives.
