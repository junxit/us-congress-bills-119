# H.R. 10297

> To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate El Salvador for temporary protected status.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “El Salvador TPS Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Designation of El Salvador for temporary protected status

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall designate El Salvador for temporary protected status until the date that is 18 months after September 9, 2026.
