# H.R. 10646

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Defense to enter into intergovernmental support agreements on behalf of Defense Agencies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Installation-support services: intergovernmental support agreements

Section 2679(f) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(5)** The term Secretary concerned includes the Secretary of Defense acting on behalf of the head of a Defense Agency.
