# H.R. 7518

> To amend the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to ensure adequate staffing and resources for the Institute of Tropical Forestry and the Institute of Pacific Islands Forestry.

## § 1. Institutes of Tropical Forestry and Pacific Islands Forestry

Section 2407 of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 6706) is amended—

- **(1)** in the section heading, by inserting “and Pacific Islands Forestry” after “Tropical Forestry”;
- **(2)** in the matter preceding paragraph (1), in the second sentence, by striking “The Institutes” and inserting the following:
  > - **(b)** *Activities* The Institutes

- **(3)** in the matter preceding subsection (b) (as so designated), by striking “The Secretary” and inserting the following:
  > - **(a)** *In general* The Secretary

- **(4)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(c)** *Staffing and resources* The Secretary shall ensure that—
  >   - **(1)** the Institute of Tropical Forestry in Puerto Rico has a staff of not fewer than 50 individuals and adequate resources to credibly and substantially advance the science, research, demonstration, and knowledge exchange relating to the activities described in subsection (b); and
  >   - **(2)** the Institute of Pacific Islands Forestry has a staff of not fewer than 30 individuals and adequate resources to credibly and substantially advance the science, research, demonstration, and knowledge exchange relating to the activities described in subsection (b).
