# H.R. 11038

> To require the Secretary of State to notify Congress of gray zone activities carried out by the Russian Federation against NATO allies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Warning Allies and Tracking Covert Hostilities by Russia Act” or the “WATCH Russia Act”.

## § 2. Congressional notification of Russian gray zone activities against NATO allies

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, and quarterly thereafter, the Secretary of State shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees a report of the gray zone activities known or reasonably suspected to have been carried out by the Russian Federation, or at the direction of the Government of the Russian Federation, against a NATO ally during the preceding quarter.
- **(b)** *Contents* A report submitted under subsection (a) shall include—
  - **(1)** a description of each gray zone activity;
  - **(2)** the NATO country in which the gray zone activity took place or the NATO country otherwise affected by such activities and the intended target of the gray zone activity;
  - **(3)** an assessment of the entities responsible for carrying out and directing such activities;
  - **(4)** an assessment of the objectives of each gray zone activity and effects of such activities;
  - **(5)** a description of any response taken or planned by the United States, NATO, or the NATO ally; and
  - **(6)** any other information the Secretary of State determines appropriate.
- **(c)** *Form* A report submitted under subsection (a) shall be submitted in unclassified form but may include a classified annex.
- **(d)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Appropriate congressional committees* The term appropriate congressional committees means the—
    - **(A)** Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives;
    - **(B)** Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives;
    - **(C)** Committee on Armed Services of the Senate; and
    - **(D)** Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate.
  - **(2)** *Gray zone activities* The term gray zone activities means activities intended to advance the political, military, economic, or security interests of a country through coercive, malign, or destabilizing means while remaining below the threshold of a conventional armed conflict, including—
    - **(A)** cyber operations;
    - **(B)** disinformation or foreign malign influence operations;
    - **(C)** sabotage or attacks against critical infrastructure;
    - **(D)** economic coercion;
    - **(E)** interference in democratic institutions or electoral processes;
    - **(F)** the use of proxies, covert operatives, or irregular forces; or
    - **(G)** incursions into the airspace, territorial waters, or exclusive economic zone of a NATO ally.
  - **(3)** *NATO* The term NATO means the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.
