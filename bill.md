# H.R. 10808

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow additional contributions to a health savings account in the case of the birth or adoption of a child, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Savings for Tomorrow’s Opportunities, Raising Kids Act” or the “STORK Act”.

## § 2. Additional contributions to health savings account allowed in case of birth or adoption of child

- **(a)** *In general* Section 223(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(9)** *Additional contributions in case of birth or adoption of child*
  >   - **(A)** *In general* For each qualifying child who is born to, placed for adoption with, or adopted by an individual before the close of the taxable year, the limitation which would (but for this paragraph) apply to such individual under subparagraphs (A) and (B) of paragraph (2) shall be increased by $2,000.
  >   - **(B)** *Married individuals* In the case of individuals who are married to each other and are eligible individuals for any month during the taxable year, the amount of increase allowed under subparagraph (A) with respect to any qualifying child who is born to, placed for adoption with, or adopted by such individuals before the close of the taxable year shall be divided between such individuals in such manner as they may elect, except that the total amount of increases allowed to both individuals with respect to any such child shall not exceed $2,000.
  >   - **(C)** *Limitation* This paragraph shall not apply with respect to any qualifying child with respect to whom an individual (or the individual’s spouse) was allowed an increase under this paragraph for any preceding taxable year.
  >   - **(D)** *Qualifying child* For purposes of this paragraph, the term qualifying child means an individual who has not attained age 18 before the close of the taxable year.

- **(d)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2026.
