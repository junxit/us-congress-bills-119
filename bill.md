# H.R. 10219

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to lower the threshold for which criminal penalties apply to a violation of that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SNAP Fraud Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Violations and enforcement of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008

Section 15(b)(1) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2024(b)(1)) is amended by striking “$5,000” each place it appears and inserting “$1,000”.
