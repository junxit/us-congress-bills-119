# H.R. 10199

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to prohibit the President, the Vice President, Members of Congress, senior United States Government officials, and their spouses and dependents from owning or trading stocks, digital assets, and prediction market contracts, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sell Your Stocks or Step Down Act”.
