# S.J. RES. 133

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Fair Credit Reporting; Background Screening”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Fair Credit Reporting; Background Screening, 89 Fed. Reg. 4171 (January 23, 2024)” (90 Fed. Reg. 20084 (May 12, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
