# S. 2769

> To repeal title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005 in order to safeguard civil liberties and individual privacy.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safeguarding Personal Information Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Repeal of certain requirements for documents accepted for Federal identification purposes

Title II of the REAL ID Act of 2005 (49 U.S.C. 30301 note; division B of Public Law 109–13) is repealed.

## § 3. Conforming amendments

- **(a)** *Benefits for Afghanistan nationals* Section 2502(b)(3) of the Afghanistan Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2022 (8 U.S.C. 1101 note) is amended by striking “under section 202 of the REAL ID Act of 2005 (division B of Public Law 109–13; 49 U.S.C. 30301 note), notwithstanding subsection (c)(2)(B) of such Act”.
- **(b)** *Grants to States* Section 475(5)(I) of the Social Security Act(42 U.S.C. 675(5)(I)) is amended by striking “in accordance with the requirements of section 202 of the REAL ID Act of 2005”.
