# S. 5079

> To amend the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to reauthorize the Genome to Phenome Initiative.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Genome to Phenome Initiative Reauthorization Act of 2026” or the “AG2PI Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Genome to Phenome Initiative

Section 1671(g) of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 U.S.C. 5924(g)) is amended by striking “2023” and inserting “2031”.
