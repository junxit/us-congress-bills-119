# H.R. 10986

> To direct the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Lebanon for temporary protected status.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lebanon TPS Act”.

## § 2. Designation of Lebanon for temporary protected status

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall designate Lebanon for temporary protected status for the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act until April 20, 2029.
