# S. 5501

> To amend the Alaska Power Administration Asset Sale and Termination Act to modify and continue an exemption from application of the Federal Power Act for the Snettisham Hydroelectric Project, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Modification and continuation of exemption

Section 104(b) of the Alaska Power Administration Asset Sale and Termination Act (Public Law 104–58; 42 U.S.C. 7152 note) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “or Snettisham”; and
- **(2)** by striking “or the Snettisham Purchaser”.
