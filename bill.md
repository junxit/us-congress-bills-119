# H.R. 10171

> To establish a supplemental food and nutrition services grant program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Improving Health Through Integrated Food and Nutrition Services at Federally Qualified Health Centers Act”.

## § 2. Findings

The Congress finds the following:

- **(1)** Millions of Americans face food insecurity, which is a key driver of poor health outcomes and chronic disease.
- **(2)** Federally qualified health centers are uniquely positioned to integrate food and nutrition services into clinical care.
- **(3)** Federally qualified health centers serve populations that are disproportionately affected by food insecurity, chronic disease, and limited access to affordable, nutritious food. Addressing food access as a health intervention is essential to improving patient outcomes and reducing long-term health care costs.

## § 3. Supplemental food and nutrition services grant program

- **(a)** *Establishment* The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Administrator of the Health Resources and Services Administration, (in this section referred to as the “Secretary”) shall establish a program to award grants, on a competitive basis, to Federally qualified health centers to—
  - **(1)** implement food insecurity screening tools;
  - **(2)** operate on-site food pantries;
  - **(3)** partner with food banks, farms, and nutrition assistance programs;
  - **(4)** employ registered dieticians and nutrition professionals; and
  - **(5)** offer culturally tailored nutrition education.
- **(b)** *Applications* To be eligible for a grant under this section, a Federally qualified health center shall submit to the Secretary an application at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Secretary may require.
- **(c)** *Technical assistance and capacity building* The Secretary shall provide to recipients of grants under this section—
  - **(1)** training and best practices for integrating food and nutrition services into care delivery; and
  - **(2)** support for data collection, evaluation, and reporting.
- **(d)** *Coordination with Federal nutrition programs* In administering the program under this section, the Secretary shall coordinate with the Secretary of Agriculture and the Administrator for the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services to—
  - **(1)** streamline referrals and enrollment in—
    - **(A)** the supplemental nutrition assistance program under the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.) (in this section referred to as “SNAP”);
    - **(B)** the special supplemental nutrition program for women, infants, and children under section 17 of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 (42 U.S.C. 1786) (in this section referred to as “WIC”); and
    - **(C)** other nutrition programs;
  - **(2)** encourage Medicaid waivers or demonstration projects that support nutrition services as reimbursable care; and
  - **(3)** explore opportunities under value-based payment models to fund food and nutrition interventions.
- **(e)** *Reporting and evaluation* As a condition of receiving a grant under this section, a Federally qualified health center shall agree to submit to the Secretary annual reports detailing—
  - **(1)** the number of patients served by the grant;
  - **(2)** the number of referrals to, and enrollments in, SNAP, WIC, and other nutrition programs; and
  - **(3)** the community partnerships and resource utilization facilitated by the grant.
- **(f)** *Federally qualified health center defined* In this section, the term Federally qualified health center has the meaning given the term in section 1861(aa) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x(aa)).
- **(g)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $100,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031.
