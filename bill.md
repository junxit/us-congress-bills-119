# H.R. 8359

> To change the address of the postal facility designated in honor of Specialist Jeffrey L. White, Jr.

## § 1. Specialist Jeffrey L. White, Jr. Post Office Redesignation

- **(a)** *Redesignation* Section 1(a) of Public Law 115–134 (132 Stat. 340) is amended by striking “324” and inserting “211”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 of Public Law 115–134, as amended by subsection (a), shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Specialist Jeffrey L. White, Jr. Post Office”.
