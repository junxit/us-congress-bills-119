# H. RES. 1629

> Expressing support for the designation of the third Wednesday of October, as “Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day”.

## Preamble

- Whereas Chambers of Commerce are champions of communities, striving to address the community’s top challenges and greatest opportunities;
- Whereas, through bold leadership and innovative problem solving, Chambers of Commerce foster stronger, more vibrant communities;
- Whereas Chambers of Commerce advocate for priorities to promote economic prosperity, enhancing the quality of life of the community;
- Whereas Chambers of Commerce create access to meaningful employment and opportunity for all residents;
- Whereas Chambers of Commerce serve as a trusted resource for businesses, residents, and other stakeholders;
- Whereas Chambers of Commerce create catalytic community partnerships to build resilient economies and promote community-wide prosperity; and
- Whereas the third Wednesday of October, would be an appropriate date to designate as “Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day”: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives supports the designation of “Support Your Local Chamber of Commerce Day”.
