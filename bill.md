# S. RES. 958

> Recognizing the week of November 9 through November 13, 2026, as “National Veterans Small Business Week”.

## Preamble

- Whereas the Armed Forces of the United States train individuals with the skills, discipline, and leadership necessary to establish and operate a successful business;
- Whereas there are 1,651,542 veteran-owned small businesses in the United States, employing 3,245,316 individuals;
- Whereas veteran-owned small businesses make up 5.3 percent of all businesses in the United States;
- Whereas veteran-owned small businesses account for more than $952,000,000,000 in total sales every year;
- Whereas the Small Business Administration hosts events honoring National Veterans Small Business Week from November 9 through November 13, 2026;
- Whereas the Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship of the Senate celebrates National Veterans Small Business Week during the week of November 9 through November 13, 2026; and
- Whereas the week of November 9 through November 13, 2026, would be an appropriate time to celebrate National Veterans Small Business Week: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate—

- **(1)** recognizes the week of November 9 through November 13, 2026, as “National Veterans Small Business Week”;
- **(2)** supports the goals and ideals of National Veterans Small Business Week;
- **(3)** recognizes the importance of creating policies that promote a business-friendly environment for small business owners that is free of unnecessary and burdensome regulations and red tape; and
- **(4)** expresses appreciation for the continued service to the United States by the veterans of the United States through small business ownership and entrepreneurship.
