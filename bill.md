# H.R. 11067

> To improve and promote commercial exports of defense articles and defense services by small businesses.

## § 1. Commercial exports of defense articles and defense services by small businesses

The head of the Directorate of Defense Trade Controls (DDTC) of the Department of State, in coordination with the head of the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization (OSDBU) of the Department of State, shall take such actions as may be necessary—

- **(1)** to establish and maintain a single point of contact for the application process for commercial exports of defense articles and defense services, with a focus on assisting small businesses; and
- **(2)** to otherwise promote commercial exports of defense articles and defense services by small businesses.
