# H.R. 10869

> To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act and the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to provide for emerging contaminants remediation funding, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protect Clean Water Act”.

## § 2. Emerging contaminants State revolving fund programs

- **(a)** *Safe Drinking Water Act program* Section 1452(t)(2) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–12(t)(2)) is amended by striking “$100,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2020 through 2024” and inserting “$1,000,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031”.
- **(b)** *Federal Water Pollution Control Act program* Title VI of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1381 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 610. Emerging contaminants
  >
  > - **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law and subject to subsection (b), amounts deposited under subsection (d) in a State loan fund established under this title may only be used to provide grants for the purpose of addressing emerging contaminants, with a focus on perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances.
  > - **(b)** *Requirements*
  >   - **(1)** *Small and disadvantaged communities* Not less than 25 percent of the amounts described in subsection (a) shall be used to provide grants to entities that—
  >     - **(A)** are eligible to receive additional subsidization under section 603(i)(1)(A); or
  >     - **(B)** are eligible to receive financial assistance provided under section 603(c) and own or operate treatment works serving fewer than 25,000 persons.
  >   - **(2)** *Priorities* A State shall select recipients of grants under subsection (a) consistent with the determination by the State under section 216 of the priority to be given categories of projects.
  > - **(c)** *No increased bonding authority* The amounts deposited in the water pollution control revolving fund of a State under subsection (d) may not be used as a source of payment of, or security for (directly or indirectly), in whole or in part, any obligation the interest on which is exempt from the tax imposed under chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
  > - **(d)** *Funding*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* Amounts made available under this subsection shall be allotted to a State as if allotted under section 604 as a capitalization grant, for deposit into the water pollution control revolving fund of the State, for the purposes described in this section.
  >   - **(2)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $1,000,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031, to remain available until expended.
