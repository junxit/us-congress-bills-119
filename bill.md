# H.R. 11129

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to establish a tax credit for composting.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Credits for Composting Act”.

## § 2. Composting credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subpart A of part IV of subchapter A of chapter 1 (relating to nonrefundable personal credits) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after section 25F the following new section:
  > ## § 25G. Composting credit
  >
  > - **(a)** *Allowance of credit* In the case of an individual, there shall be allowed as a credit against the tax imposed by this chapter for the taxable year an amount equal to 25 percent of the sum of—
  >   - **(1)** amounts paid or incurred to acquire or apply compost during the taxable year, and
  >   - **(2)** amounts paid or incurred to acquire eligible composting equipment during the taxable year.
  > - **(b)** *Annual limitation* The credit allowed under this section with respect to a taxpayer for the taxable year shall not exceed $50.
  > - **(c)** *Definitions* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *Compost* The term “compost” means a product that—
  >     - **(A)** is manufactured through a controlled aerobic, biological decomposition of biodegradable materials, and
  >     - **(B)** is used as a soil amendment or fertilizer.
  >   - **(2)** *Eligible composting equipment* The term “eligible composting equipment” means equipment that subjects compostable material to medium or high temperature, which—
  >     - **(A)** significantly reduces the viability of pathogens and weed seeds, and
  >     - **(B)** stabilizes carbon in the product such that the product is beneficial to plant growth.
  >   - **(3)** *Compostable material* The term “compostable material” means material that is a feedstock for creating compost, including—
  >     - **(A)** wood,
  >     - **(B)** agricultural crops,
  >     - **(C)** paper,
  >     - **(D)** compostable products associated with organic waste,
  >     - **(E)** organic plant material,
  >     - **(F)** marine products,
  >     - **(G)** organic waste, including food waste and yard waste, and
  >     - **(H)** other renewable or replenishable material that is composed of biomass.

- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2026.
