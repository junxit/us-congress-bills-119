# H.R. 5179

> To amend the District of Columbia Home Rule Act to provide for the appointment of the Attorney General for the District of Columbia by the President, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “District of Columbia Attorney General Appointment Reform Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Appointment of Attorney General for District of Columbia by President

- **(a)** *Appointment* Part C–i of title IV of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (sec. 1–204.35, D.C. Official Code) is amended to read as follows:
- **(b)** *Termination of service of current Attorney General* The term of service of the individual serving as the Attorney General for the District of Columbia on the day before the date of the enactment of this Act shall terminate on the date of the enactment of this Act.
