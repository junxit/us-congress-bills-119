# H.R. 10155

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to include certain prior active duty as a regular member of an Armed Force for reduced eligibility age for retirement as a member of the reserve components.

## § 1. Inclusion of certain prior active duty as a regular member of an Armed Force for reduced eligibility age for retirement as a member of the reserve components

- **(a)** *In general* Section 12731(f)(2) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “as a member of the Ready Reserve”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by adding at the end the following new clause:
    > - **(v)** Service on active duty described in this subparagraph is also service on active duty as a regular member.

- **(b)** *Retroactive applicability* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply to qualifying service on active duty performed after January 28, 2008.
