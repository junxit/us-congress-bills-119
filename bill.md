# S. 5550

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to eliminate the aggregate limitation on rollovers from a 529 program to a Roth IRA.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “529 Retirement Enhancement Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Elimination of aggregate limitation on 529 rollovers to Roth IRA

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (ii) of section 529(c)(3)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking subclause (II), and
  - **(2)** by striking “Limitations.—” and all that follows through “Clause (i) shall only apply” and inserting “Annual limitation.—Clause (i) shall only apply”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to distributions made in taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
