# H.R. 10898

> To direct the President’s Pay Agent to establish pay localities for the Orlando metropolitan area and the Tampa metropolitan area.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Locality Pay Adjustment Act”.

## § 2. Establishment of pay localities for the Orlando and Tampa metropolitan areas

Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the pay agent (as that term is defined in section 5302 of title 5, United States Code) shall—

- **(1)** conduct a study regarding establishing pay localities for the Orlando metropolitan area and the Tampa metropolitan area;
- **(2)** submit to Congress a report on the results of such study; and
- **(3)** establish pay localities for the Orlando metropolitan area and the Tampa metropolitan area.
