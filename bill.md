# H.R. 11007

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to prohibit certain conflicts of interest in the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Standards, Transparency, Accountability, Notification, and Disclosure for Vets Act” or the “STAND for Vets Act”.

## § 2. Prohibitions of certain conflicts of interest in the Department of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *Prohibition of conflicts of interest in agreements with the Secretary of Veterans Affairs* Section 513 of title 38, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by inserting “(a)” before the first sentence; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subsection:
    > - **(b)**
    >   - **(1)** An official or employee of the Department may not participate in any contract or agreement under subsection (a) between the Secretary and a private entity if such official or employee—
    >     - **(A)** is, or has ever been, employed (including as an independent contractor) by the private entity;
    >     - **(B)** serves, or has ever served, on a board of the private entity;
    >     - **(C)** is, or has ever been, an owner or fiduciary of the private entity; or
    >     - **(D)** receives, or has received, compensation or a benefit from the private entity.
    >   - **(2)**
    >     - **(A)** If the Secretary enters, or seeks to enter, into a contract or agreement with a private entity, the duties of an official or employee of the Department would require the official or employee to participate in such contract, and the official or employee may not so participate pursuant to paragraph (1), the official or employee shall submit to the Secretary a written disclosure of the relationship of the official or employee to the private entity not later than three business days after learning of such contract or agreement.
    >     - **(B)** The Secretary shall submit to the Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and Senate a copy of a disclosure received by the Secretary pursuant to subparagraph (A) not later than three business days after receiving such disclosure.
    >   - **(3)** In this subsection, the term “participate”, with regards to a contract or agreement, includes—
    >     - **(A)** requesting a proposal, quote, or information;
    >     - **(B)** negotiation; and
    >     - **(C)** performance.

- **(b)** *Prohibition of certain meetings with officials of the Department of Veterans Affairs* Subchapter I of chapter 7 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting after section 711 the following new section (and amending the table of sections at the beginning of such chapter accordingly):
  > ## § 712. Prohibition of certain meetings of Department officials with candidates for Federal office
  >
  > - **(a)** *Prohibition* An official of the Department may not meet with an individual who is a candidate in an election during the period of 180 days preceding such election or on the day of such election.
  > - **(b)** *Limitations* Subsection (a) shall not apply—
  >   - **(1)** if the Secretary submits to the Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and Senate written notification of the meeting at least seven days before such meeting; or
  >   - **(2)** to official duty under section 715 of this title.
  > - **(c)** *Definitions* In this section, the terms “candidate” and “election” have the meanings given such terms in section 301 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (Public Law 92–225; 52 U.S.C. 30101).

- **(c)** *GAO review of VA OIG*
  - **(1)** *Review* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Comptroller General of the United States shall begin a review of the Office of the Inspector General of the Department of Veterans Affairs.
  - **(2)** *Report* Not later than one year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Comptroller General shall submit to the Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and Senate a report regarding the review under paragraph (1). Such report shall include the following elements:
    - **(A)** The staffing levels of the Office of Audits and Evaluations and of the Office of Investigations of the Office of Inspector General.
    - **(B)** The staffing models used for the offices specified in subparagraph (A).
    - **(C)** Whether the Inspector General uses recruitment, retention, and relocation incentives for such offices, including bonuses, awards, and incentives under section 706 of title 38, United States Code.
    - **(D)** The assessment of the Comptroller General whether such levels, models, and incentives adequately equip the Office of the Inspector General to carry out its mission.
