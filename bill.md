# H.R. 1133

> To amend the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 to abolish certain grants for community development, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repeal Community Development Block Grants Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Abolition of grants

- **(a)** *Abolition of grants* Section 101 and sections 103 through 122 of title I of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 5301 et seq.) are repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall take effect on October 1, 2025.
