# H.R. 10127

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to conduct, support, or fund research that studies the effects of gender-related medical treatment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Terminating Radical Attempts to Negate Sex Through Government Research Act” or the “TRANS Government Research Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on availability of Federal funds for certain research

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no Federal funds may be made available to directly or indirectly conduct, support, or fund covered research.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Covered research* The term “covered research” means research that studies the effects of—
    - **(A)** gender-related medical treatment; or
    - **(B)** any other means of altering the body or physical appearance of a person to no longer correspond to the sex of such person, including by—
      - **(i)** disrupting the natural development of the body of such person;
      - **(ii)** inhibiting the natural functions of the body of such person; and
      - **(iii)** modifying the physical appearance or attire of such person.
  - **(2)** *Female* The term “female” means an individual who, but for a developmental or genetic anomaly or historical accident, naturally has, had, will have, or would have a reproductive system that produces, transports, and utilizes eggs for fertilization.
  - **(3)** *Gender-related medical treatment* The term “gender-related medical treatment” means—
    - **(A)** with respect to an individual who is female, medical treatment provided for the purpose of addressing the perception of such individual that such individual is not female, including—
      - **(i)** a surgical procedure, including—
        - **(I)** a vaginectomy;
        - **(II)** a hysterectomy;
        - **(III)** an oophorectomy;
        - **(IV)** the reconstruction of the urethra;
        - **(V)** a metoidioplasty;
        - **(VI)** a phalloplasty;
        - **(VII)** a salpingo-oophorectomy;
        - **(VIII)** a scrotoplasty;
        - **(IX)** the implantation of erection or testicular protheses;
        - **(X)** a subcutaneous mastectomy;
        - **(XI)** vocal cord surgery;
        - **(XII)** pectoral implantation; and
        - **(XIII)** penile transplantation;
      - **(ii)** an exogenous dose of testosterone or other androgen; and
      - **(iii)** a puberty blocker, including—
        - **(I)** a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonist; and
        - **(II)** a synthetic drug that—
          - **(aa)** suppresses the production of estrogen and progesterone; or
          - **(bb)** delays or suppresses pubertal development; and
    - **(B)** with respect to an individual who is male, medical treatment provided for the purpose of addressing the perception of such individual that such individual is not male, including—
      - **(i)** a surgical procedure, including—
        - **(I)** a penectomy;
        - **(II)** an orchiectomy;
        - **(III)** a vaginoplasty;
        - **(IV)** a clitoroplasty;
        - **(V)** a vulvoplasty;
        - **(VI)** an augmentation mammoplasty;
        - **(VII)** facial feminization surgery;
        - **(VIII)** vocal cord surgery;
        - **(IX)** a chondrolaryngoplasty;
        - **(X)** gluteal augmentation; and
        - **(XI)** uterine transplantation;
      - **(ii)** an exogenous dose of estrogen; and
      - **(iii)** a puberty blocker, including—
        - **(I)** a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonist; and
        - **(II)** a synthetic drug that—
          - **(aa)** suppresses the production of testosterone; or
          - **(bb)** delays or suppresses pubertal development.
  - **(4)** *Male* The term “male” means an individual who, but for a developmental or genetic anomaly or historical accident, naturally has, had, will have, or would have a reproductive system that produces, transports, and utilizes sperm for fertilization.
  - **(5)** *Sex* The term “sex” means the biological determination with respect to whether an individual is male or female.
