# H.R. 3802

> To codify Executive Order 14190 (relating to ending radical indoctrination in K–12 schooling).

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “EO 14190 Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Codification of Executive Order 14190, Ending Radical Indoctrination in K–12 Schooling

Executive Order 14190 (90 Fed. Reg. 8853; Ending Radical Indoctrination in K–12 Schooling) shall have the force and effect of law.
