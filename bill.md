# H.R. 3676

> To codify Executive Order 14293, relating to regulatory relief to promote domestic production of critical medicines.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Executive Order 14293 Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Codification of Executive Order 14293

Executive Order 14293 (90 Fed. Reg. 19615; relating to regulatory relief to promote domestic production of critical medicines) shall have the force and effect of law.
