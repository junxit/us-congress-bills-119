# H.R. 10735

> To amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act to require certain types of cattle to be included in a livestock pilot program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Coverage of certain cattle under livestock pilot program

Section 523(b)(1) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1523(b)) is amended by inserting after “cattle” the following: “(including, beginning with crop year 2027, bred cattle, replacement heifers, and cull cattle)”.
