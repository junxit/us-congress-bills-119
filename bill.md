# H. RES. 1654

> Congratulating the Milwaukee Brewers, and the fans of the Milwaukee Brewers, on winning Major League Baseball’s 2026 World Championship Series.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on October 28, 2026, the Milwaukee Brewers (referred to in this preamble as the “Brewers”) won Major League Baseball’s 2026 World Series, defeating the Tampa Bay Rays by winning the Series 4 games to 1;
- Whereas, during this unprecedented run to the Championship, the Brewers won 11 of 13 playoff games and came from behind to win 6 times;
- Whereas the Milwaukee Brewers are a professional baseball team with a rich history that was founded in 1969 as the “Seattle Pilots” before moving to Milwaukee and becoming the Milwaukee Brewers in 1970;
- Whereas the Brewers win— (1)marked the first World Series in the history of the current franchise during the second trip to the World Series in franchise history; and (2)marked the first World Series to come to Milwaukee since the then-Milwaukee Braves won the Championship in 1957;
- Whereas, during the regular season, the Brewers won 103 of the 162 games, the best record in baseball and finishing first in the National League’s Central Division, 14 games ahead of the second-place team in their division;
- Whereas that regular season record earned them home field advantage throughout the Major League Baseball Playoffs and a bye in the first round;
- Whereas the Brewers won the National League Division Series against the San Diego Padres 3 games to 1;
- Whereas the Brewers won the National League Championship Series by sweeping the Los Angeles Dodgers, the defending World Series champions and the team with the highest payroll in Major League Baseball;
- Whereas Brewers first baseman, Jake Bauers, was named the Most Valuable Player of the National League Championship Series for his timely hitting, including helping the “Brew Crew” overcome a 4-run deficit in the clinching Game 4 of that Series;
- Whereas, during the 2026 World Series, the Brewers defeated the Tampa Bay Rays, 4 games to 1;
- Whereas, in the Championship-clinching Game 5 of the World Series, the Brewers prevailed behind the superb pitching of ace pitcher, Jacob Misiorowski, who shut down the Tampa Bay Rays, allowing 2 hits through 52/3 innings and a dominant bullpen and hitting up and down the lineup;
- Whereas Brewers catcher, William Contreras, was named World Series Most Valuable Player in recognition of his deft handling of a superb pitching staff and for his exploits at the plate and behind the plate during the World Series and throughout the playoffs;
- Whereas, during the World Series, William Contreras— (1)went 8 for 21 (.381) and drove in 4 runs, including having multiple hits in each of the first 3 games; (2)went 10 for 14 in Automated Ball-Strike challenges behind the plate; and (3)threw out 4 of 9 baserunners attempting to steal a base; and
- Whereas every member of the Brewers played a key role in the quest for a championship, including Christian Yelich, the first ever team captain in Milwaukee Brewers franchise history: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** congratulates the Milwaukee Brewers, and the loyal fans of the Brewers, on winning the 2026 World Series;
- **(2)** recognizes the historic season and achievement of the entire Brewers organization, including the players, coaches, staff, and front office; and
- **(3)** directs the Clerk of the House of Representatives to transmit an enrolled copy of this resolution to—
  - **(A)** the Manager of the Brewers, Patrick Thomas Murphy, the National League Manager of the Year in 2024 and 2025, and the Milwaukee Brewers franchise; and
  - **(B)** the mayor of the City of Milwaukee, the County Executive of Milwaukee County, the Milwaukee Common Council, and the Milwaukee County Board of Supervisors.
