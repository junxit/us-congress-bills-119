# S. 3331

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 50 East 100 North in Moab, Utah, as the “2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams Post Office”.

## § 1. 2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 50 East 100 North in Moab, Utah, shall be known and designated as the “2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams Post Office”.
