# S. 5492

> To reduce crime on public transportation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safe Transit Act”.

## § 3. Establishing minimum performance standards for infrastructure to prevent fare evasion

- **(a)** *Requirements* Notwithstanding section 5334(k) of title 49, United States Code, and the rulemaking procedures under section 553 of title 5, United States Code, not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary shall establish minimum performance standards for infrastructure to prevent fare evasion under the national public transportation safety plan, which shall not be revised except by law.
- **(b)** *Consideration* In establishing the minimum performance standards under subsection (a), the Secretary shall consider—
  - **(1)** different fare collection needs of different modes of public transportation;
  - **(2)** differences in operating environments;
  - **(3)** the use of technology in fare collection enforcement;
  - **(4)** existing experience, from agencies that already are using or testing infrastructure to prevent fare evasion; and
  - **(5)** the impact of the performance standards on future rolling stock procurements and vehicles currently in revenue service.
- **(c)** *Condition* In the case of a recipient for an urbanized area with a population of not fewer than 200,000 individuals, as determined by the Bureau of the Census, as a condition of financial assistance under any formula program under chapter 53 of title 49, United States Code, for fiscal year 2027, the recipient shall certify that the recipient will acquire in the following fiscal year infrastructure to prevent fare evasion meeting the minimum performance standards established under the national public transportation safety plan under subsection (a) for all stations, terminals, and rolling stock within its public transportation system.
- **(d)** *Savings clause* Nothing in this section may be construed as prohibiting the Secretary of Transportation from issuing different comprehensive standards for infrastructure to prevent fare evasion.

## § 4. Family friendly transit scorecard

- **(a)** *Rail station or bus terminal defined* In this section, the term “rail station or bus terminal” means—
  - **(1)** a station facility where a public transportation operator has physically posted, as of the date of enactment of this Act, a system map, code of conduct, or arrivals and departures board; and
  - **(2)** a new station facility, or an improvement to an existing facility, where a public transportation operator posts a system map, code of conduct, or arrivals and departures board.
- **(b)** *Requirements* The Secretary of Transportation shall establish a methodology to aggregate data submitted to Federal Transit Administration under section 5329(m)(2) of title 49, United States Code, related to the performance targets set under subsection (d)(1)(K) of section 5329 of such title, in the form of an annual Family Friendly Transit Scorecard, which assigns a letter grade for each public transportation system.
- **(c)** *Condition* In the case of a recipient for an urbanized area with a population of not fewer than 200,000 individuals, as determined by the Bureau of the Census, as a condition of financial assistance under any formula program under chapter 53 of title 49, the recipient shall certify that the recipient has prominently displayed at each rail station and bus terminal within the recipient’s public transportation system and published on the recipient’s website—
  - **(1)** the letter grade for the Family Friendly Transit Scorecard; and
  - **(2)** the image of any person captured by security camera in the act of committing any crime on the public transportation system, including information identifying the crime committed, not later than 7 days after the commission of the crime for a period of not less than 45 days.
- **(d)** *Waiver* The Federal Transit Administration may waive the requirement under subsection (c)(2) for any rail station or bus terminal where the recipient certifies that the recipient would be impracticable to display such information.
