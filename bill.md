# H.R. 10630

> To amend the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act to require public disclosure when certain pesticides are used on Federal land.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Pesticide Transparency on Federal Land Act”.

## § 2. Public disclosure of pesticide use on Federal land

The Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (7 U.S.C. 136 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 36. Public disclosure of pesticide use on Federal land
>
> - **(a)** *Database required* Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this section, the Administrator shall establish a publicly available database to document instances in which a pesticide is applied on Federal land by a covered agency.
> - **(b)** *Agency reporting* Beginning on the date that is one year after the date of the enactment of this section, not later than 5 days after the date on which a covered agency applies a pesticide on Federal land, the head of such covered agency shall report to the Administrator all information described in subsection (c) with respect to that application.
> - **(c)** *Contents* The database required under subsection (a) shall include, with respect to each application of a pesticide on Federal land—
>   - **(1)** the name of the entity applying the pesticide;
>   - **(2)** the location of the application of the pesticide, including the address and geographic coordinates;
>   - **(3)** the specific classification of pesticide applied, including the active ingredient;
>   - **(4)** the date of the application; and
>   - **(5)** the purpose of the application.
> - **(d)** *Public disclosure*
>   - **(1)** *In general* Except as specified in paragraph (3), information required to be disclosed under this section shall not be considered protected from disclosure under section 10(b).
>   - **(2)** *Preexisting data* Except as specified in paragraph (3), not later than 1 year after that date of the enactment of this section, the Administrator shall make any data described in subsection (c) that was compiled by a covered agency prior to the date of the enactment of this section, including pesticide use proposals, publicly available on the database established under subsection (a).
>   - **(3)** *Protection from disclosure for national security reasons*
>     - **(A)** *In general* Information disclosed to the Administrator under subsection (b) shall not be included in the database under subsection (a) (or in any other manner disseminated to the public) if the head of the covered agency applying the pesticide involved submits a notification to the Administrator that the head of such agency has determined that such disclosure would harm the national security of the United States.
>     - **(B)** *Scope and duration of protection* The protection from disclosure under paragraph (1)—
>       - **(i)** shall apply only to the information necessary to protect national security, and shall not waive the requirement to transmit information to the Administrator under subsection (b);
>       - **(ii)** shall apply only for a period of not more than 1 year; and
>       - **(iii)** may be renewed if the head of the covered agency makes a subsequent determination with respect to the application of a pesticide involved under subparagraph (A).
>     - **(C)** *Notification to Congress* Not later than 30 days after submitting a notification under subparagraph (A), the head of the covered agency involved shall submit to Congress a notification describing the general basis of the determination described in subparagraph (A), which may be submitted in classified form.
> - **(e)** *Civil action* Any person may commence a civil action against a covered agency for failure to report under this section. The amount of damages that may be awarded to a prevailing plaintiff in such an action shall be such amount as may be determined by the judge adjudicating the civil action, but in no case shall exceed—
>   - **(1)** $5,000 for each instance of a failure to report; and
>   - **(2)** $25,000 for any 3-month period during the entirety of which the covered agency fails to report pesticide usage.
> - **(f)** *Regulations* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this section, the Administrator shall issue regulations to carry out this section.
> - **(g)** *Definitions*
>   - **(1)** *Covered agency* The term covered agency means each department, agency, or instrumentality of the United States (including the executive, legislative, and judicial branches of government).
>   - **(2)** *Federal land* The term Federal land means any land owned or managed by a covered agency.
