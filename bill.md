# H.R. 10385

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to increase the amount of funds authorized for the Secretary of the Army to carry out wastewater infrastructure projects in Village of Kelleys Island, Ohio, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Toledo and Oregon, Ohio, water and wastewater infrastructure modification

Section 219(f)(558) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 335; 138 Stat. 3125) is amended by striking “$1,000,000” and inserting “$16,500,000”.
