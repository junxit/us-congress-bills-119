# H.R. 10938

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase the maximum amount of a home health service benefit available to certain veterans under the laws administered by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Assistance for Better Living Environments for Veterans Act” or the “ABLE Veterans Act”.

## § 2. Increase to maximum threshold for certain Department of Veterans Affairs home health service benefit

Section 1717(a)(2)(A)(ii) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “$6,800” and inserting “$15,000”.
