# S. 5177

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to include two counties in New Mexico in a certain Veterans Integrated Service Network.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “New Mexico Rural Veteran Health Care Access Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of two counties in New Mexico in a certain Veterans Integrated Service Network

Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall redraw the boundaries of the Veterans Integrated Service Networks to include Otero County and Eddy County, New Mexico, in Veterans Integrated Service Network 17.
