# H.R. 83

> To amend part A of title I of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to allow States, in accordance with State law, to let Federal funds for the education of disadvantaged children follow low-income children to the public school, charter school, accredited private school, or supplemental educational service program they attend, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ending Common Core and Expanding School Choice Act”.

## § 2. State educational agency grants to eligible children

- **(a)** *In general* Part A of title I of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 6311 et seq.) is amended to read as follows:
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments*
  - **(1)** *Repeal of State assessment grants* Part B of title I of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1201 et seq.) is repealed.
  - **(2)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 1002 of such Act (20 U.S.C. 6302) is amended—
    - **(A)** by striking the subsection heading for subsection (a) and inserting “State educational agency grants for eligible children”; and
    - **(B)** by striking subsection (b).
