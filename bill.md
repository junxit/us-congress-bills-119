# H.R. 7392

> To impose a hiring freeze on United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Hiring freeze for U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement

- **(a)** *In general* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, beginning on the date of the enactment of this section, no Federal funds may be obligated or expended to—
  - **(1)** appoint any individual to a position within United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement; or
  - **(2)** detail or transfer any Federal employee to United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
- **(b)** *Application* Subsection (a) shall cease to apply on the date following the date of the enactment of this section that Congress enacts into law a provision that provides, by specific reference to subsection (a) of this section, that such subsection is superseded, overridden, or otherwise modified.
