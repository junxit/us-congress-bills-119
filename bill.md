# S. 2273

> To amend the Act of July 10, 1890, to modify certain provisions relating to the disposal of public land in the State of Wyoming for educational purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Wyoming Education Trust Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Disposal of public land in the State of Wyoming for educational purposes

The Act of July 10, 1890 (26 Stat. 222, chapter 664), is amended—

- **(1)** in section 5, in the first sentence, by striking “interest of” and inserting “earnings on”;
- **(2)** in section 7, by striking “interest of” and inserting “earnings on”; and
- **(3)** in section 8, in the first sentence, by striking “income thereof” and inserting “earnings on which”.
