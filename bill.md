# S. 5374

> To facilitate the conveyance of certain land in Bonners Ferry, Idaho, platted under section 2387 of the Revised Statutes, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Facilitation of conveyance of certain land in Bonners Ferry, Idaho

- **(a)** *Disclaimer of interest* To facilitate the conveyance of the covered land described in subsection (b) that was platted under section 2387 of the Revised Statutes (43 U.S.C. 718) (repealed by section 703(a) of the Federal Land Policy and Management Act of 1976 (Public Law 94–579; 90 Stat. 2790)), not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Interior shall issue a recordable disclaimer of interest of the United States in and to the covered land.
- **(b)** *Description of covered land* The covered land referred to in subsection (a) is the approximately 15.12 acres of land in Bonners Ferry, Idaho, depicted as “Eaton” on the map entitled “Eaton” and dated December 22, 1893, excluding the portion of the land within the boundary of the Kootenai Indian Reservation.
