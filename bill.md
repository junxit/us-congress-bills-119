# H.R. 7809

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 East Grant Avenue in Georgetown, Ohio, as the “Ulysses S. Grant Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Ulysses S. Grant Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 East Grant Avenue in Georgetown, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “Ulysses S. Grant Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Ulysses S. Grant Post Office Building”.
