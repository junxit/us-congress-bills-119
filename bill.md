# H.R. 11084

> To provide for the establishment of a U.S.-Ethiopia Agricultural and Critical Minerals Trade Partnership.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ethiopia Stability, Partnership, Agriculture, Resources, and Key Investments Act” or “Ethiopia SPARK Act”.

## § 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to—

- **(1)** encourage Ethiopia to establish lasting peace with Eritrea by creating a formal U.S.-Ethiopia Agriculture and Critical Minerals Trade Partnership that connects American agricultural exports, equipment, and research expertise with Ethiopia’s natural resources; and
- **(2)** once Ethiopia demonstrates substantial progress toward peaceful relations with Eritrea, regional stability, and civilian protection, to pursue expanded agricultural and mineral trade opportunities with Ethiopia.

## § 3. U.S.-Ethiopia Agricultural and Critical Minerals Trade Partnership

- **(a)** *In general* Beginning on the date on which the Secretary of State determines that Ethiopia meets the requirement described in section 2(2), the Secretary of State, in coordination with the United States Trade Representatives, the Secretary of Agriculture, and the Secretary of Commerce, shall seek to establish a formal bilateral trade partnership with Ethiopia to expand United States agricultural exports and facilitate responsible critical mineral trade. The trade partnership established pursuant to this subsection may be referred to as the “U.S.-Ethiopia Agricultural and Critical Minerals Trade Partnership”.
- **(b)** *Elements* The trade partnership established pursuant to subsection (a) should—
  - **(1)** identify opportunities to reduce tariffs and regulatory barriers affecting United States agricultural exports and services described in subsection (c);
  - **(2)** encourage mutually beneficial commercial agreements involving United States agricultural technology, mining expertise, and Ethiopian mineral resources and related actions described in subsection (d); and
  - **(3)** prioritize private-sector investment and existing United States trade authorities to minimize additional United States expenditures.
- **(c)** *United States agricultural exports and services described* The United States agricultural exports and services described in this subsection are—
  - **(1)** corn, soybeans, and other agricultural commodities;
  - **(2)** farm equipment and machinery;
  - **(3)** precision agriculture technology;
  - **(4)** irrigation systems;
  - **(5)** grain storage and agricultural processing equipment;
  - **(6)** agricultural research and technical expertise;
  - **(7)** mining equipment and technology;
  - **(8)** mine safety technology;
  - **(9)** mineral processing technology; and
  - **(10)** supply-chain expertise.
- **(d)** *Ethiopian mineral resources and related actions described* The Ethiopian mineral resources and related actions described in this subsection are—
  - **(1)** gold;
  - **(2)** platinum;
  - **(3)** copper;
  - **(4)** nickel;
  - **(5)** iron ore;
  - **(6)** tantalum;
  - **(7)** niobium;
  - **(8)** potash titanium (ilmenite and rutile);
  - **(9)** expanded commercial access to Ethiopia’s mineral exploration, mining, and processing industries;
  - **(10)** reduced tariffs and regulatory barriers on United States agricultural products and equipment; and
  - **(11)** opportunities for United States private-sector investment and long-term mineral supply agreements.
