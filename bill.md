# H.R. 10224

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6 Pennsylvania Avenue in Matamoras, Pennsylvania, as the “PFC Edward Kuhn Memorial Post Office”.

## § 1. PFC Edward Kuhn Memorial Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6 Pennsylvania Avenue in Matamoras, Pennsylvania, shall be known and designated as the “PFC Edward Kuhn Memorial Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “PFC Edward Kuhn Memorial Post Office”.
