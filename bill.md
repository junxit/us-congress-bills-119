# S. 5681

> To provide for the protection of the duties of the Inspector General of the Federal Housing Finance Agency.

## § 1. Expenses of the Inspector General of the Federal Housing Finance Agency

Section 1316(a) of the Federal Housing Enterprises Financial Safety and Soundness Act of 1992 (12 U.S.C. 4516(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (3), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (4), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(5)** the expenses of the Inspector General of the Agency, as independently determined by the Inspector General.
