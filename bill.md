# H.R. 11031

> To codify the Commercial Driver’s License Fraud Tipline into law, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “CDL Fraud Tipline Act”.

## § 2. Commercial Driver’s License Fraud Tipline

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Homeland Security, in coordination with the Secretary of Transportation, shall—
  - **(1)** operate and maintain the hotline, known as the “Commercial Driver’s License Fraud Tipline”, to receive information with respect to suspected fraud related to the issuance of commercial driver’s licenses to aliens who are unlawfully present in the United States;
  - **(2)** establish an email address, submission form, or such other method as determined appropriate by the Secretary of Homeland Security to submit such information to such hotline; and
  - **(3)** establish a website providing such email address, submission form, phone number, or other method in a manner that is easily accessible.
- **(b)** *Report* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, and annually thereafter, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall submit to Congress a report on the Commercial Driver’s License Fraud Tipline that includes—
  - **(1)** the number of tips received in the previous year; and
  - **(2)** the number of fraudulently issued commercial driver’s licenses reported in total and disaggregated by State.
