# S. 5436

> To prohibit immigration enforcement actions at child care facilities.

## § 1. Restriction of immigration enforcement actions at child care facilities

No agent, officer, or employee of the Department of Homeland Security, nor any person acting under the authority of such agent, officer, or employee, may conduct any immigration enforcement action at a child care facility.
