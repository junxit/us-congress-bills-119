# H.R. 10644

> To amend the Small Business Act to, within the Small Business Administration, modify the Office of Rural Affairs and establish the Center for Faith, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Faith and Rural Small Business Act”.

## § 2. Office of Rural Affairs and Center for Faith of Small Business Administration

- **(a)** *Office of Rural Affairs* Section 26(b) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 653(b)) is amended by striking “a director” and inserting “an Assistant Administrator”.
- **(b)** *Center for Faith* The Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 631 et seq.) is amended by inserting after section 26 the following:
  > ## § 26A. Center for Faith
  >
  > - **(a)** *Establishment* There is established within the Office of Entrepreneurial Development of the Administration a Center for Faith.
  > - **(b)** *Director of the Center for Faith* The Center for Faith shall be headed by a Director who shall be appointed by the Administrator not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this section.
