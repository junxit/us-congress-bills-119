# H.R. 5666

> To provide for continued operation of the Coast Guard in the event of a lapse in appropriation.

## § 1. Continued operation of Coast Guard

In the case of a lapse in appropriations for the Coast Guard, there are appropriated such amounts as may be necessary for the operation of the Coast Guard for the lesser of—

- **(1)** 30 days; or
- **(2)** the duration of the lapse in appropriation.
