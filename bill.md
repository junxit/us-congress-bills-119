# H.R. 11105

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to prohibit the Secretary of Agriculture from approving retail food stores to accept benefits from recipients of supplemental nutrition assistance through on-line transactions for delivery orders, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Online Delivery Allowed for SNAP Households Act” or the “NO DASH Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on SNAP food delivery orders

- **(a)** *In general* Section 7(k) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2016(k)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the heading, by inserting “for pickup orders” after “transactions”;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (1), by inserting “for pickup orders” after “transactions”;
  - **(3)** in paragraph (2)—
    - **(A)** in the text preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “for pickup orders” after “transactions”;
    - **(B)** by striking “delivery,” each place it appears; and
    - **(C)** in subparagraph (C)—
      - **(i)** in clause (i), by striking “and” at the end;
      - **(ii)** in clause (ii), by inserting “and” after the semicolon; and
      - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following:
        > - **(iii)** that the food order must be picked up at the retail food store (or another location designated by the retail food store) by—
        >   - **(I)** a member of the household; or
        >   - **(II)** a caregiver to a member of the household;

  - **(4)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(5)** *Prohibition on delivery orders* The Secretary may not approve retail food stores to accept benefits from recipients of supplemental nutrition assistance through on-line transactions for delivery orders.
    > - **(6)** *Definitions* In this subsection:
    >   - **(A)** *Caregiver* The term caregiver means an individual who has the responsibility for the care of an elderly or chronically sick individual, either voluntarily, by contract, by receipt of payment for care, or as a result of the operation of law.
    >   - **(B)** *Delivery order* The term delivery order means a food order that is transported by a retail food store, third party delivery platform, or independent contractor from a retail food store to a household or a location identified by a household.

- **(b)** *Regulations* Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary shall issue regulations to carry out the amendments made by this Act.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this Act shall take effect 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act.
