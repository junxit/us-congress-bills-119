# H.R. 11119

> To direct the Administrator of the Transportation Security Administration to establish a grant program for eligible railroads to assist in financing the cost of improving the cybersecurity of freight rail transportation systems, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Freight Rail Cybersecurity Enhancement Act”.

## § 2. Rail infrastructure cybersecurity enhancement program

- **(a)** *Establishment of strategic railroad cybersecurity enhancement program*
  - **(1)** *Establishment* Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Administrator, in consultation with the Secretary of Defense and Administrator of the Federal Railroad Administration, shall establish a program to provide grants to eligible railroads to assist in financing the cost of improving the cybersecurity of freight rail transportation systems.
  - **(2)** *Allocation of grants* The Administrator shall allocate funding to eligible railroads based on predetermined criteria, as determined by the Administrator in consultation with the Secretary of Defense, including—
    - **(A)** the strategic value to national security and military readiness;
    - **(B)** the volume of hazardous or otherwise sensitive cargo; and
    - **(C)** the available alternative transportation routes for military equipment.
  - **(3)** *Application* An eligible railroad seeking a grant under paragraph (1) shall submit to the Administrator an application at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Administrator may require.
  - **(4)** *Protection of information* Information provided to, or collected by, the Administrator to carry out this subsection—
    - **(A)** shall be exempt from disclosure under section 552(b)(3) of title 5, United States Code; and
    - **(B)** shall not be made available by any Federal agency, State, political subdivision of a State, or Tribal authority pursuant to any Federal, State, political subdivision of a State, or Tribal law, respectively, requiring public disclosure of information of records.
  - **(5)** *Implementation period* The funds under paragraph (2) are payable for months beginning on or after the date that is one year after the date of enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** *Reports to congress*
  - **(1)** *Interim report to congress* Not later than 18 months after the date of enactment of this Act, the Administrator shall submit to the relevant congressional committees an interim report on the status of the Administrator’s actions taken to carry out the grant program established under subsection (a)(1), including—
    - **(A)** the justification and criteria used for the selection of each eligible railroad applicant; and
    - **(B)** any challenges encountered during the implementation of the program.
  - **(2)** *Final report to congress* Not later than 90 days after the date on which the Program terminates under subsection (d), the Administrator shall submit to relevant congressional committees a report that describes the administration of the grant program, including—
    - **(A)** the outcome and benefits that each such eligible railroad has generated measured by data, to the maximum extent practicable;
    - **(B)** the lessons learned and any recommendations relating to future cybersecurity-related programs or strategies to improve the cybersecurity posture of railroad networks across the United States;
    - **(C)** a description of lessons learned through the identification and mitigation of cybersecurity vulnerabilities;
    - **(D)** how the Administrator will incorporate any such lessons or best practices into Federal standards or guidance for the railroad networks; and
    - **(E)** recommendations for improvement of the program, including any suggested adjustments to the implementation requirements, cost-effectiveness of the grant, and any additional information the Administrator determines necessary.
- **(c)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator $50,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2026 through 2029 to carry out this section.
- **(d)** *Termination* The authority to carry out the Program shall terminate on September 30, 2029.
- **(e)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Administrator* The term “Administrator” means the Administrator of the Transportation Security Administration.
  - **(2)** *Class I railroad* The term “Class I railroad” has the meaning given the term “railroad carriers” in section 1201.1–1 of title 49, Code of Federal Regulations.
  - **(3)** *Eligible railroads* The term “eligible railroads” means all railroads subject to rail security regulations governing security-sensitive railroads identified under section 1580.1(a)(1) of title 49, Code of Federal Regulations, except railroads that are Class I railroads, and fall under at least one of the following:
    - **(A)** Operating on a line designated as Defense Connectors by the Department of Defense.
    - **(B)** Operating on a line listed as a part of the Strategic Rail Corridor Network.
    - **(C)** Serving as critical connectors between Class I freight railroads.
    - **(D)** Class II railroad or Class III railroad, including any holding company of a Class II railroad or Class III railroad.
    - **(E)** An association representing 1 or more railroads described in (D).
  - **(4)** *Relevant congressional committees* The term “relevant congressional committees” means—
    - **(A)** the congressional defense committees;
    - **(B)** the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate;
    - **(C)** the Committee on Homeland Security of the House of Representatives;
    - **(D)** the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate; and
    - **(E)** the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure of the House of Representatives.
  - **(5)** *Strategic rail corridor network* The term “Strategic Rail Corridor Network” means the railroad corridor network designated by the Department of Defense and the Federal Railroad Administration as critical for the movement of essential military equipment.
