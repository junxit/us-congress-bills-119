# S. 5418

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude data centers from the definition of qualified opportunity zone businesses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Tax Breaks for Data Centers Act”.

## § 2. Data centers excluded from definition of opportunity zone business

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1400Z–2(d)(3)(A)(iii) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “and is not a data center” after “section 144(c)(6)(B)”.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 1400Z–2(d)(2)(D)(i) of such Code is amended by striking “trade or business” and inserting “qualified opportunity zone trade or business”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to stock, capital or profits interests, and property acquired after the date of the enactment of this Act.
