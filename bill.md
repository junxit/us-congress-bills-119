# H.R. 2464

> To repeal section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repealing Outdated and Unilateral Tariff Authorities Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1338) is hereby repealed.
