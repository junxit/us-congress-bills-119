# H.R. 319

> To authorize an additional district judgeship for the district of Idaho.

## § 1. District judgeship for the district of Idaho

- **(a)** *In general* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 1 additional district judge for the district of Idaho.
