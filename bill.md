# H.R. 6188

> To amend title 36, United States Code, to designate the composition known as “Here Rests in Honored Glory” by Donald B. Miller as the national hymn of the United States.

## § 1. National hymn

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 3 of title 36, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 307. National hymn
  >
  > The composition consisting of the words and music known as “Here Rests in Honored Glory” by Donald B. Miller is designated as the national hymn of the United States.
