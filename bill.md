# S. 1364

> To exempt articles imported from Israel or Ukraine from duties imposed under the Executive Order entitled “Regulating Imports with a Reciprocal Tariff to Rectify Trade Practices that Contribute to Large and Persistent Annual United States Goods Trade Deficits”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting American Allies Act”.

## § 2. Exemption of articles from Israel and Ukraine from Executive Order imposing reciprocal tariffs

The duties imposed under the Executive Order entitled “Regulating Imports with a Reciprocal Tariff to Rectify Trade Practices that Contribute to Large and Persistent Annual United States Goods Trade Deficits” shall not apply to any article imported from—

- **(1)** Israel; or
- **(2)** Ukraine.
