# H.R. 5641

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 240 Main Street in Northport, New York, as the “Coast Guard Petty Officer 3rd Class Nate Bruckenthal Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Coast Guard Petty Officer 3rd Class Nate Bruckenthal Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 240 Main Street in Northport, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Coast Guard Petty Officer 3rd Class Nate Bruckenthal Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Coast Guard Petty Officer 3rd Class Nate Bruckenthal Post Office Building”.
