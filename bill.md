# H.R. 9584

> To amend the United States Housing Act of 1937 to require the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to reallocate unobligated funding for tenant-based assistance to public housing agencies which are in need of more funding for tenant-based assistance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “The Housing Voucher Funding Reallocation Act”.

## § 2. Reallocation of voucher funding

Section 8(o) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(24)** *Reallocation of amounts* The Secretary shall, at the end of each fiscal year—
>   - **(A)** recapture from each public housing agency any amounts provided to such public housing agency for tenant-based assistance under paragraph (1)(A), not including any administrative fees or other amounts made available under subsection (q), that such public housing agency did not obligate during such fiscal year; and
>   - **(B)** provide amounts recaptured under subparagraph (A) to public housing agencies that used all of the amounts provided to them for tenant-based assistance under paragraph (1)(A).
