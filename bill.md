# H.R. 10767

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose an excise tax on colleges and universities allowing specified individuals to participate in intercollegiate athletics.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Play Enforcement Act”.
