# H.R. 5718

> To reinstate exemptions to SNAP work-requirements for homeless individuals, veterans, and former foster youth.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SNAP Back Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Amendment to the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008

Section 6(o) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2015(o)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (F) by striking “or” at the end,
- **(2)** in subparagraph (G) by striking the period at the end, and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(H)** a homeless individual;
  > - **(I)** a veteran; or
  > - **(J)** an individual who is 24 years of age or younger and who was in foster care under the responsibility of a State on the date of attaining 18 years of age or such higher age as the State has elected under section 475(8)(B)(iii) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 675(8)(B)(iii)).
