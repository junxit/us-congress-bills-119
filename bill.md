# S.J. RES. 176

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Disclosure of Consumer Complaint Narrative Data”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to the withdrawal of the rule relating to “Disclosure of Consumer Complaint Narrative Data (80 Fed. Reg. 15572 (March 24, 2015))” (90 Fed. Reg. 20084 (May 12, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
