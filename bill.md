# H.R. 10268

> To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to abolish the restaurant meals program under the supplemental nutrition assistance program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ending Restaurant Purchases with SNAP Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Amendments

The Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 (7 U.S.C. 2011 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 3(k)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (3), by striking “private establishments that contract with the appropriate agency of the State to offer meals for such persons at concessional prices subject to section 9(h),”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (4), by striking “or by a private establishment that contracts with the appropriate State agency to perform such services at concessional prices subject to section 9(h),”; and
  - **(C)** in paragraph (9), by striking “and by private establishments that contract with the appropriate agency of the State to offer meals for such individuals at concessional prices subject to section 9(h)”;
- **(2)** in section 7(f)(2)—
  - **(A)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “(including restaurants participating in a State option restaurant program intended to serve the elderly, disabled, and homeless)”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B)(ii), by striking “, other than restaurants participating in a State option restaurant program”;
- **(3)** in section 9—
  - **(A)** by striking subsection (h); and
  - **(B)** by redesignating subsections (i) and (j) as subsections (h) and (i), respectively; and
- **(4)** in section 11(e)—
  - **(A)** by striking paragraph (25); and
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraph (26) as paragraph (25).

## § 3. Effective date

This Act and the amendments made by this Act shall take effect 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
