# H.R. 11140

> To amend the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 with respect to micro-grants for food security, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Grow Your Own Food Act”.

## § 2. Micro-grants for food security

- **(a)** *In general* Section 4206 of the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 (7 U.S.C. 7518) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1)(A)(iii)—
      - **(i)** in subclause (II), by striking “or” at the end;
      - **(ii)** in subclause (III), by adding “or” after the semicolon at the end; and
      - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following:
        > - **(IV)** a Native Hawaiian organization;

    - **(B)** by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(3)** *Native Hawaiian organization* The term Native Hawaiian organization has the meaning given the term in section 6207 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 7517), including the Office of Hawaiian Affairs.

  - **(2)** in subsection (d)(3), in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “3 percent” and inserting “6 percent”;
  - **(3)** in subsection (e)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
      - **(i)** in subparagraph (A)—
        - **(I)** in clause (i), by striking “$5,000” and inserting “$10,000”; and
        - **(II)** in clause (ii), by striking “$10,000” and inserting “$15,000”; and
      - **(ii)** in subparagraph (B)—
        - **(I)** in the first sentence, by striking “As a condition” and inserting the following:
          > - **(i)** *In general* Except as provided in clauses (ii) and (iii), as a condition

        - **(II)** in clause (i) (as so designated), in the second sentence, by striking “A State” and inserting the following:
          > - **(ii)** *Waiver* A State

        - **(III)** by adding at the end the following:
          > - **(iii)** *Exclusions* The requirement under clause (i) shall not apply to—
          >   - **(I)** an Indian tribe, tribal organization, or Tribal College or University (as those terms are defined in subsection (b)(1)(A));
          >   - **(II)** an Alaska Native-serving institution or a Native Hawaiian-serving institution (as those terms are defined in section 317(b) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1059d(b))); or
          >   - **(III)** a Native Hawaiian organization.

    - **(B)** in paragraph (2)—
      - **(i)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “or” at the end;
      - **(ii)** in subparagraph (B), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
      - **(iii)** by adding at the end the following:
        > - **(C)** is located in a community or region in that eligible State with a high degree of food insecurity resulting from a recent or highly destructive natural disaster to food systems and supply chains.

  - **(4)** in subsection (g)(1), by striking “fiscal year 2019 and” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2019 through 2026, and $30,000,000 for”.
- **(b)** *Exclusion from gross income*
  - **(1)** *In general* Part III of subchapter B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after section 139M the following new section:
    > ## § 139N. Micro-grants for food security
    >
    > - **(a)** *In general* In the case of any eligible entity (as defined in section 4206 of the Agricultural Improvement Act of 2018 (7 U.S.C. 7518)), gross income shall not include the amount of any subgrant received under such section.
    > - **(b)** *Deductions, etc., allowed* No deduction shall be denied, no tax attribute shall be reduced, and no basis increase shall be denied, by reason of the exclusion from gross income provided by subsection (a).

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for part III of subchapter B of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after the item relating to section 139M the following new item:
  - **(3)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this subsection shall apply to taxable years ending after the date of the enactment of this Act.
