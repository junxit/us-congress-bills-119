# H.R. 5723

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to identify and report instances of fraud with respect to disability benefit questionnaire forms of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fraud Reduction And Uncovering Deception in VA Disability Exams Act” or the “FRAUD in VA Disability Exams Act”.

## § 2. Identification and reporting of fraud with respect to Department of Veterans Affairs disability benefit questionnaire forms

Chapter 53 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section (and conforming the table of sections at the beginning of such chapter accordingly):

> ## § 5322. Identification and reporting of fraud in disability benefit questionnaire forms and claims
>
> - **(a)** *In general* The Secretary shall take actions to identify fraudulent activity in disability benefit questionnaire forms of the Department submitted to the Secretary, without regard to the source of such disability benefit questionnaire forms. Such actions shall include—
>   - **(1)** implementing a process through which an individual responsible for processing claims for benefits under laws administered by the Secretary—
>     - **(A)** may identify fraudulent activity with respect to such a disability benefit questionnaire form; and
>     - **(B)** shall report, to the Office of Inspector General of the Department, suspected fraudulent activity identified pursuant to such process;
>   - **(2)** directing the appropriate entity within the Veterans Benefits Administration to routinely audit such disability benefit questionnaire forms; and
>   - **(3)** informing the claimant if information contained in such disability benefit questionnaire form or claim is insufficient to assign a rating for such disability or make a determination regarding such claim.
> - **(b)** *Annual report* On an annual basis, the Secretary shall submit to the Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the Senate and House of Representatives a report on the administration this section.

## § 3. Authorization of certain actions in an undercover investigation by the Inspector General of the Department Of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *Authorization* Section 312 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(e)**
  >   - **(1)** With respect to an undercover investigation by the Office of Inspector General that the Inspector General determines is necessary for the detection and prosecution of crimes against the United States, the Inspector General may, notwithstanding any other provision of law (including section 648 of title 18 or section 3302 of title 31), deposit sums appropriated for the Office of Inspector General or the proceeds from the undercover investigation—
  >     - **(A)** in accounts established by the Inspector General of the Department for such undercover investigation; or
  >     - **(B)** in a financial institution.
  >   - **(2)** With regards to an undercover investigation described in paragraph (1), the Secretary—
  >     - **(A)** may, notwithstanding any other provision of law (including section 648 of title 18 or section 3302 of title 31) deposit, into accounts established by the Inspector General of the Department for undercover investigations, funds available to the Secretary; and
  >     - **(B)** shall, as soon as the Inspector General determines such proceeds are no longer necessary for the conduct of the undercover investigation, deposit the balance of such proceeds into the Treasury of the United States as miscellaneous receipts.
  >   - **(3)** At the end of each calendar quarter, the Assistant Inspector General for Investigations shall conduct detailed financial audits of undercover investigations closed during such quarter and submit the results of such audits to Inspector General.
  >   - **(4)** Not later than November 30 of each calendar year, the Inspector General shall submit to the appropriate congressional committees a report regarding undercover investigative investigations in the course of which the Inspector General exercised authority established by this subsection. Each such report shall include the following elements:
  >     - **(A)** The number of undercover investigations commenced in the fiscal year that ended most recently before the date of such report.
  >     - **(B)** The number of undercover investigations closed during such fiscal year.
  >     - **(C)** The results of such undercover investigations during such fiscal year, including the outcome of criminal proceedings.
  >     - **(D)** The number of undercover investigations pending as of September 30 of such calendar year.
  >     - **(E)** With regards to an undercover investigation in which gross receipts exceeded $50,000, the results of the related audit under paragraph (3).
  >   - **(5)**
  >     - **(A)** The Inspector General may not establish a new account under paragraph (1) after the day that is three years after the date of the enactment of the FRAUD in VA Disability Exams Act except in the course of an undercover investigation started before such day.
  >     - **(B)** The Inspector General may continue, after such day, to use such an account established before such day in the course of an undercover investigation started before such day.
  >     - **(C)** if the Inspector General establishes an account pursuant to subparagraph (A) after such day, the Inspector General shall notify the appropriate congressional committees. Such notification shall include the scope, purpose, and anticipated conclusion of the relevant undercover investigation.
  >   - **(6)** In this subsection, the term “appropriate congressional committee” means the following:
  >     - **(A)** The Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives.
  >     - **(B)** The Committees on Veterans’ Affairs of the Senate.
  >     - **(C)** The Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives.
  >     - **(D)** The Committee on Appropriations of the Senate.
  >     - **(E)** The Committee on Oversight and Reform of the House of Representatives.
  >     - **(F)** The Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate.

- **(b)** *Implementation* The amendment made by this section shall take effect on the day that is 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.
