# S. 5691

> To authorize certain death-related commemorative uses of the Peace Corps seal, emblem, logo, and name.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Memorializing Peace Corps Service Act”.

## § 2. Use of official seal, emblem, logo, and name of the Peace Corps

Section 19 of the Peace Corps Act (22 U.S.C. 2518) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “or emblem” and inserting “, emblem, or logo”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “he” and inserting “the President”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (b)—
  - **(A)** paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by striking “The use” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (3), the use”; and
    - **(ii)** by striking “or emblem” and inserting “, emblem, or logo”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking “Whoever” and inserting “Except as provided in paragraph (3), whoever”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(3)** Notwithstanding paragraphs (1) and (2), the official seal, emblem, logo, and name of the Peace Corps may be used for death-related commemorative uses on any death announcement, gravestone, plaque, or other grave marker of any person who served as a volunteer or as an officer or employee of the Peace Corps, in accordance with such rules as may be prescribed by the Director.
