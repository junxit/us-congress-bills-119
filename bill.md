# S. 5608

> To extend the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secure Rural Schools Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Extension of Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000

- **(a)** *Secure payments for States and counties containing Federal land*
  - **(1)** *Secure payments* Section 101 of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7111) is amended, in subsections (a) and (b), by striking “2026” each place it appears and inserting “2029”.
  - **(2)** *Distribution of payments to eligible counties* Section 103(d)(2) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7113(d)(2)) is amended by striking “2026” and inserting “2029”.
- **(b)** *Extension of authority To conduct special projects on Federal land*
  - **(1)** *Committee composition waiver authority* Section 205(d)(6)(C) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7125(d)(6)(C)) is amended by striking “2026” and inserting “2029”.
  - **(2)** *Extension of authority* Section 208 of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7128) is amended—
    - **(A)** in subsection (a), by striking “2028” and inserting “2031”; and
    - **(B)** in subsection (b), by striking “2029” and inserting “2032”.
- **(c)** *Extension of authority To expend county funds* Section 305 of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7144) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a), by striking “2028” and inserting “2031”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (b), by striking “2029” and inserting “2032”.

## § 3. Resource advisory committee pilot program extension

Section 205(g)(5) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7125(g)(5)) is amended by striking “2026” and inserting “2029”.

## § 4. Technical corrections

- **(a)** *Definition* Section 3(2)(A)(ii) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7102(2)(A)(ii)) is amended by inserting “of” before “acres”.
- **(b)** *Submission of project proposals* Section 203(b)(6)(A) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7123(b)(6)(A)) is amended by striking “the project,” and inserting “project”.
- **(c)** *Resource advisory committees* Section 205(a)(3) of the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act of 2000 (16 U.S.C. 7125(a)(3)) is amended by striking “may,” and inserting “may”.
