# S. RES. 866

> Supporting the designation of October 2026 as “Substance Use & Misuse Prevention Month” to raise awareness of substance use and misuse in the United States.

## Preamble

- Whereas initiation of substance use during adolescence is associated with substance use and misuse in adulthood;
- Whereas, in 2025, an estimated 56,800,000 people in the United States aged 12 and older participated in binge drinking;
- Whereas, in 2025, more than 69,800,000 or 1 in 4 people in the United States aged 12 and older used an illicit drug, including cocaine and methamphetamine;
- Whereas, in 2025, an estimated 44,600,000 people in the United States aged 12 and older had a substance use disorder;
- Whereas, in 2025, an estimated 26,000,000 people in the United States aged 12 and older had an illicit drug use disorder;
- Whereas, in 2025, an estimated 7,600,000 people in the United States aged 12 and older received substance use disorder treatment; and
- Whereas, in 2025, more than 22,300,000 people in the United States aged 18 and older were in recovery from a substance use problem: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate supports—

- **(1)** effective programs to prevent substance use and misuse;
- **(2)** programs to help stem the drug addiction and overdose epidemic in the United States; and
- **(3)** the designation of October 2026 as “Substance Use & Misuse Prevention Month”.
