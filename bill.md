# H.R. 815

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend expensing of environmental remediation costs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Brownfields Redevelopment Tax Incentive Reauthorization Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Extension of expensing of environmental remediation costs

- **(a)** *In general* Section 198(h) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “incurred after December 31, 2011” and inserting the following:
  > incurred—
  > - **(1)** after December 31, 2011, and before January 1, 2025, or
  > - **(2)** after December 31, 2028.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to expenditures paid or incurred after December 31, 2024.
