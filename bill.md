# H.R. 11132

> To authorize capital punishment for murder more consistently, to provide sound capital sentencing procedures, to enable the execution of Federal capital sentences, to avoid unjustified delay in the execution of capital sentences, to accord finality to State capital judgments subject to United States Supreme Court review, and for other purposes.
