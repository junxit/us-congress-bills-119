# H.R. 3172

> To amend section 287 of the Immigration and Nationality Act with respect to certain uniform requirements for United States immigration officers.

## § 1. Uniform requirements for United States immigration officers

Section 287 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1357) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(h)**
>   - **(1)** The Secretary of Homeland Security shall require covered immigration officers to identify themselves as such officers by displaying bold and visible identification during a time of action.
>   - **(2)** For the purposes of this subsection—
>     - **(A)** the term “bold and visible identification” means—
>       - **(i)** identification of the agency that the covered immigration officer represents no smaller than 12 inches by 6 inches on the front or back of the uniform of such officer; and
>       - **(ii)** the identification described under clause (i) is not covered by external pieces of a uniform, including external armor or accessories;
>     - **(B)** the term “covered immigration officer” means—
>       - **(i)** personnel of the U.S. Customs and Border Protection;
>       - **(ii)** personnel of the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement; and
>       - **(iii)** any other official deputized by the Secretary of Homeland Security to engage in immigration enforcement actions.
>     - **(C)** the term “time of action” includes any immigration enforcement action such as a patrol, raid, pickup, or serving a warrant.
