# S.J. RES. 210

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of Decision”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to “California State Nonroad Engine Pollution Control Standards; Commercial Harbor Craft Regulations; Notice of Decision” (90 Fed. Reg. 1998 (January 10, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
