# H.R. 10422

> To prohibit the export of diesel fuel from the United States during certain periods, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Diesel Price Reduction Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Prohibition on export of diesel fuel during certain periods

- **(a)** *Prohibition* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Commerce shall prohibit the export of diesel fuel to any country during any period described in subsection (b).
- **(b)** *Period described* The period described in this subsection is the period that—
  - **(1)** begins on the date that is the fourteenth consecutive day on which the average retail price of diesel fuel in the United States is greater than $5 per gallon; and
  - **(2)** ends on the first day after the date described in paragraph (1) that is the thirtieth consecutive day on which such price is less than $4.50 per gallon.
