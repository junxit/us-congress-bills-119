# H.R. 229

> To prohibit the implementation of the Rock Springs Field Office Record of Decision and Approved Resource Management Plan.

## § 1. Prohibition on implementation

The Secretary of the Interior shall not implement, administer, or enforce the Rock Springs Field Office Record of Decision and Approved Resource Management Plan, published by the Bureau of Land Management and dated December 2024.
