# H.R. 379

> To provide that the rule relating to “Short-Term, Limited-Duration Insurance and Independent, Noncoordinated Excepted Benefits Coverage” (89 Fed. Reg. 23338 (April 3, 2024)) shall have no force or effect.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Healthcare Freedom and Choice Act”.

## § 2. Short-term, limited duration insurance rule nullification

The final interagency rule published by the Internal Revenue Service, the Employee Benefits Security Administration, and the Health and Human Services Department relating to “Short-Term, Limited-Duration Insurance and Independent, Noncoordinated Excepted Benefits Coverage” (89 Fed. Reg. 23338 (April 3, 2024)) shall have no force or effect.
