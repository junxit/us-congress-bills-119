# H.R. 4751

> To amend the Virginia Graeme Baker Pool and Spa Safety Act to reauthorize certain programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Reauthorization of certain programs

- **(a)** *Swimming pool safety grant program* Section 1405(e) of the Virginia Graeme Baker Pool and Spa Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 8004(e)) is amended by striking “for fiscal year 2023” and inserting “for each of fiscal years 2025 through 2027”.
- **(b)** *Education and awareness program* Section 1407(b) of the Virginia Graeme Baker Pool and Spa Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 8006(b)) is amended by striking “for fiscal year 2023” and inserting “for each of fiscal years 2025 through 2027”.
