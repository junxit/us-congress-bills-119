# H.R. 10319

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide for requirements sensitive Government positions relating to gender dysphoria, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Gender Delusion Disqualification Act”.

## § 2. Updating standards surrounding Federal hiring considerations relating to gender dysphoria

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 73 of title 5, United States Code, is amended by inserting at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Clerical amendment* The table of contents for such chapter is amended by inserting at the end the following:
- **(c)** *Rulemaking* Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the head of each executive agency shall issue such regulations as are necessary to carry out section 7381 of title 5, United States Code, as added by subsection (b) of this Act, to covered positions, and positions requiring covered certification, as defined in such section, within the agencies jurisdiction.
- **(d)** *Rule of construction* Nothing in this Act or the amendments made by this Act shall be construed to impose additional standards on executive branch employees who are not in a covered position.
