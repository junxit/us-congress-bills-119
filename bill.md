# H.R. 10779

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1905 Route 112 in Medford, New York, as the “Gunnery Sergeant Ruben Santos Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Gunnery Sergeant Ruben Santos Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1905 Route 112 in Medford, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Gunnery Sergeant Ruben Santos Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Gunnery Sergeant Ruben Santos Post Office Building”.
