# H.R. 10971

> To amend title 46, United States Code, to apply an exemption to certain floating dry docks in Ingleside, Texas.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Gulf Maritime Restoration Act”.

## § 2. Floating dry dock

Section 55122 of title 46, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (c) as subsection (d); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (b) the following new subsection:
  > - **(c)** *Texas dry dock* Section 55102 does not apply to the movement of a floating dry dock from a shipyard located in Ingleside, Texas, within 5 nautical miles of such shipyard when such floating dry dock is used to launch or raise a vessel in connection with the construction, maintenance, or repair of such vessel.
