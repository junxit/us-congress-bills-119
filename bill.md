# S. 5407

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to include roundabouts as eligible projects under the surface transportation block grant program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Visionary Intersections Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Surface transportation block grant program

Section 133(b) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(25)** Construction of roundabouts.
