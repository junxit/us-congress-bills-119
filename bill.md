# H.R. 4256

> To reauthorize the Digital Coast Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Digital Coast Reauthorization Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Digital Coast Act

Section 4 of the Digital Coast Act (16 U.S.C. 1467) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)(1), by inserting “and fully and freely available” after “readily accessible”;
- **(2)** in subsection (d)(3)(D), by inserting “, including data related to underground infrastructure and subsurface utilities” after “data”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (g), by striking “2025” and inserting “2030”.
