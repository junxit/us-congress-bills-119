# H.R. 3656

> To amend the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 to authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to waive the matching funds requirement under the specialty crop research initiative, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Matching funds waiver under specialty crop research initiative

Section 412(g)(3) of the Agricultural Research, Extension, and Education Reform Act of 1998 (7 U.S.C. 7632(g)(3)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “An entity” and inserting “Subject to subparagraph (C), an entity”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(C)** *Waiver* With respect to a grant awarded under this section on or after the date of enactment of this subparagraph, the Secretary may waive the matching funds requirement under subparagraph (A).
