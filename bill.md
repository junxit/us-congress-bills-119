# H.R. 3488

> To require the Administrator of the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration to apply the final rule relating to valve installation and minimum rupture detection standards to Type A gas gathering lines, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Valve Safety Fairness Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Application of rule to Type A gas gathering lines

The Administrator of the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration shall take such actions as are necessary to apply the final rule of the Administration, titled “Pipeline Safety: Requirement of Valve Installation and Minimum Rupture Detection Standards” and published on April 8, 2022 (87 Fed. Reg. 20940), to Type A gas gathering lines.
