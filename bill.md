# H.R. 10635

> To include high hazard potential dams and rural earthen dams as eligible for rehabilitation assistance under the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Saving Rural Reservoirs Act”.

## § 2. Rehabilitation of structural measures near, at, or past their evaluated life expectancy

Section 14(a) of the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act (16 U.S.C. 1012(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “a covered dam or” before “a work of improvement”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(4)** *Covered dam* The term “covered dam” means—
  >   - **(A)** an eligible high hazard potential dam (as such term is defined in section 2 of the National Dam Safety Program Act (33 U.S.C. 467)); or
  >   - **(B)** any dam—
  >     - **(i)** located in a town with a population of less than 5,000 people; and
  >     - **(ii)** for which more than 50 percent of the total volume of the embankment of the dam is formed of compacted earth layers.
