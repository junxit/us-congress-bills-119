# H.J. RES. 72

> Relating to a national emergency by the President on February 1, 2025.

That pursuant to section 202 of the National Emergencies Act (50 U.S.C. 1622), the national emergency declared by the finding of the President on February 1, 2025, in Executive Order 14193 (25 Fed. Reg. 02406), is hereby terminated.
