# H.R. 11122

> To direct the Secretary of Energy to develop and maintain a website containing information regarding data centers and LEED certifications, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Department of Energy website on data centers and LEED certifications

- **(a)** *Agreement with U.S. Green Building Council* Not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary shall seek to enter into an agreement with the U.S. Green Building Council under which the U.S. Green Building Council shall provide to the Secretary, on an ongoing basis for the duration of the agreement—
  - **(1)** an identification of any data center that has in effect a LEED Green Building Rating System certification; and
  - **(2)** information regarding the methodology underlying the process for issuing such certifications to data centers.
- **(b)** *Department of Energy website required* Beginning not later than 18 months after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary shall develop and maintain a publicly accessible website of the Department that displays the following information:
  - **(1)** An identification of each data center located in the United States, set forth in an interactive map format.
  - **(2)** If the Secretary has entered into an agreement with the U.S. Green Building Council pursuant to subsection (a), with respect to each data center identified pursuant to paragraph (1), an identification of—
    - **(A)** whether the data center has in effect a LEED Green Building Rating System certification; and
    - **(B)** the level of such certification.
- **(c)** *Definitions* In this Act:
  - **(1)** *Data center* The term “data center” means a facility that—
    - **(A)** houses information technology equipment, including servers and other high-performance computing devices, or data storage devices; and
    - **(B)** hosts information and information systems accessed by other systems or by users on other devices.
  - **(2)** *Department* The term “Department” means the Department of Energy.
  - **(3)** *LEED Green Building Rating System* The term “LEED Green Building Rating System” means the most recent version of the Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design green building rating system for Building Design and Construction or Operations and Maintenance, as developed by the U.S. Green Building Council.
  - **(4)** *Secretary* The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
