# H.R. 10743

> To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to ensure grants under the Nonprofit Security Grant Program of the Department of Homeland Security are not treated as reimbursements for costs incurred; and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nonprofit Security Grant Program Funding Improvement Act”.

## § 2. Treatment of grants under the Nonprofit Security Grant Program of the Department of Homeland Security

Paragraph (2) of section 2009(f) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 609a(f)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subparagraphs (A) through (G) as subparagraphs (B) through (H), respectively; and
- **(2)** by inserting before subparagraph (B), as so redesignated, the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(A)** ensuring grants awarded under this section are, notwithstanding any other provision of law, awarded in advance, and not on a reimbursable basis;
