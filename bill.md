# H.R. 8951

> To establish mandatory minimum terms of imprisonment for fraud offenses, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Zero Tolerance for Fraudsters Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Mandatory minimums for fraud offenses

- **(a)** *Fraud and false statements*
  - **(1)** *In general* Chapter 47 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
    > ## § 1041. Mandatory minimum sentences for certain offenses
    >
    > Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, in the case of an offense under section 1001, 1002, 1003, 1010, 1012, 1031, 1035, or 1040, the punishment shall be—
    >
    > - **(1)** if the offense involved an amount of $1,000,000 or more, but less than $5,000,000, a fine under this title, and imprisonment for not less than one year and not more than 10 years; or
    > - **(2)** if the offense involved an amount of $5,000,000 or more, a fine under this title, and imprisonment for not less than 5 years and not more than 20 years,

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for chapter 47 of title 18, United States Code is amended by inserting after the item related to 1040 the following:
- **(b)** *Mail fraud and other fraud offenses*
  - **(1)** *In general* Chapter 63 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
    > ## § 1353. Mandatory minimum sentences for certain offenses
    >
    > Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, in the case of an offense under section 1341, 1343, 1345, or 1347, the punishment shall be—
    >
    > - **(1)** if the offense involved an amount of $1,000,000 or more, but less than $5,000,000, a fine under this title, and imprisonment for not less than one year and not more than 10 years; or
    > - **(2)** if the offense involved an amount of $5,000,000 or more, a fine under this title, and imprisonment for not less than 5 years and not more than 20 years,
