# S. RES. 6

> Expressing the thanks of the Senate to the Honorable Patty Murray for her service as President Pro Tempore of the United States Senate and to desiginate Senator Murray as President Pro Tempore Emerita of the United States Senate.

That the United States Senate expresses its deepest gratitude to Senator Murray for her dedication and commitment during her service to the Senate as the President Pro Tempore.

-  Further, as a token of appreciation of the Senate for her long and faithful service, Senator Patty Murray is hearby designated President Pro Tempore Emerita of the United States Senate.
