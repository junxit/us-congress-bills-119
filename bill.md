# H.R. 490

> To prohibit Federal funds from being used for any property or entity owned by Donald J. Trump, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Constitutional Emoluments Protection of American Interests Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of funds
