# H.R. 10017

> To permanently prohibit the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System or a Federal reserve bank from issuing or creating a central bank digital currency, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanent CBDC Ban Act”.

## § 2. Permanent prohibition

Section 16A of the Federal Reserve Act is amended by striking subsection (d) and redesignating subsection (e) as subsection (d).
