# H.R. 11103

> To authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to award grants to food banks, food pantries, and emergency feeding organizations for Kosher-certified food, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Kosher Food Access Act”.

## § 2. Grants for Kosher-certified food

- **(a)** *Grants authorized*
  - **(1)** *In general* The Secretary of Agriculture shall award, on a competitive basis, grants to covered entities to carry out the activities described in subsection (c).
  - **(2)** *Grant period* A grant awarded under this section shall be for a period of 1 year.
- **(b)** *Application* A covered entity seeking a grant under this section shall submit to the Secretary an application in such form, at such time, and containing such information as the Secretary may require.
- **(c)** *Use of funds* A covered entity that receives a grant under this section shall use such grant funds to purchase Kosher-certified food for distribution to individuals served by the entity.
- **(d)** *Report* Not later than 90 days after the conclusion of the grant period, a covered entity that receives a grant under this section shall submit to the Secretary a report that includes—
  - **(1)** with respect to Kosher-certified food purchased and distributed pursuant to such grant—
    - **(A)** the amount of grant funds used to purchase such food; and
    - **(B)** the quantity of such food, disaggregated by food item;
  - **(2)** the number of individuals served by such distribution; and
  - **(3)** the ways in which the entity used such grant funds other than to purchase and distribute Kosher-certified food.
- **(e)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Covered entity* The term covered entity means a food bank, a food pantry, and an emergency feeding organization (as such terms are defined in section 201A of the Emergency Food Assistance Act of 1983 (7 U.S.C. 7501)).
  - **(2)** *Kosher-certified food* The term Kosher-certified food means food that is certified Kosher by an organization qualified to make such a certification, as determined by the Secretary.
