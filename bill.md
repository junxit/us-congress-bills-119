# H.R. 803

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to allow a surviving spouse of a Medal of Honor recipient to receive a special pension concurrently with dependency and indemnity compensation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sergeant Gary Beikirch Medal of Honor Act”.

## § 2. Modification of Medal of Honor special pension provisions to allow concurrent benefits

Section 1562(a)(2) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking subparagraph (C).
