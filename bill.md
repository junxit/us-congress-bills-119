# H.R. 10931

> To require the Director of the Institute of Museum and Library Services to award grants to eligible entities for public library construction, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Library Construction and Modernization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Public library construction grant program

- **(a)** *Program established* The Director of the Institute of Museum and Library Services shall award grants, on a competitive basis, to eligible entities to carry out one or more of the activities described in subsection (c).
- **(b)** *Application* An eligible entity desiring a grant under this section shall submit an application to the Director at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Director may require.
- **(c)** *Use of funds* An eligible entity that receives a grant under this section shall use the grant funds to carry out one or more of the following activities:
  - **(1)** Constructing or acquiring a building for use as a public library, including—
    - **(A)** acquiring land for such construction; or
    - **(B)** building an addition to an existing public library.
  - **(2)** Rehabilitating or renovating a building for use as a public library, including—
    - **(A)** increasing energy efficiency;
    - **(B)** creating collaborative activity spaces; or
    - **(C)** upgrading or modernizing such building for compliance with Federal health and safety standards.
  - **(3)** Repairing or renovating a public library building, or mechanical system or component of such building, that is deteriorated, broken, or worn—
    - **(A)** including repairs to—
      - **(i)** roofing;
      - **(ii)** flooring;
      - **(iii)** lighting;
      - **(iv)** plumbing;
      - **(v)** heating, ventilation, or air conditioning systems; or
      - **(vi)** other necessary mechanical systems or components to keep such building in efficient operating condition; and
    - **(B)** excluding custodial maintenance work that is not part of a project described in subparagraph (A) or paragraph (1) or (2).
- **(d)** *Federal share* The Federal share of the cost of an activity carried out using grant funds awarded under this section shall not be greater than 50 percent.
- **(e)** *Eligible entity defined* In this section, the term “eligible entity” means—
  - **(1)** a State;
  - **(2)** a local government; or
  - **(3)** an agency or instrumentality of a State or local government.
