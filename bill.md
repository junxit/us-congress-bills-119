# H.R. 9205

> To expand the Gun Free School Zones Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protect Every Preschooler Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Expansion of the Gun Free School Zones Act

Section 921(a)(27) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “means a school which” and inserting the following:
  > means—
  > - **(A)** a school which

- **(2)** by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(B)** an early childhood education program (as defined in section 103 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1003)); and
  > - **(C)** a preschool (which has the meaning given the term “eligible preschool program” in section 428K(g) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1078–11(g))).
