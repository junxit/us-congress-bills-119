# S. 1015

> To extend the National Flood Insurance Program through December 31, 2026.

## § 1. Extension of National Flood Insurance Program

- **(a)** *Financing* Section 1309(a) of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4016(a)) is amended, in the first sentence, by striking “September 30, 2023” and inserting “December 31, 2026”.
- **(b)** *Program expiration* Section 1319 of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4026) is amended by striking “September 30, 2023” and inserting “December 31, 2026”.
