# S. 5401

> To amend the Department of Energy Organization Act to require that financial assistance provided by the Department of Energy promote high labor standards, community benefits, domestic manufacturing, and support for small or disadvantaged communities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Good Energy Jobs Act of 2026”.
