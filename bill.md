# H. RES. 1649

> Expressing support for the designation of the first full week of November as “National Animal Shelter Appreciation Week”.

## Preamble

- Whereas “National Animal Shelter Appreciation Week” is observed annually during the first full week of November to recognize the important contributions of animal shelters and the people who support them;
- Whereas animal shelters serve as critical community resources, providing refuge, humane care, and protection for lost, abandoned, neglected, and surrendered animals;
- Whereas dedicated shelter employees, veterinary professionals, behavioral specialists, and animal care and control personnel work to protect the health, safety, and well-being of animals in need;
- Whereas volunteers, foster families, animal rescue organizations, and community members generously contribute their time and resources to support animal shelters and the animals in their care;
- Whereas many animal shelters offer community programs, including veterinary services, vaccinations, spay-and-neuter, pet food assistance, and educational initiatives that promote responsible pet ownership;
- Whereas these programs help families keep their pets during periods of hardship and preserve the human-animal bond;
- Whereas animal shelters reunite lost pets with their families and provide animals with a second chance at life through adoption and foster care programs;
- Whereas community adoption events bring together animal shelters, volunteers, local organizations, and prospective adopters to raise awareness of pets in need and match companion animals with new families; and
- Whereas animal shelters and their community partners play important roles in protecting animals affected by cruelty, neglect, natural disasters, and other emergencies: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** supports the designation of “National Animal Shelter Appreciation Week”;
- **(2)** recognizes and commends animal shelter employees, volunteers, foster caregivers, rescue organizations, and community partners for their dedication to the health and well-being of pets;
- **(3)** recognizes the important role of animal shelters in strengthening communities, supporting the human-animal bond, and helping animals find safe and permanent homes;
- **(4)** encourages individuals and communities to support their local animal shelters through adoption, fostering, volunteering, participation in adoption events, and charitable contributions; and
- **(5)** encourages continued public awareness of and support for animal shelters and the essential services they provide to animals, families, and communities across the United States.
