# H.R. 691

> To designate the Washington Dulles International Airport in Virginia as the “Donald J. Trump International Airport”.

## § 1. Designation of Donald J. Trump International Airport

- **(a)** *Designation* The Washington Dulles International Airport in Virginia shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Donald J. Trump International Airport”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the airport referred to in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Donald J. Trump International Airport.
