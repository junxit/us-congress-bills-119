# H.R. 11107

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to facilitate repatriations of certain intellectual property, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Investing in American Innovation Act”.

## § 2. Special rules for repatriations of certain intellectual property

- **(a)** *In general* Subpart F of part III of subchapter N of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after section 954 the following new section:
  > ## § 955. Repatriations of certain intellectual property
  >
  > - **(a)** *Certain transaction gain not taxed under subpart F*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* In the case of the first specified IP transaction which is part of a qualified IP repatriation, the amounts included in the gross income of any specified United States shareholder under sections 951 and 951A (determined without regard to this section) shall be reduced (but not below zero) by such shareholder’s pro rata share (determined by applying the rules of section 951(a)(2)) of any net transaction gain of the transferor in such transaction.
  >   - **(2)** *Specified United States shareholder* For purposes of this subsection, the term “specified United States shareholder” means, with respect to any qualified IP repatriation, any United States shareholder with respect to the controlled foreign corporation which is the transferor referred to in paragraph (1) if such United States shareholder is—
  >     - **(A)** the ultimate transferee with respect to such IP repatriation, or
  >     - **(B)** a member of the same consolidated group as such ultimate transferee.
  > - **(b)** *Certain payments by ultimate transferee not treated as base erosion payments* In the case of the last specified IP transaction which is part of a qualified IP repatriation, the amount paid or accrued by the ultimate transferee for the repatriated intellectual property shall not be treated as a base erosion payment for purposes of section 59A.
  > - **(c)** *Basis adjustment for ultimate transferee* In the case of the last specified IP transaction which is part of a qualified IP repatriation, notwithstanding sections 301(d) and 1012, the basis of repatriated intellectual property immediately after such transaction (in the hands of the ultimate transferee) shall not exceed the sum of—
  >   - **(1)** the basis of such property in the hands of the transferor in the first specified IP transaction (immediately before such first specified IP transaction) which is part of such qualified IP repatriation, plus
  >   - **(2)** the excess (if any) of—
  >     - **(A)** any net transaction gain of such transferor, over
  >     - **(B)** the aggregate reductions of amounts included in the gross income of United States shareholders under subsection (a).
  > - **(d)** *Application of foreign tax credit*
  >   - **(1)** *Allowance of deemed paid credit*
  >     - **(A)** *In general* For purposes of subpart A of this part, the ultimate transferee with respect to any qualified IP repatriation shall be deemed to have paid so much of the foreign income taxes of the transferor in the first specified IP transaction which is part of such qualified IP repatriation as are properly attributable to the net transaction gain of such transferor.
  >     - **(B)** *Application of certain rules* Except as otherwise provided by the Secretary, rules similar to the rules that apply for purposes of section 960(a) shall apply for purposes of this paragraph.
  >     - **(C)** *Denial of double benefit*
  >       - **(i)** *In general* Any income taxes deemed to have been paid under subparagraph (A) shall not otherwise be taken into account in determining the foreign tax credit of any taxpayer under subpart A of this part.
  >       - **(ii)** *Cross reference for treatment of transaction gain not deemed paid by ultimate transferee* For rules applicable to taxes deemed paid by a United States shareholder that is a domestic corporation other than the ultimate transferee (or a member of the same consolidated group as the ultimate transferee), see subsection (a) or (d) of section 960, as applicable.
  >   - **(2)** *Application of foreign tax credit limitation*
  >     - **(A)** *Separate application* In the case of any item of repatriated intellectual property, subsections (a), (b), and (c) of section 904 shall be applied separately to income derived from such property (and to any taxes paid or accrued with respect to such income and any taxes deemed paid under paragraph (1)).
  >     - **(B)** *Treatment of certain U.S. source income*
  >       - **(i)** *In general* For purposes of the application of section 904 pursuant to subparagraph (A), 50 percent of a taxpayer’s gross income derived from repatriated intellectual property which is from sources within the United States shall be treated as from sources without the United States (and not from sources within the United States).
  >       - **(ii)** *Coordination with recharacterization of overall domestic loss* For purposes of subsection (g) of section 904—
  >         - **(I)** clause (i) shall apply before the application of such subsection, and
  >         - **(II)** income derived from repatriated intellectual property shall be treated as a separate income category (within the meaning of such subsection).
  >       - **(iii)** *Exclusion of certain gains on transferred property* For purposes of this subparagraph, income derived from repatriated intellectual property shall not include gain from the sale or other disposition of repatriated intellectual property if such sale or other disposition is before the date that is 5 years after the date of the last specified IP transaction which is part of the qualified IP repatriation of such property.
  >     - **(C)** *Period of application* Subparagraphs (A) and (B) shall only apply with respect to income received or accrued during the period—
  >       - **(i)** beginning on the date of the first specified IP transaction which is part of the qualified IP repatriation of the repatriated intellectual property, and
  >       - **(ii)** ending with the close of the first taxable year—
  >         - **(I)** for which all credits of the taxpayer subject to limitation under subparagraph (A) (determined after application of subparagraph (D)(i)) with respect to income derived from such property have been allowed, or
  >         - **(II)** which includes the first date on which such property ceases to be of commercial value or is sold (or otherwise disposed of) by the taxpayer.
  >     - **(D)** *Application of carryback and carryforward* For purposes of the application of section 904(c) pursuant to subparagraph (A)—
  >       - **(i)** *20-year carryforward* Such section shall be applied by substituting “in any of the first 20 succeeding taxable years” for “in any of the first 10 succeeding taxable years”.
  >       - **(ii)** *No carryback or carryforward to years to which separate application does not apply* Such section shall not result in any amount being carried to any taxable year if no portion of such taxable year is part of the period described in subparagraph (C).
  >     - **(E)** *Treatment of related intellectual property* The Secretary may, under regulations or other guidance, allow or require that the subsections referred to in subparagraph (A) (and the related provisions of this paragraph) be applied separately with respect to the income derived from multiple items of repatriated intellectual property if such property, or the income derived from such property, is sufficiently related that the Secretary determines that such application would aid in the efficient administration of this title. Such regulations or other guidance may provide for such modifications to the application of subparagraph (C) as the Secretary determines appropriate.
  > - **(e)** *Qualified IP repatriation* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *In general* The term “qualified IP repatriation” means any IP repatriation if—
  >     - **(A)** all of the transactions comprising such IP repatriation are—
  >       - **(i)** specified IP transactions, and
  >       - **(ii)** completed before the earlier of—
  >         - **(I)** the due date (including extensions) of the return of tax for the United States shareholder referred to in paragraph (2)(A) for the taxable year that includes the date of the first such specified IP transaction, or
  >         - **(II)** the date that such return is filed, and
  >     - **(B)** an election described in subsection (i) is made with respect to such IP repatriation.
  >   - **(2)** *IP repatriation* The term “IP repatriation” means a transaction, or any series of transactions, in which specified intellectual property is transferred from a controlled foreign corporation to an ultimate transferee which is—
  >     - **(A)** a United States shareholder with respect to such controlled foreign corporation, or
  >     - **(B)** a member of the same consolidated group as such United States shareholder.
  >   - **(3)** *Ultimate transferee* The term “ultimate transferee” means the transferee in the last transaction which is part of an IP repatriation.
  > - **(f)** *Specified IP transaction* For purposes of this section, the term “specified IP transaction” means any transfer of specified intellectual property which is part of an IP repatriation if—
  >   - **(1)** such transfer is—
  >     - **(A)** a sale or other disposition to which section 1001 applies,
  >     - **(B)** a distribution to which section 301 applies, or
  >     - **(C)** to the extent provided by the Secretary—
  >       - **(i)** an amount to which section 331 applies,
  >       - **(ii)** a distribution to which section 332 applies, or
  >       - **(iii)** a part of any reorganization described in section 368(a) with respect to which gain is recognized,
  >   - **(2)** the transferor is—
  >     - **(A)** the controlled foreign corporation referred to in subsection (e)(2) with respect to such IP repatriation, or
  >     - **(B)** a controlled foreign corporation that received such property in a prior specified IP transaction which is part of such IP repatriation,
  >   - **(3)** the transferee (or a member of the same consolidated group as the transferee) owns directly or indirectly (within the meaning of section 958(a)) 80 percent or more of the transferor by vote or value, and
  >   - **(4)** the transferee is—
  >     - **(A)** the ultimate transferee with respect to such IP repatriation, or
  >     - **(B)** a controlled foreign corporation with respect to which the person described in subparagraph (A) is a United States shareholder.
  > - **(g)** *Net transaction gain* For purposes of this section, the term “net transaction gain” means, with respect to the first specified IP transaction which is part of a qualified IP repatriation, the excess (if any) of—
  >   - **(1)** so much of any gain recognized by the transferor on such transaction as is taken into account (determined without regard to this section) in determining the subpart F income of such transferor under section 952 or the tested income of such transferor under section 951A, over
  >   - **(2)** any deductions properly allocable to such gain.
  > - **(h)** *Repatriated intellectual property; specified intellectual property* For purposes of this section—
  >   - **(1)** *Repatriated intellectual property* The term “repatriated intellectual property” means any specified intellectual property which is transferred in a qualified IP repatriation as described in subsection (e)(1).
  >   - **(2)** *Specified intellectual property* The term “specified intellectual property” means property described in section 197(d)(1)(C)(iii).
  > - **(i)** *Election* Any election under subsection (e)(1)(B)—
  >   - **(1)** shall be made—
  >     - **(A)** not later than the date described in subsection (e)(1)(A)(ii), and
  >     - **(B)** in such manner as the Secretary may provide,
  >   - **(2)** shall apply to the United States shareholder referred to in subsection (e)(2)(A) and to all members of the same consolidated group as such United States shareholder,
  >   - **(3)** shall identify—
  >     - **(A)** the specified intellectual property being transferred, and
  >     - **(B)** all the transactions comprising the IP repatriation of such property,
  >   - **(4)** shall include such other information as the Secretary may require, and
  >   - **(5)** once made, may be revoked only with the consent of the Secretary.
  > - **(j)** *Regulations* The Secretary shall issue such regulations or other guidance as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of this section, including regulations or other guidance—
  >   - **(1)** treating United States persons which are partners of a domestic partnership which is the ultimate transferee with respect to a qualified IP repatriation as ultimate transferees with respect to such repatriation if the requirement of subsection (f)(3) is met with respect to such United States persons determined by only taking into account the aggregate interests of such United States persons in the partnership,
  >   - **(2)** for determining the amount of income derived from repatriated intellectual property, and the foreign taxes properly attributable to such income, for purposes of subsection (d),
  >   - **(3)** providing for proper coordination of subsection (a) and (g) of section 904 in cases to which subsection (d)(2)(B)(ii) applies,
  >   - **(4)** to prevent the abuse of any provision of this section, including subsection (d)(2)(B)(iii),

- **(b)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections for subpart F of part III of subchapter N of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting after the item relating to section 954 the following new item:
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to IP repatriations (as defined in section 955(e)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986) beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.

## § 3. Certain research or experimental expenditures for the production of intellectual property not treated as base erosion payments

- **(a)** *In general* Section 59A(d)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(5)** *Exception for certain amounts with respect to services*
  >   - **(A)** *In general* Paragraph (1) shall not apply to so much of any qualified services expenditure as does not exceed the total services cost with no markup component.
  >   - **(B)** *Qualified services expenditure* For purposes of this paragraph, the term “qualified services expenditure” means—
  >     - **(i)** any amount paid or accrued for services if such services are services which meet the requirements for eligibility for use of the services cost method under section 482 (determined without regard to the requirement that the services not contribute significantly to fundamental risks of business success or failure), and
  >     - **(ii)** any amount paid or accrued for services if—
  >       - **(I)** such amount is a research or experimental expenditure paid or accrued solely to produce specified intellectual property (as defined in section 955(h)(2)) that is owned entirely by the taxpayer, and
  >       - **(II)** the taxpayer is a related person with respect to the service provider (within the meaning of section 954(d)(3)).
  >   - **(C)** *Regulations* The Secretary shall issue such regulations or other guidance as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of this paragraph, including regulations or other guidance for determining that amount paid or accrued that constitutes total services cost with no markup component for purposes of subparagraph (A).

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or accrued in taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
