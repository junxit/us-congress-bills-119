# H.R. 11093

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to include fire investigators for public safety officers’ death benefits, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fire Investigators Recognition Act”.

## § 2. Public Safety Officers’ Death Benefits

Section 1204 of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10284) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending paragraph (7) to read as follows:
  > - **(7)** “firefighter” includes an individual serving as—
  >   - **(A)** an officially recognized or designated member of a legally organized volunteer fire department, including an individual who, as such a member, engages in scene security or traffic management as the primary or only duty of the individual during emergency response; or
  >   - **(B)** a fire investigator;

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(15)** “fire investigator” means an individual serving as an officially recognized or designated member of a legally organized public safety agency, and is officially authorized to conduct, coordinate, and complete fire or explosion investigations and is conducting, coordinating, and completing an investigation responding to a current or ongoing emergency of a fire or explosion.
