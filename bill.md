# H.R. 10777

> To codify certain policies to combat transnational cyber-enabled crime, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Coordination Center and Commercial Cyber Capabilities Codification Act”.

## § 2. Codification of policy to combat transnational cyber-enabled crime

- **(a)** *Codification* A covered order or memorandum shall have the force and effect of law.
- **(b)** *Covered order or memorandum defined* The term “covered order or memorandum” means each of the following:
  - **(1)** Executive Order 14390 (91 Fed. Reg. 12051, relating combating cybercrime, fraud, and predatory schemes against American citizens (March 6, 2026)).
  - **(2)** Section 6 of Executive Order 14159 (90 Fed. Reg. 8443, relating to protecting the American people against invasion (Janurary 20, 2025)).
  - **(3)** National Security Presidential Memorandum, relating to expanding capabilities to combat transnational cyber-enabled crime (August 12, 2026).
