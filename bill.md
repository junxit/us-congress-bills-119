# H.R. 6020

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 14855 South Van Dyke Road in Plainfield, Illinois, as the “Staff Sergeant Jose Dueñez Jr. Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Staff Sergeant Jose Dueñez Jr. Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 14855 South Van Dyke Road in Plainfield, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Staff Sergeant Jose Dueñez Jr. Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Staff Sergeant Jose Dueñez Jr. Post Office Building”.
