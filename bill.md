# H.R. 10201

> To amend the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act to ensure that students with an individualized education program receive a certificate of completion upon completion of secondary school education.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Certificate of Merit for Persons with Abilities and Special Support Needs Act” or the “COMPASS Act”.

## § 2. Individualized education programs

Section 614(d)(1)(A)(i)(VIII) of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1414(d)(1)(A)(i)(VIII)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and” at the end of item (bb);
- **(2)** by redesignating item (cc) as item (dd); and
- **(3)** by inserting after item (bb), the following:
  > - **(cc)** the projected date for the child’s completion of the child’s secondary education program, and a statement that the child will receive a certification of completion on such date, without regard to whether the child is eligible to receive a regular secondary school diploma; and
