# H.R. 10698

> To require the Secretary of Homeland Security and the Attorney General to share evidence with appropriate State and local law enforcement agencies related to shootings, injuries, or killings, by officers or employees of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement or U.S. Customs and Border Protection, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “ICE Shootings Transparency Act”.

## § 2. Information sharing with State and local law enforcement related to shootings, injuries, or killings by ICE and CBP

In the case that an officer or employee of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement or U.S. Customs and Border Protection discharges a firearm in the course of their official duties, or injures or kills another person, the Secretary of Homeland Security and the Attorney General shall, without unreasonable delay—

- **(1)** provide access to the location of the incident to State and local law enforcement agencies with jurisdiction over such location upon the arrival, for the purpose of carrying out regular investigative functions, of a State or local law enforcement officer at such location, subject to legitimate scene-safety or evidence-preservation needs of the Secretary and Attorney General; and
- **(2)** share any evidence and investigatory materials related to the incident with such State and local law enforcement agencies, including by preserving and disclosing all relevant evidence on an ongoing basis as it is developed, including body-worn camera footage, surveillance video, radio communications, dispatch records, digital communications, photographs, forensic evidence, witness statements, and other investigative materials.
