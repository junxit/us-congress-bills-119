# H.R. 5625

> To direct the Attorney General to make publicly available a list of each State and unit of local government that permits cashless bail, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cashless Bail Reporting Act”.

## § 2. Publication of cashless bail jurisdictions

Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Attorney General shall make publicly available a list of each State and unit of local government that permits the release pending trial of a person charged with an offense on personal recognizance or upon execution of an unsecured appearance bond. The Attorney General shall update such list on a quarterly basis.
