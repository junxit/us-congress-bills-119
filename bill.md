# H.R. 9387

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to ensure consumer choice by requiring truthful labeling of lab-created butter, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Recognizing Engineered Alternatives as Lab-Created Butter Act” or the “REAL Butter Act”.

## § 2. Ensuring consumer choice by requiring truthful labeling of lab-created butter

Section 403 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 343) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(z)**
>   - **(1)** If it is a synthesized butter product or contains such a product and its label does not bear immediately before the name of the product “lab-created butter” or “contains lab-created butter”, respectively.
>   - **(2)** For purposes of this paragraph:
>     - **(A)** The term ‘synthesized butter product’ means a product that—
>       - **(i)** is marketed as butter, but utilizes sources of milkfat that are synthesized through non-agricultural processes; and
>       - **(ii)** fails to conform to the standard of identity for butter specified in the Act of March 4, 1923 (42 Stat. 1500).
>     - **(B)** The term butter has the meaning given such term in the Act of March 4, 1923 (42 Stat. 1500).
