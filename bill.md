# H.R. 10269

> To amend title 18 to include an offense for motor vehicle collision fraud, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Auto Fraud Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Motor vehicle collision fraud

- **(a)** *Offense*
  - **(1)** *In general* Chapter 63 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
    > ## § 1353. Motor vehicle collision fraud
    >
    > Whoever knowingly executes, or attempts to execute, a scheme or artifice—
    >
    > - **(1)** to cause, stage, fabricate, or simulate a motor vehicle accident; and
    > - **(2)** to submit any false, fictitious, or fraudulent claim for payment of a loss under an insurance policy, including a loss related to medical treatment, repair costs, lost wages, or lost benefits,

  - **(2)** *Clerical amendment* The table of sections at the beginning of chapter 63 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
- **(b)** *Criminal fines deposited in highway trust fund* Any fines collected under section 1353 of title 18, United States Code, shall be deposited into the Highway Trust Fund established by section 9503(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
