# H.R. 10798

> To establish a grant program for funding silicosis treatment and education, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Silicosis Prevention and Treatment Act”.

## § 2. Silicosis Clinics Program

- **(a)** *Establishment* The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Administrator of the Health Resources and Services Administration, (in this section referred to as the “Secretary”) shall award grants, on a competitive basis, to eligible entities to treat individuals with silicosis, regardless of their ability to pay, and provide education to workers in industries with high exposure to silica.
- **(b)** *Eligible entities* To be eligible for a grant under this section, an entity shall be—
  - **(1)** a federally qualified health center (as defined in section 1861(aa) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x(aa)));
  - **(2)** a hospital;
  - **(3)** a State or local health department; or
  - **(4)** a nonprofit health care clinic or nonprofit health care provider.
- **(c)** *Use of funds* A grant awarded under this section may be used for—
  - **(1)** outreach and education to raise awareness of silicosis;
  - **(2)** screening, diagnosis, and treatment of silicosis;
  - **(3)** education programs for patients, family members, and workers in industries heavily impacted by silicosis;
  - **(4)** benefits counseling related to silicosis (including with respect to State and Federal compensation programs);
  - **(5)** patient care and coordination related to silicosis (including individual patient care plans and referrals, as needed);
  - **(6)** pulmonary rehabilitation to improve breathing in relation to silicosis; or
  - **(7)** such other activities as the Secretary determines appropriate.
- **(d)** *State defined* In this section, the term State means each of the several States, the District of Columbia, a territory or possession of the United States, or an Indian Tribe.
- **(e)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031.
