# H.R. 11019

> To limit the use of Federal funds to carry out the proposed rule titled “Confidential Government Information Nondisclosure Agreement” relating to nondisclosure agreements for Federal employees, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Limitation on use of funds to carry out rule relating to nondisclosure agreements for Federal employees

No Federal funds may be used to develop, finalize, implement, administer, or enforce the proposed rule titled “Confidential Government Information Nondisclosure Agreement” published by the Office of Personnel Management in the Federal Register on May 27, 2026 (91 Fed. Reg. 31478), or any substantially similar rule.
