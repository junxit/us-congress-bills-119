# H.R. 2119

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to establish St. Patrick’s Day as a Federal holiday.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “St. Patrick’s Day Act”.

## § 2. St. Patrick’s Day

Section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by inserting after the item relating to Washington’s Birthday the following:

> -  St. Patrick’s Day.
