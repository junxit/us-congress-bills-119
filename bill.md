# H.R. 410

> To extend the Alaska Native Vietnam era veterans land allotment program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Alaska Native Vietnam Era Veterans Land Allotment Extension Act of 2025”.

## § 2. Extension of the Alaska Native Vietnam Era Veterans Land Allotment Program

Section 1119(b)(3)(B) of the John D. Dingell, Jr. Conservation, Management, and Recreation Act (43 U.S.C. 1629g–1(b)(3)(B)) is amended by striking “5-year period” and inserting “10-year period”.
