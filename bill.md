# S. 296

> To make certain modifications to the repayment for the Arkansas Valley Conduit in the State of Colorado.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Finish the Arkansas Valley Conduit Act”.

## § 2. Arkansas Valley Conduit, Colorado

Public Law 87–590 (76 Stat. 389) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (c) of the first section (76 Stat. 390; 123 Stat. 1320), in the second sentence—
  - **(A)** by inserting “, without interest,” before “in an amount”;
  - **(B)** by striking “that is comprised of revenue generated by payments” and inserting “, which payment shall only consist of funding provided during construction from any person other than the Secretary”;
  - **(C)** by inserting “of” before “revenue that”; and
  - **(D)** by inserting “over a period of 100 years” after “project facilities”; and
- **(2)** in section 2(b)(3)(A) (76 Stat. 390; 123 Stat. 1321)—
  - **(A)** by striking “plus interest”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “this section” and inserting “subsection (c) of the first section”.
