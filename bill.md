# H.R. 10994

> To reauthorize the Rural Business Development Grant Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Supporting Rural Business Development Act”.

## § 2. Rural business development grants

Section 310B(c)(4)(A) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 U.S.C. 1932(c)(4)(A)) is amended by striking “2014 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
