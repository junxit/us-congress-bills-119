# H.R. 4574

> To amend the District of Columbia Home Rule Act to permit the Council of the District of Columbia to enact laws with respect to the organization and jurisdiction of the District of Columbia courts.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “District of Columbia Courts Home Rule Act”.

## § 2. Authority of Council of District of Columbia to enact laws with respect to District of Columbia Courts

Section 602(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (sec. 1–206.02(a), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking paragraph (4).
