# S. 4827

> To reauthorize a program for preventing outages and enhancing the resilience of the electric grid, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Outages With Enhanced Resilience and Operations Nationwide Act of 2026” or the “POWER ON Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Reauthorization

Section 40101 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (42 U.S.C. 18711) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (i)(1), by striking “2026” and inserting “2031”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (j), by striking “2022 through 2026” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
