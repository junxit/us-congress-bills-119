# H.R. 5446

> To limit the authority to impose duties on articles of food.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Tariffs on Groceries Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on authority to impose duties on articles of food

- **(a)** *In general* The President may not impose duties proposed by the Presidential Administration on articles of food unless—
  - **(1)** the President transmits to Congress a request to impose such duties; and
  - **(2)** such request is subsequently and specifically approved by an Act of Congress.
- **(b)** *Exception* The limitation in subsection (a) shall not apply with respect to existing tariff-rate quotas on articles of food.
- **(c)** *Articles of food defined* In this section, the term “articles of food” means—
  - **(1)** articles used for food or drink for man or other animals;
  - **(2)** articles used for components of any articles described in paragraph (1); and
  - **(3)** seeds, fertilizers, manures, and agro-chemicals.
