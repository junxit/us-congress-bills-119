# S. 5680

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to rename, redesignate, or otherwise change the name of the vessel designated as CVN–81 from the “USS Doris Miller”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Maintaining Integrity of Military Legacy and Enduring Recognition Act” or the “MILLER Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of funds to rename, redesignate, or otherwise change the name of CVN–81 from USS Doris Miller

No Federal funds may be obligated or expended to rename, redesignate, or otherwise change the name of the vessel designated as CVN–81 from the “USS Doris Miller”.
