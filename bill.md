# S.J. RES. 30

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the National Park Service relating to “Glen Canyon National Recreation Area; Motor Vehicles”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the National Park Service relating to “Glen Canyon National Recreation Area; Motor Vehicles” (90 Fed. Reg. 2621 (January 13, 2025)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
