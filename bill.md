# S. 1518

> To redesignate the Saratoga National Historical Park as the “Saratoga National Battlefield Park”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Strengthening America’s Turning Point Act”.

## § 2. Saratoga National Battlefield Park

- **(a)** *Redesignation* The Saratoga National Historical Park shall be known and designated as the “Saratoga National Battlefield Park”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Saratoga National Historical Park shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Saratoga National Battlefield Park”.
