# H.R. 10598

> To direct the Secretary of Transportation to establish a multimodal transportation demonstration program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Improving Rural Transportation Act”.

## § 2. Multimodal transportation demonstration program

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter I of chapter 55 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 5508. Multimodal transportation demonstration program
  >
  > - **(a)** *Establishment* The Secretary may establish a program to make grants to eligible entities to carry out demonstration projects in small- or mid-sized communities for advanced transportation technologies related to surface passenger transportation modes (including any technical support and planning costs related to such projects), which may include any of the following:
  >   - **(1)** A project related to data availability or interoperability, a traveler support tool or service, an automated vehicle, an active demand management, micro-transit, or a micromobility device to demonstrate first-mile and last-mile transportation and intermodal connections to a regional transportation system, including with respect to the following:
  >     - **(A)** Local transit, intercity bus, rail, and aviation systems.
  >     - **(B)** Electric or zero-emission systems.
  >   - **(2)** A project related to developing a ride-share program for persons in rural communities.
  >   - **(3)** Any other project the Secretary determines appropriate.
  > - **(b)** *Application* If the Secretary establishes the program described in subsection (a), an eligible entity that seeks a grant under such program shall submit to the Secretary an application at such time, in such manner, and containing such information as the Secretary may require, including information relating to how such entity would collaborate with any of the following:
  >   - **(1)** Institutions of higher education.
  >   - **(2)** States.
  >   - **(3)** Units of local government.
  >   - **(4)** Regional transportation planning organizations.
  >   - **(5)** Nonprofit research organizations.
  >   - **(6)** Private sector entities.
  > - **(c)** *Priority* If the Secretary establishes the program described in subsection (a), the Secretary shall prioritize any application submitted under subsection (b) from an eligible entity that demonstrates scalability, operational reliability, cost efficiency, and technology validation with respect to real-world conditions.
  > - **(d)** *Funds*
  >   - **(1)** *Federal share* If the Secretary establishes the program described in subsection (a), the Federal share of the cost of a demonstration project with respect to which a grant is made under such program may not exceed 80 percent of such cost.
  >   - **(2)** *Sources* If the Secretary establishes the program described in subsection (a), the non-Federal share of the cost of a demonstration project with respect to which a grant is made under such program may include amounts made available to the recipient of such grant under any of the following:
  >     - **(A)** Section 504(b) of title 23.
  >     - **(B)** Section 505 of such title.
  > - **(e)** *Definitions* In this section:
  >   - **(1)** *Eligible entity* The term eligible entity means any of the following entities:
  >     - **(A)** A State department of transportation.
  >     - **(B)** A unit of local government.
  >     - **(C)** A metropolitan planning organization.
  >     - **(D)** A public transportation agency.
  >     - **(E)** Any other entity the Secretary determines appropriate.
  >   - **(2)** *Institution of higher education* The term institution of higher education has the meaning given such term in section 101 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1001).
  >   - **(3)** *Metropolitan planning organization* The term “metropolitan planning organization” has the meaning given such term in subsection (b) of section 5303.
  >   - **(4)** *Micromobility device* The term micromobility device means a wheeled vehicle that satisfies the following requirements:
  >     - **(A)** Includes a low-powered electric motor.
  >     - **(B)** Is designed for human transport.
  >     - **(C)** Weighs not more than 100 pounds.
  >     - **(D)** Is not capable of attaining a maximum speed greater than 20 miles per hour.
  >   - **(5)** *Public transportation agency* The term “public transportation agency” has the meaning given such term in section 1402 of the National Transit Systems Security Act of 2007 (6 U.S.C. 1131).
  >   - **(6)** *Regional transportation planning organization* The term regional transportation planning organization means an organization designated under subsection (l) of section 5304.
  >   - **(7)** *Ride-share program* The term “ride-share program” means a commercial service whereby a passenger may use a smartphone app to request private, point-to-point transportation from an independent driver for a fee.
  >   - **(8)** *Secretary* The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Transportation.
  >   - **(9)** *Small- or mid-sized community* The term small- or mid-sized community means an area with a population of not more than 200,000 individuals.
