# H.R. 8348

> To amend title 23, United States Code, with respect to the amounts States are guaranteed under the highway apportionment formula, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Guaranteed amounts

Section 104(c)(1)(B) of title 23, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (ii) by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in clause (iii) by striking the period and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(iv)** at least 0.5 percent of the total apportionment.
