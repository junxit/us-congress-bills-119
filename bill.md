# H.R. 10999

> To reauthorize the Expansion of 911 Access Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keeping Rural America Safe Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of 911 access

Section 315(d) of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 940e(d)) is amended by striking “2008 through 2023” and inserting “2027 through 2031”.
