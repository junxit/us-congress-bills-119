# H.R. 10998

> To reauthorize USDA guarantees for Bonds and Notes issued for utility infrastructure purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Building Out Utility Infrastructure in Rural America Act”.

## § 2. Guarantees for bonds and notes issued for utility infrastructure purposes

Section 313A(f) of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 U.S.C. 940c–1(f)) is amended by striking “2023” and inserting “2031”.
