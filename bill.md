# H.R. 1596

> To designate the U.S. National Poultry Research Center of the Department of Agriculture located in Athens, Georgia, as the “Abit Massey Poultry Research Center”.

## § 1. Abit Massey Poultry Research Center

- **(a)** *Designation* The U.S. National Poultry Research Center of the Department of Agriculture located in Athens, Georgia, shall be known and designated as the “Abit Massey Poultry Research Center”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Abit Massey Poultry Research Center”.
