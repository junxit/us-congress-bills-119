# H.R. 5292

> To reimpose Jackson-Vanik restrictions on the Russian Federation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “No Trade with Terrorists Act”.

## § 2.

Secs. 102, 201, 202, 203 & 204 of PL 112–208 are repealed.
