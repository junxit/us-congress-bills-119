# H.R. 10978

> To amend the Small Business Act to eliminate the requirement for a written justification and approval for certain contracts awarded to small business concerns, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Cutting Red Tape for Small Businesses Act”.

## § 2. No written justification and approval required for certain contracts awarded to small business concerns

The Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 631 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 8(m), by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(9)** *Justification and approval not required* A written justification and approval is not required for a contract awarded to small business concerns owned and controlled by women under this subsection.

- **(2)** in section 31(c)(2)(B), by adding at the end the following new sentence: “A written justification and approval is not required for a contract awarded under this subparagraph.”; and
- **(3)** in section 36(d), by adding at the end the following new sentence: “A written justification and approval is not required for a contract awarded under this subsection.”.
