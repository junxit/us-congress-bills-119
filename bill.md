# H.R. 9793

> To amend title 28 to include additional limitations on the intentional tort exception, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Weaponization Offender Claims Ban Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Exceptions

Section 2680(h) of title 28, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “Any claim” and inserting “(1) Any claim”;
- **(2)** by inserting “subject to paragraph (2),” before “with regard to acts”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** A private individual may only bring such a claim if such individual has not been convicted of (without regard to whether that person was pardoned or any sentence entered against that person was commuted)—
  >   - **(A)** assault or battery against a Federal law enforcement officer;
  >   - **(B)** an offense related to interference with the administration, certification, or counting of a Federal election;
  >   - **(C)** an offense related to obstruction, interference, or unlawful influence over a Federal governmental process;
  >   - **(D)** any felony offense related to any confrontation with a Federal law enforcement officer; or
  >   - **(E)** any felony offense involving theft, embezzlement, misuse of Federal funds, obstruction of Federal oversight or investigative activities, or interference with appropriations or budgetary processes.
