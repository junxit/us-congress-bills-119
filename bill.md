# S. 5764

> To amend title 28, United States Code, to authorize the Attorney General to transfer certain amounts from the working capital fund of the Department of Justice to the Crime Victims Fund, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Crime Victims Fund Prioritization Act”.

## § 2. Transfers to crime victims fund

Section 527 of title 28, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “There is” and inserting “(a) There is”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(b)**
  >   - **(1)** Notwithstanding any other provision of law and subject to paragraph (2), on September 1 of each year during the period beginning on the date of enactment of this subsection and ending on September 30, 2029, the Attorney General shall transfer the unobligated balance of the fund to the Crime Victims Fund established under section 1402 of the Victims of Crime Act of 1984 (34 U.S.C. 20101).
  >   - **(2)** The amounts transferred under this subsection shall not exceed any limitation enacted pursuant to an appropriation Act on the availability to obligate certain amounts from the Crime Victims Fund.
  >   - **(3)** Not later than 30 days after the date on which the Attorney General makes a transfer under this subsection, the Attorney General shall submit to the Committee on the Judiciary of the Senate and the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives a notification of such transfer.
