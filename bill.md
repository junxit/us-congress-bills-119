# H.R. 5525

> To repeal the authority of the District of Columbia to use automated traffic enforcement systems and to erect signage prohibiting right turns at a red traffic signal.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop DC Capital Authoritarian Motorvehicle Enforcement and Restoration of Autonomy Act” or the “Stop DC CAMERA Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of authority to use automated traffic enforcement systems and to erect signage prohibiting certain right turns at red traffic signal

- **(a)** *Repeal of authority to use automated traffic enforcement systems*
  - **(1)** *In general* Title IX of the Fiscal Year 1997 Budget Support Act of 1996 (sec. 50–2209.01 et seq., D.C. Official Code) is hereby repealed.
  - **(2)** *Expansion plan* Section 103 of the Safety-Based Traffic Enforcement Amendment Act of 2012 (Sec. 50–2209.11, D.C. Official Code) is hereby repealed.
- **(b)** *Repeal of authority to erect signage prohibiting certain right turns at red traffic signal* Section 9e of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925 (sec. 50–2201.04e, D.C. Official Code) is hereby repealed.
