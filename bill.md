# S. 5435

> To prohibit immigration enforcement actions at school bus stops for children.

## § 1. Restriction of immigration enforcement actions at school bus stops for children

No agent, officer, or employee of the Department of Homeland Security, nor any person acting under the authority of such agent, officer, or employee, may conduct any immigration enforcement action at a school bus stop for children.
