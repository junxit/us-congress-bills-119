# H. RES. 312

> Dismissing the election contest relating to the office of Representative from the Fourteenth Congressional District of Florida.

That the election contest filed on January 9, 2025, relating to the office of Representative from the Fourteenth Congressional District of Florida is dismissed on the grounds of untimely filing with the House of Representatives.
