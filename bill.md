# H.R. 11028

> To direct the Secretary of the Treasury to list the North American Islamic Trust and its subsidiaries and affiliates as Specially Designated Global Terrorists, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Designating North American Islamic Trust Act of 2026” or as the “DesigNAIT Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Findings

Congress finds the following:

- **(1)** The North American Islamic Trust (NAIT) is an Illinois not-for-profit corporation originally incorporated in the State of Indiana on May 23, 1973, by the Muslim Students Association of the United States and Canada, the predecessor of the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA). NAIT states that it “maintains its foundational supporting relationship” with both organizations and holds title as a perpetual Islamic endowment (waqf) to the real estate of Islamic centers and schools in 42 States. NAIT serves as trustee for “400+ Waqf institutions” in North America with a market value totaling “several hundred million dollars”.
- **(2)** NAIT states that property placed in its waqf is “restricted on a perpetual basis” for the benefit of the Islamic community, and that neither the donor, the beneficiary, nor even NAIT may unilaterally sell, mortgage, encumber, or otherwise dispose of such property. This structure allows certain properties placed under NAIT’s control to remain dedicated to extremist causes in perpetuity, preserving radical institutional control and enabling the ideologies advanced by those institutions to endure across generations.
- **(3)** Unlike many religious endowments organized as separate legal entities from houses of worship, which file publicly available IRS Form 990s, NAIT does not make comparable tax filings publicly available, limiting public transparency into its finances, assets, and operations.
- **(4)** NAIT’s founding general manager, Jamal Barzinji, was a founding trustee of the SAAR Foundation and a founder and officer of the International Institute of Islamic Thought (IIIT). Both entities were among the Northern Virginia organizations searched by Federal agents on March 20, 2002, in Operation Green Quest, a Department of the Treasury-led terrorism financing investigation.
- **(5)** Records of a Federal Bureau of Investigation inquiry conducted in 1987 and 1988 explain that a source advised the FBI that “the IIIT, NAIT and all the subsidiary and sponsoring Muslim organizations under the control of the IIIT and the SAAR Foundation are in fact IKHWAN organizations”, the term Ikhwan referring to the Muslim Brotherhood, and that the leadership of NAIT was attempting to conceal its funding or support of political activities in the United States. In a declassified FBI file dated December 15, 1987, a source alleged that NAIT received money from Iran, Libya, Kuwait, and Saudi Arabia to support the Islamic Revolution, and that NAIT recruited and trained Black Muslim separatists and funded their travel to Iran.
- **(6)** The Holy Land Foundation for Relief and Development (HLF) was designated a Specially Designated Global Terrorist by the Department of the Treasury on December 4, 2001. In United States v. Holy Land Foundation for Relief and Development, HLF and 5 of its officers were convicted in November 2008 of providing more than $12,000,000 in material support to Hamas, a designated foreign terrorist organization, in the largest terrorism-financing prosecution in United States history. HLF temporarily operated out of ISNA’s headquarters in Plainfield, Indiana, a property built and owned by NAIT.
- **(7)** Presented as a Government Exhibit in the HLF trial, the May 22, 1991, “Explanatory Memorandum on the General Strategic Goal for the Group in North America” written by Muslim Brotherhood official Mohamed Akram and obtained following a Federal search warrant, describes the Brotherhood’s work in the United States as “a kind of grand Jihad in eliminating and destroying the Western civilization from within”. An attached “list of our organizations and the organizations of our friends” names NAIT and 2 NAIT imprints, American Trust Publications and Islamic Book Service, as Muslim Brotherhood entities, along with at least 3 NAIT officials.
- **(8)** Prosecutors in the HLF trial presented Government Exhibits establishing that HLF raised funds for and supported Hamas through a bank account it maintained with ISNA at NAIT. ISNA checks deposited into the ISNA/NAIT account for HLF were often made payable to “the Palestinian Mujahadeen,” the original name for the Hamas military wing. From the ISNA/NAIT account, HLF subsequently transferred hundreds of thousands of dollars to Mousa Marzouk, then-Chairman of the Hamas Political Bureau, and other individuals and entities associated with Hamas.
- **(9)** Filed on May 29, 2007, Attachment A to the Department of Justice’s trial brief in the HLF case included NAIT and ISNA among a “List of Unindicted Co-conspirators and/or Joint Venturers”.
- **(10)** NAIT was identified as “individuals/entities who are and/or were members of the US Muslim Brotherhood,” alongside ISNA. Attachment A separately names Gaddoor (Gaddor Ibrahim) Saidi, the current Chairman of NAIT’s Board of Trustees, as a United States Muslim Brotherhood member.
- **(11)** In response to a motion filed by NAIT and ISNA that sought to place Attachment A under seal and expunge their names from the list of unindicted co-conspirators, District Judge Jorge Solis ruled in a July 1, 2009, memorandum opinion and order that the “Government has produced ample evidence to establish the associations of” NAIT and ISNA “with Hamas”. The court placed the listing under seal but declined to expunge NAIT’s name from the Government’s filings.
- **(12)** On October 20, 2010, the United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit upheld the lower court’s ruling and denied NAIT’s request that its name be expunged from the Government’s list.
- **(13)** On January 13, 2026, the United States Department of the Treasury, in coordination with the United States Department of State, listed the Egyptian, Jordanian, and Lebanese branches of Muslim Brotherhood as Specially Designated Global Terrorists for providing material support to Hamas. Lebanon’s Muslim Brotherhood branch was also designated as a Foreign Terrorist Organization for planning terrorist attacks and launching rockets into Northern Israel. The Sudanese Muslim Brotherhood was subsequently designated as a terrorist organization on March 9, 2026, for receiving training and support from Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps and for conducting mass executions of civilians.
- **(14)** NAIT serves as a financial custodian and real estate trustee to some of the most radical Islamist institutions in North America, including multiple organizations involved in providing material support to terrorists. A representative sample includes the following:
  - **(A)** The Mosque Foundation, a Bridgeview, Illinois, Islamic center with close links to Hamas and the Muslim Brotherhood. A 2004 Chicago Tribune investigation uncovered NAIT’s role in appropriating control of the mosque from its moderate founders and installing religious hardliners who enforced extreme ideological practices. Mosque leaders raised money for the families of Palestinian suicide bombers and donated to 4 charities that were shut down for financing terrorism. After the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks, FBI agents placed the Mosque Foundation under continuous surveillance for several years, and its bank account was closed in 2005 in response to terror financing concerns.
  - **(B)** Brighter Horizons Academy, a K–12 school in Garland, Texas, operated by the Islamic Services Foundation (ISF) on a campus to which NAIT has held title since 1998. ISF’s 2001 tax return listed as its vice president Mufid Abdulqader, who was convicted in 2008 of conspiring to provide material support to Hamas through the Holy Land Foundation for Relief and Development (HLF) and sentenced to 20 years in prison, and as a board member Rasmi Almallah, whom Federal prosecutors identified in the HLF case as an HLF director or officer, a member of the Muslim Brotherhood’s Palestine Committee, and an unindicted co-conspirator. ISF’s curriculum director from 2006 to 2010 was Nabil Sadoun, whom the FBI concluded “was affiliated with HAMAS” and “knowingly provided material support to the HLF”, and who was ordered removed from the United States in 2010 for concealing those ties.
  - **(C)** The Dar al-Hijrah Islamic Center in Falls Church, Virginia, which was purchased on June 19, 1983, with the participation of NAIT. According to the Final Report of the National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States, September 11 hijackers Nawaf al-Hazmi and Hani Hanjour attended that mosque in April 2001, when Anwar al-Awlaqi, later designated a Specially Designated Global Terrorist, served as one of its imams. Nidal Malik Hasan, the former Army Major responsible for the November 5, 2009, mass shooting at the Soldier Readiness Center in Fort Hood, Texas, that killed 13 people and wounded 32 others, worshiped at Dar Al-Hijrah.
  - **(D)** The Islamic Society of Greater Chattanooga, whose congregation grew out of a small mosque purchased by NAIT in 1997, and where NAIT agent Arif Shafi reportedly served as ISGC’s registered agent and an incorporator at its 2007 chartering. Mohammad Youssef Abdulazeez regularly attended the Tennessee mosque before he killed 4 Marines and a Navy sailor in a July 16, 2015, shooting attack at a Chattanooga recruiting center.
  - **(E)** The Islamic Center of Tucson, whose property NAIT has held title to since 1991. Plaintiffs in the multi-district litigation, In re Terrorist Attacks on September 11, 2001, alleged that from 1985 to 1993, “the Islamic Center of Tucson served as the de-facto headquarters of Al-Qaeda in the United States”. Wael Julaidan, the Islamic center’s president in the mid-1980s, left Tucson to fight alongside Osama bin Laden in Afghanistan, later headed organizations that provided financial and logistical support to al-Qaeda, and was designated a Specially Designated Global Terrorist in 2002. Wadih el-Hage, bin Laden’s personal secretary, attended the Tucson mosque and was sentenced to life in prison in 2001 for his role in the 1998 bombings of the U.S. embassies in Kenya and Tanzania that killed 224 people.
  - **(F)** The Islamic Community Center of Phoenix (ICCP), whose current website confirms “the land is held in Islamic trust” by NAIT. Elton “Ebrahim” Simpson and Nadir Soofi worshipped at ICCP before they obtained semiautomatic rifles, drove to Garland, Texas, and opened fire on security personnel outside the Curtis Culwell Center during a “Muhammad Art Exhibit and Contest”, wounding a security officer before being killed by law enforcement. Prior to the attack, Simpson used social media to pledge allegiance to ISIS. In 2009, U.S. Navy veteran Hassan Abu-Jihaad, another congregant, was sentenced to 10 years for communicating classified national defense information to unauthorized persons by providing Azzam Publications, an Al Qaeda-linked website, with the anticipated movement and vulnerabilities of a Navy Battle Group transiting the Strait of Hormuz. Abu-Jihaad’s former roommate, Derrick Shareef, also attended the Phoenix mosque and was sentenced to 35 years for plotting to detonate grenades at a Rockford, Illinois, shopping mall.
  - **(G)** The Islamic Community of Tampa, Inc. (ICT), or Al-Qassam Mosque, was formally incorporated in 1992 by Sami al-Arian, who pleaded guilty in 2006 to conspiring to make or receive contributions of funds, goods, or services for the benefit of the Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ) and was sentenced to 57 months in prison and later deported. According to the St. Petersburg Times, individuals related to and associated with al-Arian reportedly stormed the mosque in May 1987 to drive out moderate worshippers. During the altercation, a pregnant woman was assaulted and later suffered a miscarriage, according to a police report. Al-Arian’s faction took control of the mosque and handed the title over to NAIT. Hatem Fariz, the current executive director and registered agent of ICT, was sentenced to at least 37 months in 2006 for providing material support to PIJ.
  - **(H)** The Islamic Academy of Florida (IAF), a Tampa school founded in 1992 by Sami Al-Arian, who served as its founder and board chairman. The February 2003 Federal indictment against Al-Arian alleged the school was used as a base of support for the PIJ “in order to assist its engagement in, and promotion of, violent attacks designed to thwart the Middle East Peace Process”. After Florida revoked IAF’s voucher eligibility in 2003, the school dissolved and was reconstituted as the American Youth Academy (AYA), retaining the same building, staff, and programming. NAIT held a mortgage with AYA until 2011, and granted the school a perpetual easement to an adjoining property in 2021.
- **(15)** On November 18, 2025, the Governor of Texas designated the Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR) and the Muslim Brotherhood as foreign terrorist and transnational criminal organizations under State law. CAIR has been identified by Federal prosecutors as a Muslim Brotherhood front organization and appeared with NAIT on the Holy Land Foundation trial’s list of unindicted co-conspirators.
- **(16)** On November 26, 2025, 8 days after Texas designated CAIR as a terrorist organization under State law, CAIR’s national headquarters at 453 New Jersey Avenue SE, Washington, DC, was conveyed to a newly formed entity, Sage Foundation, for a recited $10. On February 13, 2026, approximately 1 month after the Federal Government’s designation of Muslim Brotherhood branches described in this Act, Sage Foundation conveyed an undivided 45 percent interest in the property to NAIT for a stated consideration of $4,000,000 and gave NAIT the right to prevent the sale, transfer, financing, or encumbrance of its remaining interest.
- **(17)** The transactions described in paragraph (16) resulted in NAIT acquiring a direct interest in CAIR’s national headquarters and control over the disposition of the remaining interest. NAIT’s stated purpose includes the objective to, “Shield Islamic Centers’ properties from legal liabilities,” and this structure could place CAIR’s property beyond the practical reach of a Federal asset freeze imposed on CAIR.
- **(18)** Designating entities as Specially Designated Global Terrorists under Executive Order 13224 (September 23, 2001) allows the United States to block assets and prohibit transactions to disrupt terrorist financing and activities.
- **(19)** Such a designation is necessary to protect the national security interests of the United States by preventing the perpetual religious endowment system from being used to shelter, transfer, or perpetuate the assets of persons who support terrorism, while preserving the ability of local congregations to worship on property held in trust for their benefit.

## § 3. Designation as a specially designated global terrorist

- **(a)** *Designation* Not later than 30 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Secretary of State and the Attorney General, shall designate the North American Islamic Trust, Inc., an Illinois nonprofit corporation (in this Act referred to as “NAIT”), including all its subsidiaries, affiliates, and successors, as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist pursuant to section 1 of Executive Order 13224 (50 U.S.C. 1701 note; relating to blocking property and prohibiting transactions with persons who commit, threaten to commit, or support terrorism), as amended.
- **(b)** *Covered entities* For purposes of a designation under subsection (a), the subsidiaries and affiliates of NAIT include—
  - **(1)** Allied Asset Advisors, Inc., and any investment company or fund it advises or sponsors, including the Iman Fund;
  - **(2)** American Trust Publications and Islamic Book Service;
  - **(3)** the Islamic Centers Cooperative Fund and any other pooled fund, loan program, or waqf program administered by NAIT; and
  - **(4)** any entity that is owned or controlled by, or that acts for or on behalf of, NAIT, except that a property, trust, waqf, or similar arrangement with NAIT shall not, by itself, establish that an entity is a subsidiary or affiliate of NAIT.
- **(c)** *Limitation on removal* The Secretary of the Treasury may not revoke the designation required by subsection (a), or remove NAIT or any entity described in subsection (b) from the Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List maintained by the Office of Foreign Assets Control of the Department of the Treasury subsequent to such designation, unless, not later than 30 days before the revocation or removal takes effect, the Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Secretary of State and the Attorney General, submits to the appropriate committees of Congress—
  - **(1)** a certification that the entity with respect to which such revocation or removal, as applicable, will take effect no longer meets the criteria for designation set forth in Executive Order 13224 or under subsection (b), respectively; and
  - **(2)** a detailed justification as to which such criteria are no longer met.

## § 4. Protection of houses of worship and other beneficiary institutions

- **(a)** *General license for beneficiary institutions* Not later than 90 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury shall issue a general license authorizing a beneficiary institution to continue to possess, occupy, use, maintain, insure, and conduct religious, educational, and charitable activities on any covered property, and to pay taxes, utilities, and other ordinary operating expenses relating to that property, notwithstanding the blocking of NAIT’s interest in that property.
- **(b)** *Transfer of NAIT’s interest*
  - **(1)** *In general* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury, in consultation with the Attorney General, shall establish a process under which NAIT’s interest in a covered property may be conveyed, by license, from NAIT to—
    - **(A)** a beneficiary institution with respect to the covered property; or
    - **(B)** a successor trustee that is not owned or controlled by, and does not act for or on behalf of, NAIT or any other designated person, upon certification by the transferee, under penalty of perjury, that it is not owned or controlled by, and does not act for or on behalf of, any such designated person.
  - **(2)** *Multiple beneficiary institutions* The process established under paragraph (1)(A) shall include procedures under which the beneficiary institutions with respect to a covered property that has more than 1 beneficiary institution determine the transferee of NAIT’s interest in the covered property, including procedures for resolving any disagreement among those beneficiary institutions.
- **(c)** *Deadline for covered properties* Not later than 180 days after the date on which the Secretary of the Treasury publishes the process established under subsection (b), each beneficiary institution with respect to a covered property shall—
  - **(1)** obtain a conveyance of NAIT’s interest in the covered property under such process; or
  - **(2)** otherwise terminate every declaration of trust, trust agreement, or similar instrument with NAIT relating to the covered property.
- **(d)** *Expiration and extension of general license*
  - **(1)** *Expiration* The general license issued under subsection (a) shall terminate with respect to a covered property on the day after the deadline described in subsection (c).
  - **(2)** *Extension* The Secretary of the Treasury may extend the deadline under subsection (c) with respect to a covered property for 1 or more periods of not more than 180 days each upon a showing by an applicable beneficiary institution of good cause, such as the refusal of NAIT to execute a conveyance, a dispute among the beneficiary institutions with respect to the covered property, pending litigation concerning title, or a lien or encumbrance that prevents conveyance.
- **(e)** *Orderly redemption of investment interests* The Secretary of the Treasury shall issue a license authorizing the orderly redemption or transfer of shares held by United States persons who are not designated persons in any investment company advised or sponsored by a subsidiary of NAIT, on such terms as the Secretary determines appropriate to prevent the transfer of value to NAIT.
- **(f)** *Rule of construction*
  - **(1)** *In general* Nothing in this Act shall be construed—
    - **(A)** to prohibit religious worship on a covered property by individuals who are not designated persons; or
    - **(B)** to authorize the closure of a house of worship operated on a covered property by a beneficiary institution.
  - **(2)** *Limitation* This subsection shall not be construed to limit subsection (c), (d), or (e).
- **(g)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Beneficiary institution* The term beneficiary institution means a mosque, Islamic center, school, cemetery, or other religious, educational, or charitable organization that possesses, occupies, or uses a covered property under a declaration of trust, trust agreement, or similar instrument with NAIT, and that is not itself a designated person.
  - **(2)** *Covered property* The term covered property means real property in the United States—
    - **(A)** to which NAIT holds legal title for the benefit of a beneficiary institution; or
    - **(B)** in which NAIT holds any interest (as trustee, waqf holder, or otherwise) for the benefit of a beneficiary institution.
  - **(3)** *Designated person* The term designated person means—
    - **(A)** NAIT;
    - **(B)** each entity described in section 3(b);
    - **(C)** a person designated under Executive Order 13224 or section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1189); and
    - **(D)** a person whose property and interests in property are otherwise blocked under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.).

## § 5. Report to Congress

- **(a)** *Report required* Not later than 30 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury and the Secretary of State, in consultation with the Attorney General, shall submit to the appropriate committees of Congress—
  - **(1)** a detailed report on NAIT, including a description of the conduct of NAIT relevant to the criteria for designation as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist set forth in Executive Order 13224;
  - **(2)** actions taken to implement the requirements of section 3 as a consequence of the designation required by section 3(a);
  - **(3)** an inventory, by Treasury, of each parcel of real property in the United States to which NAIT holds legal title or in which it holds any interest, the beneficiary institution of each such parcel, and the assessed value of each such parcel;
  - **(4)** an accounting of all funds received by NAIT or its subsidiaries from foreign governments, foreign government-controlled institutions, or foreign persons since January 1, 1973, to the extent ascertainable; and
  - **(5)** a description of each transaction since November 18, 2025, in which NAIT acquired an interest in real property from, or a right of consent over real property held by, any person that is the subject of a pending or enacted designation under Federal or State law.
- **(b)** *Form* The report required by subsection (a) shall—
  - **(1)** be submitted in unclassified form, but may include a classified annex; and
  - **(2)** be made available only in electronic form, except if a printed copy is requested by any member of the appropriate committees of Congress.
- **(c)** *Publication* The unclassified portion of the report required by subsection (a) shall be published to a publicly available website of the Department of the Treasury not later than 7 days after the date on which the report is submitted pursuant to such subsection.

## § 6. Appropriate committees of Congress defined

In this Act, the term appropriate committees of Congress means—

- **(1)** the Committee on Armed Services, the Committee on Financial Services, the Committee on Foreign Affairs, the Committee on the Judiciary, the Committee on Homeland Security, the Committee on Ways and Means, and the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence of the House of Representatives; and
- **(2)** the Committee on Armed Services, the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs, the Committee on Foreign Relations, the Committee on the Judiciary, the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, the Committee on Finance, and the Select Committee on Intelligence of the Senate.
