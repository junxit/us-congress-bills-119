# H. CON. RES. 119

> Celebrating the 65th anniversary of the establishment of the Peace Corps, and commending its volunteers and contributions to global peace, friendship, and development.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on March 1, 1961, President John F. Kennedy issued his historic call to service and established the Peace Corps, launching a new era of American volunteerism abroad;
- Whereas, over the past 65 years, nearly 250,000 Americans have served as Peace Corps volunteers in 144 countries, fostering people-to-people diplomacy in areas such as education, health, agriculture, environment, youth in development, and community economic development;
- Whereas the Peace Corps continues to exemplify “the toughest job you’ll ever love,” building sustainable partnerships and strengthening communities around the world;
- Whereas the Peace Corps has enhanced global perceptions of the United States by demonstrating American values through service, humility, cultural exchange, and partnership rather than through political or military means;
- Whereas returned Peace Corps volunteers bring home invaluable linguistic, cultural, and technical skills that strengthen the U.S. workforce, expand America’s global competency, and enhance the nation’s diplomatic and economic competitiveness;
- Whereas the Peace Corps has contributed to U.S. national security by strengthening international stability, promoting economic growth, and addressing root causes of conflict through education, empowerment, and community development;
- Whereas Peace Corps volunteers have exemplified American leadership through volunteerism, generosity and service to others; and
- Whereas Peace Corps service has inspired generations of Americans to pursue careers in public service, education, economic development, diplomacy, and health, creating a lasting pipeline of globally minded civic leaders; Now, therefore, be it

That Congress—

- **(1)** Celebrates the 65th anniversary of the founding of the Peace Corps;
- **(2)** Honors the nearly 250,000 individuals who have served as Peace Corps volunteers for their dedication to peace, friendship, and community-driven development; and
- **(3)** Commends the Peace Corps’ enduring mission to put purpose, passion, and skills to work for America and the world.
