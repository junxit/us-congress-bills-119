# H.R. 10755

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 475 East FM 1382 in Cedar Hill, Texas, as the “Eugene Gates Jr. Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Eugene Gates Jr. Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 475 East FM 1382 in Cedar Hill, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Eugene Gates Jr. Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Eugene Gates Jr. Post Office Building”.
