# H.R. 10086

> To reauthorize the National Diabetes Prevention Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Diabetes Prevention Program Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of National Diabetes Prevention Program

Section 399V–3(d) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 280g–14(d)) is amended by striking “such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2010 through 2014” and inserting “$39,300,000 for fiscal year 2027, $44,300,000 for fiscal year 2028, $49,300,000 for fiscal year 2029, $54,300,000 for fiscal year 2030, and $59,300,000 for fiscal year 2031”.
