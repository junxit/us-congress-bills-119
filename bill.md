# S. 5237

> To require the Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases to award a competitive grant for the Antibiotic Resistance Leadership Group for the purpose of facilitating a clinical research network on antibiotic resistance, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Superbugs Act”.

## § 2. Funding for antibiotic resistance clinical research

- **(a)** *In general* The Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases shall award a competitive grant for the Antibiotic Resistance Leadership Group for the purpose of facilitating a clinical research network on antibiotic resistance, which shall include focuses on—
  - **(1)** supporting rapid recruitment of clinical trial participants;
  - **(2)** conducting clinical trial recruitment outreach in community settings;
  - **(3)** supporting the pairing of diagnostic and pharmaceutical innovations during clinical trials; and
  - **(4)** developing patient-reported outcomes in clinical studies.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to the Director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases such sums as may be necessary to carry out subsection (a).
