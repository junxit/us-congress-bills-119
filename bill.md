# H.R. 10974

> To rescind appropriations provided to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement and U.S. Customs and Border Protection by the One, Big, Beautiful Bill Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “DHS Funding Accountability Act”.

## § 2. Rescission of ICE and CBP funding

The unobligated balance of funds appropriated or otherwise made available under sections 90001, 90002, 90003, 90004, 90005(b), 90007, 100051, and 100052 of Public Law 119–21 (commonly referred to as the “One, Big, Beautiful Bill Act”) are rescinded and such sections are repealed.
