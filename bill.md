# H.R. 10423

> To provide for a temporary prohibition on the export of diesel fuel.

## § 1. Temporary prohibition on export of diesel fuel

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the export of diesel fuel shall be prohibited during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this subsection and ending on the date December 31, 2026.
