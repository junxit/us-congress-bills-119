# S. 2245

> To amend the Digital Coast Act to improve the acquisition, integration, and accessibility of data of the Digital Coast program and to extend the program.

## § 1. Acquisition, integration, and accessibility of data of Digital Coast program

Section 4 of the Digital Coast Act (16 U.S.C. 1467) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)(1), by inserting “and fully and freely available” after “readily accessible”;
- **(2)** in subsection (d)—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (3)—
    - **(i)** in subparagraph (D), by inserting “, including data related to underground infrastructure and subsurface utilities” after “data”; and
    - **(ii)** in subparagraph (H), by striking “and”;
  - **(B)** in paragraph (4), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(5)** limit trainings to technical instruction in the use of data and tools provided under this section.

- **(3)** in subsection (g), by striking “2025” and inserting “2030”.
