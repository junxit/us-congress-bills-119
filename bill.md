# H.R. 6761

> To amend title 54, United States Code, apply certain National Historic Preservation Act requirements to the White House, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “People’s White House Historic Preservation Act”.

## § 2. Inapplicability of division

Section 307104 of title 54, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “the White House or its grounds”; and
- **(2)** by striking “, or” and inserting “or”.
