# S. 5406

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the limitation on waiver for pay of critical health care personnel of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “VA Salary Cap Waiver Adjustment Act”.

## § 2. Modification of limitation on waiver for pay of critical health care personnel

Section 7431(e)(6) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subparagraph (D);
- **(2)** by redesignating subparagraphs (E), (F), and (G) as subparagraphs (D), (E), and (F), respectively; and
- **(3)** by amending subparagraph (F), as redesignated by paragraph (2), to read as follows:
  > - **(F)** The authority of the Secretary under subparagraph (A) shall terminate on the date that is five years after the date of the enactment of the VA Salary Cap Waiver Adjustment Act.
