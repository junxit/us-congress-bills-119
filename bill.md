# H.R. 10788

> To amend title XXVII of the Public Health Service Act, the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, and the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to make certain adjustments to the surprise medical billing requirements for private health insurance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lower Premiums, Faster Payments Act”.
