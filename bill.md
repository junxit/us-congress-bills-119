# H.R. 10903

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to seek to enter into a lease for the purpose of establishing a Vet Center in Socorro, New Mexico, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Socorro Vet Center Act”.

## § 2. To direct Secretary of Veterans Affairs to seek to enter into lease for purpose of establishing Vet Center in Socorro, New Mexico

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall seek to enter into a lease—

- **(1)** commencing not later than December 31, 2027; and
- **(2)** for the purpose of establishing a Vet Center (as such term is defined in section 1712A(h) of title 38, United States Code) in Socorro, New Mexico.
