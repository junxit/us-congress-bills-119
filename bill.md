# H.R. 11078

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to require the Secretary of Homeland Security to maintain an annual limit on the amount of tuition assistance available for a member of the Coast Guard when the Coast Guard is not operating as a service in the Navy at an amount equal to the annual limit established by the Secretary of Defense with respect to tuition reimbursement for members of the Armed Forces, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Coast Guard Education Parity Act”.

## § 2. Amendment to require parity of annual limit on payment of tuition for off-duty training or education for members of the Coast Guard

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2007(f) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “This section” and inserting “(1) This section”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(2)** The Secretary of Defense or, with respect to the Coast Guard when it is not operating as a service in the Navy, the Secretary of Homeland Security, shall maintain regulations that prescribe an annual limit on the amount of charges allowed to be paid to a member of the armed forces under subsection (a).
    > - **(3)** In carrying out paragraph (2)—
    >   - **(A)** the Secretary of Defense shall ensure that any annual limit established by the Secretary with respect to the armed forces does not differ based on the armed force in which the member serves; and
    >   - **(B)** the Secretary of Homeland Security shall ensure that any annual limit established by the Secretary with respect to members of the Coast Guard when the Coast Guard is not operating as a service in the Navy is equal to the limit established by the Secretary of Defense with respect to the armed forces.

- **(b)** *Initial parity* Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Homeland Security shall, if necessary, prescribe regulations that establish an annual limit on the amount of charges allowed to be paid under section 2007 of title 10, United States Code (as amended by this section), to a member of the Coast Guard when the Coast Guard is not operating as a service in the Navy so that such annual limit is equal to the annual limit established by the Secretary of Defense with respect to the amount of charges allowed to be paid to members of the Armed Forces under such section.
