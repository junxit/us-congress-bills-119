# H.R. 5640

> To amend the definition of State in title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Northern Mariana Islands and American Samoa Criminal Justice Support Act”.

## § 2. Definition of State

Section 901(a)(2) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10251(a)(2)) is amended by striking “Northern Mariana Islands” and all that follows through “Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.” and inserting “Northern Mariana Islands;”.
