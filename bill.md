# H.R. 1098

> To reauthorize the Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994.

## § 1. Reauthorization of Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994

- **(a)** *Definition of State* Section 5 of the Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994 (16 U.S.C. 719b–1) is amended—
  - **(1)** by inserting “and” after “Guam,”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “, and any other territory or possession” after “Virgin Islands”.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* Section 6 of the Junior Duck Stamp Conservation and Design Program Act of 1994 (16 U.S.C. 719c) is amended in subsection (a) by striking “2006 through 2010” and inserting “2025 through 2031”.
