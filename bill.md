# H.R. 6308

> To direct the Librarian of Congress to obtain a stained glass panel depicting the seal of the District of Columbia and install the panel among the stained glass panels depicting the seals of States, which overlook the Main Reading Room of the Library of Congress Thomas Jefferson Building.

## § 1. Including stained glass panel depicting seal of District of Columbia in Library of Congress Thomas Jefferson Building

The Librarian of Congress shall obtain a stained glass panel depicting the seal of the District of Columbia and install the panel among the panels depicting the seals of States, which overlook the Main Reading Room of the Library of Congress Thomas Jefferson Building.
