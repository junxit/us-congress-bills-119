# H.R. 10848

> To require the Comptroller General of the United States to review the Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Shipyard Modernization Sustainment Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Comptroller General review of Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program

- **(a)** *Review* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act, and annually until January 1, 2031, the Comptroller General of the United States shall review the following:
  - **(1)** The costs, timeframes, risks, and progress of the Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program of the Department of the Navy.
  - **(2)** The status of each project conducted under the Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program.
- **(b)** *Briefing* Not later than March 1, 2027, and annually thereafter until March 1, 2031, the Comptroller General shall provide to the congressional defense committees (as defined in section 101 of title 10, United States Code) a briefing on findings from the review required under subsection (a).
- **(c)** *Report* Not later than June 1, 2031, the Comptroller General shall provide to the congressional defense committees a report on the findings from the review required under subsection (a).
