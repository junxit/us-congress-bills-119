# H.R. 9044

> To amend title V of the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize the Minority Fellowship Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Minority Fellowship Program Reauthorization Act of 2026”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Minority Fellowship Program

Section 597(c) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 297ll(c)) is amended by inserting “, and $27,000,000 for each fiscal years 2028 through 2032” after “2027”.
