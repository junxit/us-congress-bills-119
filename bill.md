# S. RES. 965

> Congratulating the Milwaukee Brewers on winning the 2026 Major League Baseball World Series.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on October 28, 2026, the Milwaukee Brewers (referred to in this preamble as the “Brewers”) defeated the American League champions, the Tampa Bay Rays, by a score of 4 to 1 to win the 2026 Major League Baseball World Series in 5 games;
- Whereas this victory marks the first World Series championship in Brewers franchise history and the first Major League Baseball championship for the city of Milwaukee since the Milwaukee Braves won the World Series in 1957;
- Whereas the Brewers completed the 2026 regular season with a franchise-record 103 victories, marking the most successful regular season in franchise history;
- Whereas the Brewers captured their fourth consecutive National League Central Division title in 2026, continuing an extraordinary period of sustained success for the franchise;
- Whereas, during the 2026 postseason, the Brewers defeated the San Diego Padres and the Los Angeles Dodgers before defeating the Tampa Bay Rays in the World Series;
- Whereas the Brewers swept the Los Angeles Dodgers in the National League Championship Series to secure their first pennant since 1982 and advance to the World Series for the second time in franchise history;
- Whereas the Brewers achieved an 11–2 record in the postseason;
- Whereas every member of the 2026 Brewers roster played an important role in the team's historic season, demonstrating exceptional teamwork, resilience, and determination;
- Whereas Brewers pitcher Jacob Misiorowski delivered a historic season, showcasing extraordinary pitching velocity and recording more than 1,000 pitches reaching or exceeding 100 miles per hour during the regular season;
- Whereas Brewers designated hitter, former National League Most Valuable Player, and first captain in franchise history Christian Yelich demonstrated exceptional leadership on and off the field, guiding the Brewers to their first World Series championship;
- Whereas Brewers catcher William Contreras was named Most Valuable Player of the 2026 World Series after batting .381 with 4 runs batted in and provided remarkable leadership behind home plate throughout the regular season and postseason;
- Whereas Brewers first baseman Jake Bauers enjoyed an outstanding season, contributing critical offensive performances and earning Most Valuable Player honors in the National League Championship Series;
- Whereas the Brewers’ bullpen demonstrated extraordinary depth and dominance throughout the postseason, pitching 65 innings with a 1.38 earned run average and holding opposing batters to a .173 batting average;
- Whereas Brewers manager Pat Murphy provided exceptional leadership throughout the 2026 season, guiding the team to a franchise-record regular season and its first World Series championship;
- Whereas the entire Milwaukee Brewers organization, including its players, coaches, front office, and support staff, contributed to the team's historic achievement;
- Whereas the Brewers’ dedicated stadium and support staff, including ushers, concession workers, groundskeepers, maintenance crews, security personnel, guest services representatives, and countless others, worked tirelessly to provide a safe and memorable experience for fans throughout the 2026 season;
- Whereas the Brewers have remained a source of tremendous pride for the city of Milwaukee, the State of Wisconsin, and baseball fans throughout the country; and
- Whereas Brewers fans demonstrated unwavering loyalty and enthusiasm throughout the 2026 season and postseason, filling American Family Field and Tropicana Field, supporting the team on its historic championship run, and celebrating the team’s victory with an estimated 750,000 fans attending the Milwaukee Brewers World Series parade and rally: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate—

- **(1)** congratulates the Milwaukee Brewers on winning the 2026 Major League Baseball World Series;
- **(2)** recognizes the historic achievements of the Milwaukee Brewers during the 2026 Major League Baseball season, including winning a franchise-record 103 regular-season games and capturing the first World Series championship in franchise history;
- **(3)** commends the players, coaches, management, and support staff of the Milwaukee Brewers organization for their dedication, perseverance, and contributions to bringing the Commissioner's Trophy to Milwaukee;
- **(4)** recognizes the loyal fans of the Milwaukee Brewers and the people of Wisconsin for their unwavering support of the team; and
- **(5)** respectfully directs the Secretary of the Senate to transmit an enrolled copy of this resolution to—
  - **(A)** the chairman and principal owner of the Milwaukee Brewers, Mark Attanasio;
  - **(B)** the president of baseball operations and general manager of the Milwaukee Brewers, Matt Arnold; and
  - **(C)** the manager of the Milwaukee Brewers, Pat Murphy.
