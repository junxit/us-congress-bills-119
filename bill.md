# S. 1858

> To require that national cemeteries be open to visitors on legal public holidays.

## § 1. National cemeteries open on legal public holidays

Each national cemetery administered by the Department of Defense, the Department of Veterans Affairs, or the National Park Service shall be open to visitors on the legal public holidays described in section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code.
