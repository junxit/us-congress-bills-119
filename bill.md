# H.R. 4590

> To codify Executive Order 14305 (relating to restoring American airspace sovereignty).

## § 1. Codification of executive order

Executive Order 14305 (90 Fed. Reg. 24719; relating to restoring American airspace sovereignty) shall have the force and effect of law.
