# H.R. 7141

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the maximum guaranty amount of certain home loans guaranteed by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Affordable Housing Guarantee Act”.

## § 2. Maximum guaranty amount of certain home loans guaranteed by the Department of Veterans Affairs

Section 3703(a)(1)(A)(i)(IV) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “title, 25 percent of the loan; or” and inserting “title—”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following new items:
  > - **(aa)** in the case of a veteran with a service-connected disability whose entitlement under this chapter is unused or has been restored in full, 50 percent of the loan; and
  > - **(bb)** in the case of a veteran other than a veteran described in item (aa), 25 percent of the loan; or
