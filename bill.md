# S. 5464

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the report on the health of women veterans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Veterans’ Outcomes, Inclusion, Care, and Experience for Women Veterans Act” or the “VOICE for Women Veterans Act”.

## § 2. Modification of report on health of women veterans

Section 7310A of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “Chief Officer of Women's Health” and inserting “Secretary”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “through (g)” and inserting “through (l)”;
- **(2)** by striking subsection (c);
- **(3)** by redesignating subsections (d) through (g) as subsections (e), (g), (h), and (m), respectively;
- **(4)** by inserting after subsection (b) the following:
  > - **(c)** *Utilization of services* Each report under subsection (a) shall include the following:
  >   - **(1)** The number of women veterans who reside in each State, territory, or possession of the United States.
  >   - **(2)** The number of women veterans enrolled in the system of annual patient enrollment of the Department established and operated under section 1705(a) of this title, disaggregated by—
  >     - **(A)** State, territory, or possession of the United States; and
  >     - **(B)** medical facility of the Department at which the veteran is registered.
  >   - **(3)** The number of women veterans newly enrolled in such system since the submittal of the previous report under subsection (a), disaggregated by State, territory, or possession of the United States.
  >   - **(4)** Of the women veterans enrolled in such system, the number who received health care under the laws administered by the Secretary at least once during the one-year period preceding the submittal of the report.
  >   - **(5)** The number of unique women veterans who received health care at each medical facility of the Department during such one-year period, disaggregated by facility.
  >   - **(6)** The number of appointments completed by women veterans at medical facilities of the Department during such one-year period, disaggregated by—
  >     - **(A)** medical facility;
  >     - **(B)** type of care, including—
  >       - **(i)** primary care;
  >       - **(ii)** specialty care; and
  >       - **(iii)** mental health care; and
  >     - **(C)** modality of care, including—
  >       - **(i)** in-person care; and
  >       - **(ii)** care furnished through the use of telehealth.
  >   - **(7)** A summary of the geographic distribution of health care furnished by the Department to women veterans, including any significant geographic trends in enrollment or utilization.
  >   - **(8)** An assessment of utilization of health care furnished by the Department to women veterans, disaggregated, to the extent practicable, by—
  >     - **(A)** age;
  >     - **(B)** era of service; and
  >     - **(C)** military occupational specialty or other comparable career specialty in the Armed Forces.
  >   - **(9)** A summary of—
  >     - **(A)** enrollment of women veterans in, and utilization by women veterans of, Centers of Excellence of the Department; and
  >     - **(B)** participation by women veterans in clinical, disease, and exposure-based condition registries of the Department.
  >   - **(10)** An identification of—
  >     - **(A)** the medical facility of the Department in each Veterans Integrated Service Network with the greatest rate of increase in the population of women veteran patients, as measured by the change in unique women veteran patient utilization since the previous reporting period; and
  >     - **(B)** the medical facility of the Department in each Veterans Integrated Service Network with the greatest rate of decrease in the population of women veteran patients, as measured by the change in unique women veteran patient utilization since the previous reporting period.
  >   - **(11)** A comparison of the information required under paragraphs (1) through (10) with the corresponding information from previous reporting periods, to the extent such information is available.
  > - **(d)** *Access to gender-Specific services*
  >   - **(1)** Each report under subsection (a) shall include an analysis of the access of women veterans to and the use by women veterans of gender-specific services—
  >     - **(A)** furnished by the Department at Department facilities; and
  >     - **(B)** furnished by the Department under contracts, agreements, or other arrangements with non-Department medical providers entered into by the Secretary for the provision of hospital care or medical services to veterans.
  >   - **(2)** The analysis under paragraph (1) shall include data and performance measures for the availability of gender-specific services, disaggregated by services described in subparagraphs (A) and (B), including—
  >     - **(A)** the average wait time between the preferred appointment date of the veteran and the date on which the appointment is completed;
  >     - **(B)** the average driving time required for veterans to attend appointments;
  >     - **(C)** reasons why appointments could not be scheduled with non-Department medical providers; and
  >     - **(D)** the number of women veterans utilizing non-Department care.

- **(5)** by inserting after subsection (e), as redesignated by paragraph (3), the following:
  > - **(f)** *Other care items* Each report under subsection (a) shall include a description of the following:
  >   - **(1)** The referral process, timeliness, and effectiveness of referrals to primary or specialty care for the provision of gender-specific services by providers of the Department.
  >   - **(2)** The referral process, timeliness, and effectiveness of referrals to primary or specialty care for the provision of gender-specific services by non-Department providers.
  >   - **(3)** The utilization and effectiveness of the self-referral gynecology appointment process of the Department, or any successor similar process.
  >   - **(4)** Mechanisms of the Department to provide oversight of care quality and timeliness regarding gender-specific services.
  >   - **(5)** Utilization by women veterans of Centers of Excellence of the Department, including registration and actual appointments served.
  >   - **(6)** Health care sought by women veterans relating to exposure to toxic substances, including an identification of the presumptive location, type of exposure, and type of disease treated.

- **(6)** in subsection (g), as so redesignated—
  - **(A)** in the matter preceding paragraph (1), by striking “paragraph (4)” and inserting “paragraph (5)”;
  - **(B)** by redesignating paragraphs (4) through (6) as paragraphs (5) through (7);
  - **(C)** by inserting after paragraph (3) the following:
    > - **(4)** The number of full- and part-time specialized providers of the Department who provide gender-specific services, disaggregated by gender-specific service.

  - **(D)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(8)** Vacant positions for designated women’s health care providers, disaggregated by medical center of the Department, including an assessment of barriers experienced by the Department when hiring such providers and an assessment of active efforts by the Department to address such vacancies.
    > - **(9)** Any positions relating to women's health that have been realigned, removed, or are otherwise no longer dedicated to providing gender-specific services since the submittal of the previous report, including an identification of the facilities at which those positions were located.
    > - **(10)** Provider training standards for the provision of gender-specific services.
    > - **(11)** Training offered by the Department for community care providers, including the number of community care providers who provide gender-specific services who have received certified training for cultural competency, suicide prevention, lethal means safety training, military sexual trauma training, and any other training the Department offers or requires of providers of gender-specific services.

- **(7)** by inserting after subsection (h), as so redesignated, the following:
  > - **(i)** *Oversight of the Deborah Sampson Act* Each report under subsection (a) shall include a description of the efforts of the Department in implementing sections 5104, 5107, 5108, 5302, and 5305 of the Deborah Sampson Act of 2020 (title V of Public Law 116–315; 134 Stat. 5021), including the amendments made by those sections.
  > - **(j)** *Unique diseases* Each report under subsection (a) shall include a description of—
  >   - **(1)** emerging diseases and other medical conditions affecting women veterans and the ability of the Department to provide treatment for such diseases and other medical conditions; and
  >   - **(2)** existing diseases and other medical conditions unique to women veterans, the rate at which the Department is diagnosing and treating such diseases and other medical conditions, and the number of claims for benefits relating to such diseases and other medical conditions, including the number of such claims subject to appeal and the outcomes of such appeals.
  > - **(k)** *Veterans Benefits Administration data*
  >   - **(1)** Each report under subsection (a) shall include, to the extent practicable—
  >     - **(A)** data collected in connection with reports on claims for benefits under the laws administered by the Secretary submitted by women veterans for adjudication relating to emerging diseases or other medical conditions, including—
  >       - **(i)** the number of such claims;
  >       - **(ii)** the ratio of favorable and unfavorable adjudications;
  >       - **(iii)** the number of such claims for which a higher-level review is requested, a supplemental claim is filed, or an appeal to the Board of Veterans’ Appeals is initiated; and
  >       - **(iv)** the outcomes of those reviews and appeals; and
  >     - **(B)** information regarding the utilization by women veterans of benefits administered by the Veterans Benefits Administration, including—
  >       - **(i)** the number of women veterans who are using or have used educational assistance under the laws administered by the Secretary;
  >       - **(ii)** the number of women veterans who are receiving or have received services or assistance under chapter 31 of this title;
  >       - **(iii)** with respect to compensation under chapter 11 of this title—
  >         - **(I)** the number of women veterans receiving disability compensation;
  >         - **(II)** the number of women veterans with pending claims for disability compensation;
  >         - **(III)** the number of women veterans whose claims for disability compensation were denied during the reporting period;
  >         - **(IV)** the average disability rating for women veterans receiving compensation; and
  >         - **(V)** such other information as the Secretary determines appropriate regarding the use by women veterans of benefits administered by the Veterans Benefits Administration.
  >   - **(2)** To the extent practicable, the information required under this subsection shall be disaggregated by age, era of service, and other demographic characteristics as the Secretary determines appropriate.
  > - **(l)** *Consolidation of reports*
  >   - **(1)** Subject to paragraph (2), the Secretary may consolidate into the report under subsection (a) other reports required by law if—
  >     - **(A)** such consolidation will not delay the delivery by the Department of the report under subsection (a) or any other report by more than 30 days; and
  >     - **(B)** all information required for the other report is addressed in the report under subsection (a).
  >   - **(2)** The Secretary may not take any action under paragraph (1) to consolidate reports until the date that is 45 days after the Secretary submits to the appropriate congressional committees a report that describes the proposed consolidation and the reasons for such proposed consolidation.

- **(8)** in subsection (m), as so redesignated, by amending paragraph (2) to read as follows:
  > - **(2)** The term gender-specific services means reproductive health services, specialized mental health services, counseling and treatment for military sexual trauma, gynecological care, maternity care, ovarian cancer care, breast cancer care, cervical cancer care, endometrial cancer care, thyroid cancer care, mammography, obstetric care, uterine cancer care, fallopian tube cancer care, primary peritoneal cancer care, vaginal cancer care, vulvar cancer care, gestational trophoblastic neoplasia care, care relating to other prevalent diseases and conditions affecting women veterans, and such other services as the Secretary determines appropriate.
