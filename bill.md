# S.J. RES. 221

> Providing for congressional disapproval of the agreement for civilian nuclear cooperation between the United States and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia pursuant to section 123 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954.

That, the Congress does not favor the proposed agreement for cooperation transmitted to the Congress by the President on September 14, 2026.
