# H.R. 11044

> To amend chapter 88 of title 10, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Defense to establish an internship program for military child and youth programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Military Child Care Workforce Development Act”.

## § 2. Establishment of a student internship program for military child and youth programs

Subchapter II of chapter 88 of title 10, United States Code, is amended by inserting after section 1792 the following new section:

> ## § 1792a. Internship program for military child and youth programs
>
> - **(a)** *Establishment* Not later than one year after the date of the enactment of this section, the Secretary of Defense shall establish and carry out an internship program through which an individual described in subsection (b) may perform duties as an intern in a military child and youth program to prepare the individual for possible future employment in a military child and youth program.
> - **(b)** *Eligible individuals* An individual eligible to be an intern participating in the internship program under this section is an individual who—
>   - **(1)** is a student in good standing at an institution of higher education; and
>   - **(2)** the Secretary determines—
>     - **(A)** to be qualified and talented; and
>     - **(B)** has passed appropriate screening and background checks.
> - **(c)** *Program requirements* Requirements of the internship program shall include the following:
>   - **(1)** An intern may perform duties only in a military child development center under the supervision of a child care employee.
>   - **(2)** Pursuant to section 1588(a)(3)(B) of this title, a student intern shall perform their duties on a voluntary basis.
>   - **(3)** A student intern may not work more than eight hours a day or a total of 40 hours per week.
>   - **(4)** An intern may not be included in any calculation to determine the ratio of children to staff in a military child and youth program.
>   - **(5)** An intern may not be used to fill a vacancy, replace an employee, reduce the hours of an employee, or to avoid an employee incurring overtime.
> - **(d)** *Authorization of certain expenses*
>   - **(1)** The Secretary shall establish procedures to provide for the payment of expenses described in paragraph (2) for participation in the internship program under this section. Such procedures shall be designed to ensure expenses are paid in a manner that minimizes financial barriers to participation in the internship program.
>   - **(2)** The expenses described in this paragraph are the following:
>     - **(A)** Lodging expenses.
>     - **(B)** Subsistence expenses.
>     - **(C)** Incidental expenses.
>     - **(D)** Transportation expenses for transportation between the residence of the intern and the military installation where the intern performs duties under the internship program under this section.
> - **(e)** *Academic credit* The Secretary shall, to the greatest extent practicable, seek to enter into agreements with institutions of higher education to ensure that each individual participating in the internship program earns academic credit towards an undergraduate or graduate degree at the institution of higher education attended by the individual for duties performed by the individual as part of the internship program under this section.
> - **(f)** *Post-Internship employment* The Secretary of Defense shall—
>   - **(1)** establish an expedited hiring process for individuals who complete an internship under this section and are otherwise qualified for permanent full-time or part-time employment in a military child and youth program to be hired into such a program; and
>   - **(2)** ensure individuals performing duties as an intern under this section are made aware of permanent full-time and part-time positions that are open during the participation of such individual in the internship program under this section.
> - **(g)** *Annual report* Not later than one year after the establishment of the internship program required by subsection (a), and annually thereafter, the Secretary of Defense shall submit to the congressional defense committees a report on the activities of the internship program during the preceding year, including—
>   - **(1)** the number of individuals that participated in the internship program as interns;
>   - **(2)** an identification of the locations in which interns participated in the internship program, including the number of interns at each location;
>   - **(3)** the total and average number of hours in which interns participated in the internship program; and
>   - **(4)** an identification of any available positions for interns that were not filled.
> - **(h)** *Definitions* In this section:
>   - **(1)** The term institution of higher education has the meaning given that term in section 102 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1002).
>   - **(2)** The term military child and youth program includes a military child development center and any other youth program of the Department of Defense.
